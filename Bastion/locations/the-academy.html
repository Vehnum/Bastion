The Academy

Where Great Souls are reborn, ranked, and quietly sorted into winners and losers

The Academy is Bastion's present-day institution for reincarnated Great Souls: part reincarnation temple, part military school, part political proving ground. Built after the original campus (see the Old Academy) was abandoned to the encroaching ruins, it sits close to the Graveyard market and is closely tied to, but formally independent of, the Four Houses.

The basilica & the Archspire

At the Academy's heart stands the Aureate Hall and its basilica, a vast domed chamber ringed with jeweled reincarnation biers surrounding the Archspire. Chancellor Pyre Lord Praximar presides over cohort-wide ceremonies here, including the reincarnation rite that opens Book 1 and the speeches that open each round of the class tournament.

Student life

Students live in shared, multi-bedroom suites arranged around communal courtyards such as the Hall of Golden Reflections, where Scorio, Lianshi, Leonis, and (from Book 1, Chapter 43) Naomi share a four-bedroom suite complete with a private heated pool. Mornings begin with a group run around the Amber Circuit, a subterranean oval corridor lit by 176 lanterns per lap (every 44th painted red); the standard run is twenty red lanterns.

Meals are taken in a cavernous mess hall where treasure allocation is directly tied to class ranking — see The Igneous Heart & Mana for the full pill/treasure tier breakdown. The gap between the bottom three hundred students and the top five is stark enough that several characters, including Naomi, call it a deliberately self-fulfilling system.

Curriculum

Each cohort of roughly 500 students is split into groups of fifty, each assigned four instructors across a weekly rotation: History of Bastion and Mana Studies under Instructor Hera, Geography of Hell under Instructor Helminth, Fiends under Instructor Rex, Mana Theoretics, and Great Soul Organizations (a comparatively recent addition, forced onto the curriculum eight years ago after the collapse of the Empyreal Prophecy). Separately, Sparring and Forms is run by Instructor Feng in a sunken combat hall, and team tactics by Instructor Rex in a shape-shifting training cavern governed by his purple "Eye." See Other Characters for details on each instructor.

The library

A sprawling, half-abandoned warren of hexagonal "cells," each linked to six neighboring subjects by named corridors (Parafiendology, Inimical Botany, Banned Histories, and dozens more), overseen by a handful of librarians and populated as much by outside researchers as by students. Many shelves stand conspicuously sparse; Lianshi discovers, via her Emberling senses, that numerous books are hidden behind subtle wards invisible to Cinders and Chars, including at least one, The Provenance of the Winged Plinth, that neither she nor anyone else present can actually open. It's here, in Book 1, Chapter 61, that Scorio — aided by House Chimera recruiter Gelegos, an off-duty regular — digs up the obscure treatise that eventually leads him to Nox and the Delightful Secret Marinating technique.

The tournament

Each cohort runs a double-elimination tournament inside the Gauntlet across the semester, watched by House recruiters looking for sponsorship candidates. By the time Scorio is admitted, the tournament has reached its seventh round, with sixty-four contestants left in each of the winner's and loser's brackets. Fights take place in a colossal, oval Arena of red stone that can host up to thirty-two simultaneous sparring circles at once. Wins draw House attention; a strong showing from the loser's bracket, per Instructor Hera, is one of the only realistic paths to sponsorship for a student without connections.

House sponsorship offices

Each House maintains its own open-plan recruitment space within the Academy. Scorio visits House Chimera's wing in Book 1, Chapter 50 following his tournament win, described as filled with communal reading nooks, treasure altars, and a private interview chamber equipped with an Attenuation Spider crystal used to gauge a candidate's Heart capacity.
