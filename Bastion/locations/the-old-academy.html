The Old Academy

Bastion's original Academy, abandoned centuries ago — and not quite dead

The Old Academy is the original Academy building, standing at the far end of Bastion's ruins near the Dead Portal, where the city's founders first settled. It was abandoned centuries ago when the encroaching ruins consumed the entire original city center, forcing a rebuild (the current Academy, in use today) elsewhere. It is the most dangerous, deepest-Coal-saturated region of the ruins Scorio, Leonis, and Lianshi have explored.

Discovery

Scorio first spots the ruined structure while scavenging in Book 1, Chapter 24, recognizing its resemblance to the current Academy. Believing an untapped deposit of high-grade mana might still exist beneath it, he leads Leonis and Lianshi there in Book 1, Chapter 30 — fighting past three territorial guardian fiends en route: a talking, engorged tongue-toad (later identified as Nox, once he and Scorio are on friendlier terms); a "white worm" with a human male torso for a head; and an unseen shadow-jumping predator.

The interior largely mirrors the current Academy's layout, including a matching Path of Ascension mosaic in its Aureate Hall, but is thoroughly decayed: thin ambient mana, no surviving treasure (long since looted), and an enormous dead spider-fiend, legs alone some six yards long, mummified in a web spanning a quarter of the ruined basilica.

The still-functioning biers

Most of the old basilica's reincarnation biers are destroyed, but a cluster near the base of the broken Archspire remain pristine and mana-active despite the centuries. Scorio discovers these can be manually activated exactly like a normal reincarnation crystal — sending a living person into a still-functioning, completely unmonitored version of the Gauntlet. Because the old Academy is abandoned, no one at the current Academy can see or log these entries, giving Scorio's group effectively unlimited access to death-based training with none of the official two-runs-a-year restriction. This becomes their single most important (and most dangerous) training method through the rest of the book.

The mana mystery

Naomi detects a powerful, unidentifiable current of mana still flowing through the broken Archspire itself — sheathed and inaccessible, "beyond her ability to parse." This turns out to be exactly what draws Imogen, an Imperator, to Bastion in Book 1, Chapter 38: she mistakes the signal for the long-sealed Dead Portal reopening. Her search for its source, and her fury at finding the Portal still closed, triggers the climactic battle in Book 1, Chapters 38–40, fought substantially within and around the old Academy's ruined basilica.
