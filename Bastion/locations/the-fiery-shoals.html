The Fiery Shoals

A crashed island, still bleeding Gold

The Fiery Shoals is Nova, the White Queen's seat of power, at the far edge of the Rascor Plains. Referenced only by name in Book 1, it becomes a major setting in Book 2, Chapters 16–20, after Nova rescues the fellowship and brings them there directly by portal.

Origin

The Shoals was not always a fortress: it was once one of the six floating Gold islands that orbit the Farmlands. Centuries ago, someone tampered with the Gold mana stream that kept it aloft, and the island crashed, tearing a two-hundred-mile furrow across the Plains and coming to rest as a permanent lake of lava ringed by black volcanic cliffs. The island's original Gold-drawing mechanism, called the Crucible, was never destroyed; it still operates far beneath the fortress, continuously drawing Gold mana up from the depths and keeping the lava field molten. Its access point, the Golden Cupola, is heavily guarded, most visibly by a Dread Blaze named Bron who has personally watched over it for a decade.

The castle

A sprawling, obsidian fortress of stark, torch-lit halls, home to roughly five thousand people at any given time — mostly transient Bastion-born service staff on month-long rotations, supplemented by House-affiliated Great Souls passing through and smaller independent outfits. Amenities for guests include vast Copper-infused hot pools, a library, a dining hall, and the Cliff Trail, which runs along the lava field's edge past the wind-warped, luminously pink-canopied Lava Trees, whose fruit ("moltens") is prized and exported deeper into Hell. Guests are free to move between the third and sixth floors down; everything below that is restricted.

Preeminence & surveillance

As a Charnel Duchess, Nova held preeminence over the Shoals and much of the surrounding Plains for "almost a decade" (her own words, Book 2, Chapter 21) — a rank-based authority that let her sense powerful fiends the moment they Ignited nearby, and which functioned as ambient security within her own halls. She did not eavesdrop on private conversations as a rule, but channeling significant mana anywhere in the fortress, or coming to open conflict, would draw her attention. This section now describes the arrangement as it stood before Book 2, Chapter 21; see below for what has since changed.

The White Queen's departure (Book 2)

In Book 2, Chapter 21, Nova summons Bastion's Council and the Houses' Autocrators to a grand assembly and announces she is permanently leaving the Fiery Shoals for Deep Hell, withdrawing her preeminence over the Shoals and the Rascor Plains for good. Her stated reasons include a grim accounting of the wider state of Hell — the Blood Ox's conquest of LastRock, unchecked incursions elsewhere, and the Academy's Archspire eventually failing within "less than ten years" — alongside her belief that the Rascor Plains no longer needs her personal presence to hold. She formally hands governance to a restructured Celestial Consortium (see The Four Houses for the full terms of the resulting Queen's Accord) and departs for good at the close of the public signing ceremony in Imperator Square (Book 2, Chapter 30). As of Book 2, Chapter 37, no word of her has reached Bastion since; she is assumed, but not confirmed on the page, to have reached Deep Hell safely.

The Celestial Coffer & the Consortium

Harvested Gold mana is shipped out of the Shoals aboard the Celestial Coffer, an enormous, luxuriously appointed whale ship far grander than any House vessel, captained by Captain Thorne. The ship ultimately belongs to the Iron Tyrant of the Fury Spires, out in the Iron Weald, but is operated by the Celestial Consortium, the multi-party body Nova's Book 2, Chapter 21 restructuring put in place to manage the mana's distribution among the four Houses and Bastion itself once she stepped back from overseeing it personally. See The Four Houses for the ratified distribution split and rotating-leadership terms.

Key figures at the Shoals

Besides Nova and her longtime aide Desiree, notable residents encountered in Book 2 include Kyrie, a young Tomb Spark and past-life fiend scholar who guides the fellowship; Pyre Lady Moira, House Hydra's liaison to the Shoals; the craggy Great Soul Amity, present at both the Imogen battle and Nova's Book 2 council; and a yellow-robed hardliner named Balco, of a group called the Sun Hearts.
