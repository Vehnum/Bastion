The Warrens

Where Bastion disposes of the souls it can't bring itself to kill outright

The Warrens are a network of caves and tunnels beneath the Academy, reached through the Final Door — a one-way passage for Red Listed Great Souls condemned by Pyre Lord Praximar and the Academy's leadership.

Original purpose

The Warrens were designed centuries ago as a trap for monstrous "fiends" that infiltrate Bastion's crumbling outer ruins. A huge portal called the Brass Door opens once a month, drawing wandering fiends inside; once trapped, most die off naturally within a few weeks after the door closes, for reasons no one fully understands. More recently, the same mechanism has been repurposed to dispose of Red List Great Souls without directly killing them — sparing the Academy the risk of a vengeful reincarnation.

Inhabitants

A small community of long-term exiles has survived in the Warrens for years by fortifying a hidden cave, learning safe water sources, and timing their movements around the Brass Door's monthly cycle. When Scorio is thrown through the Final Door, he is found and taken in by four of them: Havert, Hestia, Nissa ("the Flayer of Men" in her past life), and Salamander ("Sal"), the group's longest-tenured member.

Sal claims a former visitor, an explorer named Radert, entered the Warrens voluntarily years earlier, shared secrets and promises of return that never materialized, and is presumed lost. Scorio later finds Radert's corpse and his cache of magical items in a deeper, unmapped cavern.

The failed escape

Using a rope-and-pulley system engineered by Sal, the group nearly forces the Brass Door open, but the mechanism fails at the last second. A monstrous fiend forces the door the rest of the way open from the other side, killing Havert and Hestia in the chaos; Sal and Nissa flee into the tunnels ahead of Scorio, and the group is effectively scattered, their fates beyond that point unknown.

Fleeing alone, Scorio falls into a deeper cavern system, escapes a nest of giant predatory insects, and discovers Radert's remains along with his cache of magical treasures — the discovery that ultimately allows him to climb free of the Warrens entirely.
