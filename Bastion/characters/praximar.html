Pyre Lord Praximar

"You are nothing more than an obligatory duty that I must execute."

Pyre Lord Praximar is the Chancellor of the Academy and Autocrator of House Hydra. He presides over the annual reincarnation ceremony, welcoming each cohort of newly-reborn Great Souls with practiced warmth — a persona that evaporates instantly when Scorio's past life is revealed.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Tall and slender with a broad, expressive face, close-cut iron-gray hair and beard. Wears ornate ceremonial robes and a bejeweled hat for public occasions; switches to severe black robes, belted with a cord of gold, for private business such as sentencing a Red Lister.

Personality

Warm, practiced, and paternal in public — a showman who clearly relishes the ceremony of his role. That warmth is revealed as entirely performative the moment he deals with anyone he considers beneath him or a threat to his authority, at which point he becomes cold, clinical, and casually cruel. Naomi, who saw him from the inside as a menial servant, calls him "a weak excuse for a Pyre Lord" who stopped growing once he'd secured comfort and status.

Biography

Book 1

Role & standing

Praximar has died and been reborn 203 times, one of the highest counts referenced in the story so far. He appears genuinely respected and beloved by the assembled crowd during the ceremony, delivering an emotional speech about the Great Souls' shared purpose before his tone turns clinical and cruel the moment he's dealing with someone he considers a threat.

Condemning Scorio

When the Archspire names Scorio a Red Lister, Praximar has him seized immediately, dismissing the crowd's alarm as routine. In a private holding cell, he coldly informs Scorio that "the seeds of sins past ride upon the soul and never fail to bloom," declares him Proscribed, and personally places a hand on his chest to forcibly drain a portion of his Igneous Heart as "payment" for the inconvenience — a visibly effortful, almost intimate violation that leaves Scorio devastated. He then orders him thrown through the Final Door into the Warrens.

Praximar also references a deeper institutional failure: Bastion has never anointed an Infernarch in nearly a thousand years, and he blames Red List souls like Scorio, at least rhetorically, for that failure.

Facing Imogen

When the Imperator Imogen arrives at Bastion in Book 1, Chapter 38, Praximar is among the first to try to placate her diplomatically, offering hospitality and pleading with her not to force the Dead Portal open. He is ignored and visibly frightened by her power, though he retains enough presence of mind to personally evacuate Scorio's group from the danger zone. He immediately recognizes Scorio as the Red Lister at the center of the incident and, alongside Instructor Helminth, presses him for an explanation, ordering him kept under close watch — an order that lapses amid the ensuing citywide battle.

Forced to readmit Scorio

When the Charnel Duchess Nova informs Praximar that Imperator Sol credits Scorio with helping defeat Imogen, Praximar's composure visibly cracks — "for a moment, tendons stood out in Praximar's neck" — before he recovers and welcomes Scorio back "with all honors," even producing an apology that Scorio can't quite decide is sincere. He readmits Naomi as well at Sol's request, admitting he doesn't even recognize her name.

The final warning

After Scorio and Naomi are involved in a mess-hall altercation with Kuragin, Praximar summons them personally. He punishes Kuragin only lightly, on the grounds that he is "an exemplary student," while giving Scorio and Naomi a final, explicit warning: any further infraction, of any kind, will see them expelled — Imperator's favor notwithstanding. The disparity is not lost on either of them, and confirms Naomi's blunt read of the situation: the Academy wants them gone and will use any excuse it can find.

Blackmailing Scorio

In Book 1, Chapter 53, Praximar summons Scorio privately and reveals he has learned of Scorio's earlier crime with Dola — via a contact he refers to only as "Kayla" — and uses it as leverage rather than grounds for a third, expulsion-triggering infraction. He threatens to force a Heart Oath from Scorio rather than let the matter go through normal channels, and lays out explicit terms in exchange for his silence: Scorio must deliberately lose his next tournament match, must not progress past the sixth chamber of the old Gauntlet during official Academy testing, and must be gone from the Academy by the end of the semester. The scene marks the clearest evidence yet that Praximar's earlier "readmission with all honors" was never sincere.

Trading Naomi's readmission for a Heart Oath

When Scorio needs Naomi formally readmitted to round out his Gauntlet team, Praximar extracts a binding Heart Oath in exchange: if the team fails to outperform Jova Spike in the coming run, both Scorio and Naomi will accept whatever terms of expulsion he chooses. Confident the bet is unwinnable given Scorio's fractured Heart, he agrees readily — a decision that proves catastrophic to his own standing by the semester's end.

Public humiliation

When the Archspire reveals that Scorio, not Jova, went the furthest into the Gauntlet and reached Tomb Spark in the process, Praximar's composure shatters on stage in front of thousands of witnesses; he tries and fails to have the result overturned, then attempts to spin the win as proof of the Academy's own benevolence. Scorio publicly refuses further training and all House sponsorship anyway, and when Praximar tries to disqualify him and hand the victory to Jova instead, she declines it too. Both leave the Academy together in front of the entire assembled cohort and city dignitaries, Praximar shouting threats after them that go unanswered.

Book 2

Ruling Bastion from his study (Book 2)

With Scorio gone, Praximar spends his evenings reviewing weekly Academy class reports by lantern-light and quietly rigging outcomes he considers unsatisfactory — increasing one underperforming Cinder's treasure dosage, ordering another's diluted — a level of behind-the-scenes manipulation of student advancement not previously confirmed on the page. He also keeps a private illustrated archive called the Tome of Souls, profiling history's Imperators, and appears to genuinely mourn the ones who died without ever being reborn (Book 2, Interlude — Praximar).

A telepathic report from Pyre Lady Moira, House Hydra's liaison at the Fiery Shoals, reveals that Nova, the White Queen, intends to permanently withdraw her protection from the Rascor Plains and demand Heart Oaths from Bastion's Autocrators to keep the peace in her absence. Praximar and Moira immediately begin coordinating a response: concede minor, cosmetic points (a non-voting Council seat for the commoners, talk of reduced taxes, opening some wards) while working to protect House Hydra's cut of the Fiery Shoals' Gold mana before any binding Oath takes effect. Praximar then sends an unsigned, deliberately anonymized letter to Eira, Autocrator of House Basilisk, via his aide Veris — the contents and purpose of which are not shown on the page.

Abilities

  • Rank: Pyre Lord, the highest rank attainable while remaining in Bastion.
  • Duplication: can manifest up to five simultaneous illusory duplicates of himself, each apparently capable of independent perception and flight.
  • Institutional leverage: uses information, threats, and the machinery of the Academy itself as weapons, most notably the Book 1, Chapter 53 blackmail of Scorio and the Book 1, Chapter 67 Heart Oath extracted for Naomi's readmission.
  • Covert influence over student advancement (Book 2): personally adjusts individual students' treasure dosages to accelerate or throttle their progress, based on his own judgment of who deserves to advance.

Relationships

Scorio

The man who condemned and physically wounded him. Scorio's private goal of proving himself is, at least in part, aimed squarely at Praximar.

Instructor Helminth

His subordinate at the Academy; the two coordinate closely when investigating Scorio's reappearance during the Imogen crisis.

Pyre Lady Moira

A secret ally and informant: Moira can contact him telepathically across great distances and feeds him real-time intelligence from the Fiery Shoals, including advance warning of Nova's plans for Bastion. The two openly conspire together over Bastion's response (Book 2, Interlude — Praximar).

Trivia

  • His public speech to newly-reincarnated Great Souls draws explicitly on his own memory of waking up disoriented and afraid 203 lifetimes ago.
  • His Book 1, Chapter 53 blackmail terms — throw a match, don't clear the sixth Gauntlet chamber, leave by semester's end — are the first fully explicit statement of what Praximar actually wants from Scorio.
  • His own confident Heart Oath wager over the Gauntlet run backfires completely: he loses both Scorio and Jova Spike, the semester's two Tomb Sparks, to public defection on the same day.
  • Keeps a private book called the Tome of Souls profiling history's Imperators, including several — Moravius the Black, Branzina the Cruel — who died and were never reborn (Book 2, Interlude — Praximar).
  • Sends an anonymous letter to Eira, Autocrator of House Basilisk, immediately after learning of Nova's plans — contents unknown, and a notable contrast to Nissa's claim (see her article) that Basilisk is backing Bastion's rebellion against Hydra and Kraken specifically.
