Pyre Lord Praximar

"You are nothing more than an obligatory duty that I must execute."

Pyre Lord Praximar is the Chancellor of the Academy and Autocrator of House Hydra. He presides over the annual reincarnation ceremony, welcoming each cohort of newly-reborn Great Souls with practiced warmth — a persona that evaporates instantly when Scorio's past life is revealed.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Tall and slender with a broad, expressive face, close-cut iron-gray hair and beard. Wears ornate ceremonial robes and a bejeweled hat for public occasions; switches to severe black robes, belted with a cord of gold, for private business such as sentencing a Red Lister.

Personality

Warm, practiced, and paternal in public — a showman who clearly relishes the ceremony of his role. That warmth is revealed as entirely performative the moment he deals with anyone he considers beneath him or a threat to his authority, at which point he becomes cold, clinical, and casually cruel. Naomi, who saw him from the inside as a menial servant, calls him "a weak excuse for a Pyre Lord" who stopped growing once he'd secured comfort and status.

Biography

Role & standing

Praximar has died and been reborn 203 times, one of the highest counts referenced in the story so far. He appears genuinely respected and beloved by the assembled crowd during the ceremony, delivering an emotional speech about the Great Souls' shared purpose before his tone turns clinical and cruel the moment he's dealing with someone he considers a threat.

Condemning Scorio

When the Archspire names Scorio a Red Lister, Praximar has him seized immediately, dismissing the crowd's alarm as routine. In a private holding cell, he coldly informs Scorio that "the seeds of sins past ride upon the soul and never fail to bloom," declares him Proscribed, and personally places a hand on his chest to forcibly drain a portion of his Igneous Heart as "payment" for the inconvenience — a visibly effortful, almost intimate violation that leaves Scorio devastated. He then orders him thrown through the Final Door into the Warrens.

Praximar also references a deeper institutional failure: Bastion has never anointed an Infernarch in nearly a thousand years, and he blames Red List souls like Scorio, at least rhetorically, for that failure.

Facing Imogen

When the Imperator Imogen arrives at Bastion in Chapter 38, Praximar is among the first to try to placate her diplomatically, offering hospitality and pleading with her not to force the Dead Portal open. He is ignored and visibly frightened by her power, though he retains enough presence of mind to personally evacuate Scorio's group from the danger zone. He immediately recognizes Scorio as the Red Lister at the center of the incident and, alongside Instructor Helminth, presses him for an explanation, ordering him kept under close watch — an order that lapses amid the ensuing citywide battle.

Abilities

  • Rank: Pyre Lord, the highest rank attainable while remaining in Bastion.
  • Duplication: can manifest up to five simultaneous illusory duplicates of himself, each apparently capable of independent perception and flight.

Relationships

Scorio

The man who condemned and physically wounded him. Scorio's private goal of proving himself is, at least in part, aimed squarely at Praximar.

Instructor Helminth

His subordinate at the Academy; the two coordinate closely when investigating Scorio's reappearance during the Imogen crisis.

Trivia

  • His public speech to newly-reincarnated Great Souls draws explicitly on his own memory of waking up disoriented and afraid 203 lifetimes ago.
