Nox

"Favorite Friend Scorio, Leonis, Lianshi"

Nox is a large, eyeless, horned Imperial Ghost Toad who has guarded the sealed Old Academy and its private Gauntlet for longer than anyone at the current Academy knows. First encountered as an unnamed threat, he becomes one of Scorio's most consequential allies in the back half of Book 1.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Large, eyeless, and horned — a toad-fiend built on a scale that dwarfs the small, ordinary "imp" creatures found elsewhere in the Gauntlet. Lives in a burrow-warren beneath the Old Academy's ruins near its monolithic bridge, centered on a pool of his own mana-conductive "imperial gel."

Personality

Simple, literal-minded, and endearingly earnest in speech ("Favorite friend Scorio, Leonis, Lianshi"). Deeply invested in the toad mating "parliaments" held every decade or so, and motivated less by threat or reward than by genuine transactional fairness — he keeps every bargain he makes.

Biography

Nox is first encountered, unnamed, as one of three territorial guardian fiends Scorio, Leonis, and Lianshi fight past when they first discover the Old Academy in Chapter 30 — described at the time only as a talking, engorged tongue-toad. He resurfaces, now named, during Imogen's Chapter 38–40 rampage: grateful to Scorio for a warning given during the chaos, Nox shows him a hidden tunnel entrance that bypasses Chancellor Praximar's seal on the Old Academy entirely.

He becomes central to Scorio's push for Emberling rank in Chapter 61: in exchange for a treatise on toad "Quantics" mating-contest strategy dug out of the Academy library's restricted stacks, Nox teaches Scorio the outline of the Delightful Secret Marinating technique and lets him use his imperial gel pool to attempt it. Nox additionally agrees to guard — and ultimately keep, as payment — a second, much larger Black Star farm Scorio plants nearby, though the arrangement nearly ends in disaster when an Alben Worm surfaces to devour it before Naomi's intervention saves the harvest.

Abilities

  • Imperial gel: a pool of mana-conductive gel central to the Delightful Secret Marinating technique, capable of forcing ignition past a Heart's natural limits.
  • Guardianship: has defended the Old Academy and its still-active reincarnation biers for an unknown, apparently very long span of time.
  • Physical size and toad-fiend combat capability sufficient to have been treated as a serious threat on first encounter.

Relationships

Scorio

Addresses him affectionately as "Favorite Friend Scorio." Their bargain — the Quantics book for the Marinating technique and gel access — is the turning point that gets Scorio to Emberling.

Trivia

  • The wiki's earlier description of Nox as "a small, translucent, floating toad-like fiend" was an error based on incomplete information; he is in fact a large, eyeless, horned guardian with his own burrow-warren.
  • His ultimate prize for helping Scorio — a book about toad mating-contest strategy — underscores how literally the wiki should take his priorities.
