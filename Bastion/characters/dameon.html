Dread Blaze · Manticore's Leader

Dameon

"All plans fall apart the moment the fight begins, anyway."

Dameon is the de facto leader of the five Dread Blazes who founded Manticore, and the one whose approval the fellowship's provisional membership actually depends on. Named but unseen through Book 2's first twenty chapters, he finally appears on the page in Chapter 24 — glib, likeable, and considerably sharper than his easy manner first suggests.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Athletic and tall, broad-shouldered and powerfully built, with golden stubble and carefully coiffed blond hair shaved along the sides and swept up on top.

Personality

Warm, playful, and quick to laugh, running his own team scrimmages like a showman ("Fun. Fun and winning."). Underneath it he's a pragmatist who doesn't soften bad news: he tells Scorio bluntly that fixing his untempered body will cost him "double" whatever anyone else in Manticore has to put in, and later admits, without much apparent guilt, that recruiting Scorio, Naomi, Leonis, and Lianshi at all was mostly a means of securing Jova. He keeps every bargain he strikes, however, and never withdraws an offer once made.

Biography

A name before a face

Dameon is referenced by name from early in Book 2 as the Dread Blaze with final say over whether the fellowship's provisional Manticore membership becomes permanent, but doesn't appear on the page in person until Book 2, Chapter 24, when the fellowship finally meets him in Bastion alongside fellow Dread Blazes Simeon, Davelos, and Evelyn.

Fiend's Heart, and a diagnosis

Dameon runs the fellowship through a friendly capture-the-brick scrimmage, splitting teams and calling plays with genuine tactical skill; his own team wins on a misdirection play from Simeon. Afterward, he pulls Scorio aside and diagnoses a real problem: months of training on nothing but raw Coal mana have left Scorio's body venting mana faster than it can be absorbed into physical resilience, on top of his already-fractured Heart. Dameon still commits to recruiting him, but warns him plainly the fix will take far more work than anyone else in the group requires (Book 2, Chapters 24–25).

To the Chasm

After the Queen's Accord is signed, Dameon leads the fellowship out of Bastion aboard Manticore's whale ship, The Sloop, to the Chasm — Manticore's base of operations, and the site of a specific lost artifact, the Font, that he has spent well over half a year hunting using memories from a past life spent in one of the Chasm's collapsed colonies (Book 2, Chapters 30–33).

The Coal purge, and a hard truth

Dameon personally sets Scorio and Naomi's five-month, Ignition-forbidden purge regimen, checking in periodically and calling their eventual completion of it unprecedented. When he finally ends the regimen, he admits candidly that recruiting the rest of the fellowship was mostly a "package deal" to secure Jova — whose stone-mastery power he considers formidable enough to change what Manticore can accomplish — and takes a personal hand in her training afterward, escorting her himself to temper on rare Bronze mana (Book 2, Chapters 34–38).

The vault raid

With House Kraken's Pyre Lady Druanna brought in to force open a collapsed vault holding the Font, Dameon leads the raid himself, descending to the Chasm's Gold level with six other Dread Blazes. The raid, expected to take about two hours, stretches to nearly three full day-night cycles. Book 2, Chapter 40 ends on an unresolved cliffhanger: Dameon is the one who returns to the surface, carrying Druanna's body — but Dread Blazes Evelyn, Simeon, Mavel, and Tsing Ma are all missing from the cage. What happened below, and whether the Font was recovered, is not yet known.

Abilities

  • Forcefields: protective bubbles around himself or others; effectiveness weakens the more he generates simultaneously.
  • Charged strike: a heavy punch that grows stronger the longer he holds the charge before releasing it.
  • Dread Blaze power — path visualization: can visualize different courses of action and gauge their probability of success, useful for long-term strategy but of no help mid-fight.
  • Past-life memory retention: uniquely among Manticore's Dread Blazes, Dameon retains memories and learned skills from (at least) one specific past life, which Naomi identifies on sight as "an Imperator's power." Simeon confirms Dameon gained this specifically upon reaching Dread Blaze, and calls it the source of his unusual versatility and his knowledge of the Chasm's artifact. The text does not state what rank Dameon's own past life actually was — only that the ability to carry memories forward this way is characteristic of Imperators.

Relationships

Scorio

Recruits him with eyes open to the cost, diagnoses his untempered body bluntly, and sets him a punishing recovery regimen — but never wavers on the offer itself, and calls Scorio and Naomi's completion of it unprecedented.

Jova Spike

His real reason for recruiting the fellowship at all, by his own later admission. Takes a personal hand in her training, and per Lianshi, is one of the only people who can make her laugh.

Simeon, Davelos & Evelyn

Fellow founding Dread Blazes of Manticore — see Simeon, Davelos, and Evelyn's individual pages. Simeon, Evelyn, and two of House Kraken's Dread Blazes go missing alongside him during the Book 2, Chapter 40 vault raid; Dameon alone returns to the surface.

Trivia

  • Named "Camp Manticore" himself — "as I so creatively named it," by his own dry admission.
  • Runs an impromptu team-building game called "Fiend's Heart" as an informal way of evaluating new recruits' tactics and teamwork.
  • Was previously noted as having been "touched" by Pyre Lady Moira of House Hydra, treated as an ongoing security concern as of Book 2, Chapter 20; the matter has not been mentioned again on the page since.
