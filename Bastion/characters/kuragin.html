Kuragin

"Sir Kuragin, the King's Warmonger" — or so he claims to remember

Kuragin is one of the cohort's top-five Emberlings, massive and openly hostile, with a monstrous crab-like transformation. He is Scorio's most persistent personal rival across Book 1 — not through any organized House scheme, but through two separate, escalating one-on-one confrontations.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Physically massive even before transforming; his Emberling power adds huge digging claws, a segmented carapace shell, and a club-tipped tail, giving him a crab-like combat silhouette unlike anyone else in the top five.

Personality

Blunt, aggressive, and quick to escalate — but not dishonorable. He publicly accuses Scorio of treachery without much evidence, but honors the humiliating terms of his own unsanctioned duel with Scorio once he loses it, even furious and freshly beaten.

Biography

Kuragin first collides with Scorio in the mess hall (Chapter 48), publicly accusing him of having sided with Imogen during her assault before switching allegiance at the last second. Scorio headbutts him in response, nearly getting both himself and Naomi expelled; Kuragin is punished lightly (groundskeeping duty under Master Pully) compared to Scorio and Naomi's final warning — a disparity Naomi predicts before it even happens. He goes on to win the Academy's tournament outright and enters the final Gauntlet run on Jova Spike's team.

The night before that run, Scorio provokes him into a second, unsanctioned duel — the stakes being that regardless of outcome, Scorio refuses all healing before the Gauntlet begins. Kuragin loses badly once Scorio reveals a hidden Emberling transformation, but honors the terms and reveals what his own Emberling trial showed him of Scorio's past life: as "Sir Kuragin, the King's Warmonger," he claims to have captured Scorio's past-life brother Eberro after Scorio himself betrayed and discarded him once he'd outlived his usefulness to their rebellion — a claim Scorio cannot reconcile with his own trial memories, and which the wiki has not been able to confirm as "true," given how Great Soul memories are known to branch across different past lives. As of Chapter 80, Kuragin remains at the Academy; Scorio has left with the fellowship without the claim ever being resolved.

Abilities

Relationships

Jova Spike

Trivia

  • Wins the Academy's tournament outright in Chapter 60, the same tournament that ends Scorio's own run.
  • The truth of his Eberro-betrayal claim is unresolved as of Chapter 80 — a loose thread the wiki flags as worth revisiting once Book 2 clarifies more about how past-life memory branching works.
