Character Index · Supporting Cast

Other Characters

Everyone besides Scorio, Lianshi, Leonis, and Naomi — the four characters expected to remain central throughout the series. This page is just a directory: full articles for names with enough material, and a one-line index for everyone else, each linking out to their own (sometimes very short) page.

Full articles

Pyre Lord · Antagonist Pyre Lord Praximar

Chancellor of the Academy. Condemned and drained Scorio's Heart in Book 1, Chapter 6.

 Tomb Spark · Fellowship Jova Spike

The name carved into Scorio's own empty locker turned out to hold a warning — and now she's leaving Bastion with him.

 Imperator · Antagonist Imogen

A grief-shattered, Fallen Imperator descends on Bastion — and is finally stopped by her own estranged husband.

 House Basilisk Dola

The crew boss whose job — and stolen Sapphire mana — started Scorio down his current path.

 Imperator Sol

Imogen's estranged husband. His duel with her, and Scorio's role in it, ends Bastion's crisis.

 Emberling · Rival Kuragin

A persistent rival with a claim about Scorio's past-life brother the wiki still can't confirm.

 Emberling · Fellowship Juniper

A name out of Lianshi's own centuries-old journals, now walking out of Bastion with the fellowship.

 Emberling · Fellowship Zala

Jova's best friend, following her out of Bastion without needing to understand why.

 Imperial Ghost Toad Nox

The Old Academy's guardian toad-fiend, and an unlikely key to Scorio's Emberling breakthrough.

 Charnel Duchess · Ruler of the Fiery Shoals Nova, "the White Queen"

Led Bastion's defense against Imogen. Now central to Book 2: she's leaving the Fiery Shoals for good.

 Red Lister · House Basilisk Asset Nissa

The Warrens exile who once abandoned Scorio returns in Book 2 as a rebel agitator secretly working for House Basilisk.

 Dread Blaze · Manticore's Leader Dameon

Finally appears in person in Book 2, Chapter 24 — and turns out to carry an Imperator's power: memories from a past life.

 Dread Blaze · Manticore Founder Davelos

Skeptical of seven new recruits at once — and the one who tests them all personally in the Farmlands.

 Dread Blaze · Manticore Founder Evelyn

The first of Manticore's founders the fellowship meets — now missing after the Book 2, Chapter 40 vault raid.

 Manticore · Pilot of The Sloop Ydrielle

Guides The Sloop over the Rain Wall with a mana-manipulation skill even Scorio can't fully parse.
