Other Characters

Street musicians, later club owners · Chapters 9–22

Feiyan & Helena

Two commoner street performers Scorio meets in the ruins just after climbing out of the Warrens. Feiyan is blunt, muscular, and wary, a former Basilisk operative who's gone straight; Helena is a charming, glib fiddler. They trade Scorio food, clothes, and an introduction to Dola in exchange for a stolen glowing "sunphire," which funds a successful nightlife venue, the Flame, in wealthy Ward 11. Feiyan remains wary of Scorio but agrees, for a price, to look into smuggling him and Naomi onto the Rascor Plains.

Deceased explorer · Chapter 9

Radert

An explorer who once entered the Warrens deliberately through the Brass Door, spent time there, and promised to return for his friend Salamander — a promise never kept. Scorio finds his mummified remains deep in an unmapped cavern, along with the bridge, steel rod, and chalk that become his core toolkit for the rest of the book.

"The Hell Whip" · Dread Blaze · Chapters 6, 27, 38, 47–50

Instructor Helminth

The Academy instructor who physically drags Scorio away after his Red List reveal in Chapter 6. A Dread Blaze nicknamed "the Hell Whip" by students, with a whip-and-gravity combat style and a sharp inferential mind. She nearly identifies Scorio when he infiltrates the Academy disguised as a courier ("Memek") in Chapter 27, and does identify him during Imogen's assault in Chapter 38, pressing him for an explanation before the crisis overtakes everyone's attention.

Also serves as Geography of Hell instructor and the tournament's presiding official. Grants Scorio's Chapter 47 request to enter the loser's bracket, then publicly offers any of the sixty-four remaining loser's-bracket contestants the chance to earn glory by fighting him in a preliminary bout — the move that gets him into the tournament at all. Her private training regimen involves squatting under an iron bar heavy enough to crack the stone floor while conducting business as usual.

History & Mana Studies instructor · Dread Blaze · Chapters 44, 46

Instructor Hera

Severe and exacting in the lecture hall, warm and searching in private one-on-one meditation sessions — a contrast Scorio finds genuinely disorienting. Teaches both History of Bastion (five documented historical eras, from the Red Peace to the present) and Mana Studies. It's Hera who diagnoses the severe fracturing in Scorio's Heart, and who explains the mechanics of Heart burn-off, the Fallen, and the Four Trials to him in depth — see The Igneous Heart & Mana for details.

Sparring & Forms instructor · Dread Blaze · Chapters 44–45

Instructor Feng

A precise, unhurried hand-to-hand specialist whose signature power is a cone of pale dust that slows anything caught in it while accelerating him; anyone in the cone who loses direct sight of him also briefly forgets he's there. Defeats both Scorio and Naomi in their first sparring bouts, but singles out Naomi for particular humiliation — a callback to having refused to train her years earlier and telling her, in front of a class, that she "wasn't a true Great Soul."

Tactics instructor · Dread Blaze · Chapters 46, 48

Instructor Rex

Blunt, weathered, and openly contemptuous of sloppy thinking, Rex runs team-tactics drills in a cavernous hall whose terrain and "ghost" enemies he shapes at will using a large purple gem, the Eye. His stated priorities are teamwork, adaptability, and never assuming anything about the battlefield — a lesson Scorio's team learns the hard way when supposedly solid walls prove to conceal clawed enemies.

Apothecary · Chapters 19–37

Jelan

The proprietor of the Sincere Refinery, a small apothecary shop. Teaches Scorio (and later helps Naomi) process Black Star flowers into concentrated mana pills and tinctures, and explains the underlying mechanics of mana grades, mana traps, and fiend heartstones. Dry, transactional, occasionally droll.

Oud musician · Chapter 22

Old Memek

A garrulous, self-mythologizing elderly street musician who briefly guides Scorio through Ward 11 in search of Feiyan and Helena's club. Comic relief; Scorio later borrows his name as a cover identity when infiltrating the Academy.

Fellow Red List exiles · Chapters 6–10

The Warrens Group: Havert, Hestia, Nissa & Salamander

The small community of long-term exiles Scorio finds sheltering in the Warrens. Havert (stocky, wry, cheroot-smoking) and Hestia (freckled, wary, ~1 year in the Warrens) find him half-dead by an underground stream. Nissa ("the Flayer of Men" in her past life, ~3 years in) has grown quietly nihilistic about their odds of survival. Salamander ("Sal"), the group's longest-tenured member (~5 years), engineers their escape attempt using a rope-and-pulley system to force open the Brass Door.

The escape goes badly: a monstrous fiend forces the door the rest of the way open, killing Havert and Hestia; Nissa mercy-kills Havert mid-crisis in a moment of panic. Sal flees through the door into fiend territory, abandoning the others. Hestia is caught and dragged off while Scorio tries to free her. Sal and Nissa's fates beyond that point are unknown.

Bastion's assembled defenders · Chapters 38–40

Defenders of Bastion

A large ensemble of high-ranking Great Souls who converge to delay Imogen's assault on Bastion, coordinated by the tactician Desiree under the overall command of Nova, the White Queen. None of them expect to defeat her — only to buy time for reinforcements.

The cohort's Top Five · Chapters 44, 49

The Cohort's Top Five (overview)

The cohort of roughly 500 Great Souls is ranked by treasure tier, from one-notch badges (bottom 300, "Black Star" pills) up to the top five, who receive "one of everything" daily. Alongside Jova Spike, the year's top five were Ravenna Accardi, Zala, Kuragin, and Chen She. See their individual entries (or full articles) for how each fared once the tournament and Gauntlet run concluded.

Emberling, later Tomb Spark · Chapters 63, 68

Ravenna Accardi

Jova's public rival for most of the semester, favored by House Hydra once Jova turned the sponsorship down, and capable of tearing huge chunks of stone from the ground to hurl as projectiles. Faces Jova in what onlookers call the tournament's best match, landing hit after hit and very nearly winning before Jova abruptly reaches Tomb Spark mid-fight; Ravenna's own attacks then begin missing entirely as Jova's new aura overwhelms her nerve, and she is shoved from the ring in terrified retreat rather than beaten outright.

Emberling · Top Five · Chapters 63, 79

Chen She

Unnervingly pleasant on the surface; Leonis warns Scorio to fear him most of the top five. Wins a brutal, bloody tournament bout against Kuragin, and ultimately places third in the final Gauntlet run, reaching the thirteenth chamber — a dramatic improvement over his first Gauntlet attempt, in which he reached only the third. Sponsored by House Kraken.

Fellow Cinders · Chapter 43

Exero & Famissa

Two Cinders assigned alongside Scorio to help restore order in Ward 4 after Imogen's assault. Exero, a Flame Vault with whip-like green-flame constructs, is patient but privately contemptuous of Scorio's ignorance of basic Academy facts; Famissa is warmer and openly curious about him, and offers — somewhat pointedly — to show him around the Academy.

House Chimera · Chapter 50

Selena, Wrack & Gelegos

House Chimera's recruitment team, who approach Scorio immediately after his round-of-32 tournament win. Principal Recruiter Selena (a Dread Blaze) leads the interview, direct and shrewd, ultimately extending Scorio's sponsorship offer herself. Recruiter Wrack is clinical and openly skeptical, probing for signs of opportunism in Scorio's account of the Imogen fight. Recruiter Gelegos is affable and eccentric, and personally evaluates Scorio's Heart using an Attenuation Spider crystal ("Crystal Gauge"), rating his capacity as high Iron verging on Bronze — rank 35, unusually strong for a Cinder.

Tournament opponent · Chapter 60

Chloe

Scorio's opponent in the tournament bout that ends his run in Chapter 60. Fights with a short-range teleportation power that lets her reposition instantly around an opponent, which she uses to out-maneuver Scorio's slower Iron-mana reflexes rather than overpower him directly.

Praximar's informant · Chapter 53 (mentioned)

Kayla

Referenced, not yet seen directly on the page: the source through whom Chancellor Praximar learns of Scorio's crime with Dola, giving him the leverage for his Chapter 53 blackmail. Her exact connection to Dola's crew, or to Praximar, is not yet explained.

Unrest instigator · Chapter 59 (mentioned)

Makiros

Named in connection with a violent incident in Bastion's streets during the deepening civic unrest following Imogen's assault — part of a wider pattern of hostility toward Great Souls that the book has begun tracking alongside Scorio's own arc. Details remain secondhand.

Tournament fighter · Chapter 57

Massamach

One of Jova Spike's tournament opponents, briefly featured in a bout that further establishes the gap between the cohort's top five and everyone else. Physically imposing, straightforward brawling style; defeated by Jova without much difficulty.
