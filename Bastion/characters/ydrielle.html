Ydrielle

"Nobody flies like Ydrielle."

Ydrielle pilots Manticore's whale ship, The Sloop — an elite mana-manipulation specialist rather than a Dread Blaze, and one of the few crew members with a skill Scorio himself can't fully parse. Confirmed on the page in Book 2, Chapter 31, correcting an earlier, more speculative note on this wiki naming a different crew member.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

A shaved head, sallow and sunken features "as if recovering from a long-term illness," and dark, bird-like eyes.

Personality

Reserved, in a way that takes real effort to warm up to — a contrast to the more immediately welcoming Manticore founders like Evelyn. Openly affectionate with Simeon specifically, suggesting the reserve is more a general default than a fixed trait.

Biography

Crossing the Rain Wall

Ydrielle guides The Sloop up and over the Rain Wall by releasing and shaping Iron, Copper, and Bronze mana with a precision Scorio calls beyond anything he'd thought possible, including weaving a repulsion field to pull the ship free of a Coal vortex mid-crossing. She uses a technique for splitting an incoming mana stream between active Ignition and a stored compression tank — recognizably similar in principle to Nox's Delightful Secret Marinating Technique, though her own version is unrelated in origin and immediately draws her scrutiny when Scorio recognizes it (Book 2, Chapters 31–33).

The vault raid

Unlike Simeon, Evelyn, and Druanna's escorts Mavel and Tsing Ma, Ydrielle is not among those missing after the Book 2, Chapter 40 vault raid — she remains topside at Camp Manticore throughout.

Abilities

  • Rank: Not a Dread Blaze; an elite mana-manipulation specialist, evaluated by grade rather than the Path of Ascension.
  • Precision mana shaping: releases and shapes Iron, Copper, and Bronze mana with a control Scorio describes as beyond anything he'd thought possible, used to guide The Sloop through the Rain Wall's hazards.
  • Repulsion fields: capable of weaving a repulsion field strong enough to pull an entire ship free of a Coal vortex mid-crossing.
  • Split-stream compression technique: divides an incoming mana stream between active Ignition and a stored compression tank — similar in principle, though unrelated in origin, to Nox's Delightful Secret Marinating Technique.

Relationships

Simeon

In a romantic relationship with the founding Dread Blaze; the two are openly affectionate on the page. Missing alongside Simeon is not the case for Ydrielle as of Book 2, Chapter 40.

Scorio

Her mana-shaping technique draws her scrutiny the moment Scorio recognizes its similarity to Nox's Delightful Secret Marinating Technique.

Trivia

  • Her confirmation on the page in Chapter 31 corrected an earlier, more speculative note on this wiki naming a different crew member; that earlier name does not recur in the text and appears to have been an early misreading.
