Jova Spike

The name carved into Scorio's own empty locker

Jova Spike was the standout Great Soul of this year's reincarnation cohort from the moment she stepped off her bier — fastest of the three who reached the fifth chamber of the Gauntlet, and the first of anyone in the cohort to pass her First Trial and reach Emberling. As of Book 1, Chapter 51, she and Scorio have finally spoken directly — and the exchange has bound their fates together for the rest of the semester.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Medium height, light brown skin, black hair shorn close on one side and left long on the other, falling past her right shoulder. Moves with a panther-like intensity and a near-permanent frown; keeps to herself and rarely engages with the crowds around her.

Personality

Extraordinarily disciplined and famously strong-willed. She keeps to herself and to two close roommates/friends, Juniper and Zala, otherwise holding even the Houses courting her at arm's length — she notably refused a full House Hydra patronage offer outright, without explanation, causing a minor scandal. She takes Academy rules with total seriousness, once publicly returning an extra Apotheosis pill she'd been mistakenly issued twice rather than keep an unearned advantage, unnerving the staff member involved. She appears to live almost entirely for training; some speculate her will alone could carry her to Tomb Spark before most of her peers even reach Emberling.

Biography

Book 1

The locker mystery

When Scorio finally gains access to his own Academy locker, it is completely empty except for one thing: Jova Spike's name, carved deep and deliberately into the back wall. Scorio, Leonis, and Lianshi reason this out together — a past-life Blood Baron as notorious as Scorio's former self would have known his locker was certain to be looted the instant he died, making any hidden treasure or journal there pointless. Leaving a name instead, entrusted to someone "nobody would mess with in their right mind," suggests Scorio's past self left something of real importance in Jova's keeping — and that the two of them likely had some kind of significant relationship or arrangement in that past life.

The problem: Jova is intensely private, has no patience for Academy politics, and would very plausibly turn Scorio in on principle if he approached her carelessly. As of Book 1, Chapter 40, Scorio has not yet found a way to safely make contact.

A possible connection to Lianshi's past?

Jova's close friend Juniper shares a name with a figure from Lianshi's own centuries-old journals — a person from one of Lianshi's countless past lives who Lianshi wondered, with some dread, "might seek her revenge and draw my blood." Whether this is the same soul, the same person reincarnated, or simply a coincidence of naming has not been established. Juniper has since surfaced twice more: once volunteering to tutor Scorio (Book 1, Chapter 44), and again competing in the tournament's winner's bracket as one of the cohort's newer Emberlings (Book 1, Chapter 55), with a power that leaves a hovering, blinding after-image of light in an opponent's eyes with each landed punch.

The confrontation and the wager

Freshly sponsored by House Chimera, Scorio finally approaches Jova directly to ask her to check her own journals and mementos for any record of him from 233 years ago, the timeframe of his last death. She refuses flatly, on principle: he is a Red Lister, and no House sponsorship or Imperator endorsement will change that fact in her eyes. Stung, Scorio needles her into a wager: if he outperforms her in the semester's final Gauntlet run, she'll help him search her records; if he doesn't, he swears, as a Great Soul, to withdraw from the Academy entirely, even if invited to continue into the second semester. Jova accepts on the spot, warning him the consequences of breaking his word "will be lethal." Neither of them mentions a formal Heart Oath, but both friends and Scorio himself treat the promise as unbreakable.

The sparring duel

When students from the tournament's winner's bracket are rotated through the cohort's sparring classes as informal opponents, Scorio publicly requests to fight Jova instead of his assigned opponent, Kuragin. She accepts. Under no-powers rules, Scorio lands two of the fight's three scoring points against her — a genuine surprise to everyone watching — before she ends it with a single spinning kick. Her reaction afterward is uncharacteristically raw: she snarls "I won't believe your lies!" at him as he lies dazed on the ground, an outburst that doesn't obviously follow from anything said during the bout itself, and which Scorio can't parse.

Versus Massamach

Jova's round-of-32 tournament bout against Massamach gives the clearest demonstration yet of her manifested power: she deliberately absorbs a string of brutal, bone-breaking blows from his terrain-devastating leaps, taking heavier and heavier damage without ever retaliating, until her crackling black aura reaches full intensity and she becomes functionally impervious. She then closes the distance and ends the fight in two strikes. Lianshi speculates she deliberately let herself be hurt just to speed up the process, rather than needing to.

Reaching Tomb Spark

In the tournament's final winner's bracket, Jova faces Ravenna Accardi — the cohort's other standout — in what onlookers call the best match of the semester. Ravenna very nearly wins, battering Jova with an escalating barrage of hurled boulders, but at the brink of defeat Jova abruptly vanishes mid-fight: she has broken through to her Second Trial and returns moments later as a fully healed Tomb Spark, complete with a defensive shroud and an intimidating aura that reduces Ravenna to terrified flight before Jova ends the match. She is only the first of two Great Souls in the cohort to reach Tomb Spark before the semester's end — the second being Scorio himself.

The journal message

Prompted by Praximar's mention that Scorio last died 233 years ago, Jova locates and rereads an entry from one of her own past-life journals, describing a visit from a hunted Blood Baron she'd known at the time who left her an encoded warning meant for his own future self: not to trust the Houses, the Imperators, or a group referred to only as "the Herdsmen," and that the only way to break Bastion's cycle is to become the Infernarch. She privately makes a bet with herself — she will believe the two-centuries-old warning, and Scorio's identity as the man who left it, only if he manages to best her at the coming Gauntlet run, a feat she considers essentially impossible.

The Gauntlet run & besting Scorio

Jova enters the Gauntlet with a team drawn from the tournament's other finalists — Kuragin, Chen She, and Chloe — rather than her own close friends, and pushes herself brutally hard from the outset. She reaches the eighteenth chamber, a room of five rotating platforms guarded by masked executioners, fighting on through catastrophic wounds with sheer will after her team has scattered around her, and is finally stopped on the third platform. Scorio, arriving at the same chamber shortly after via the Gauntlet's shared final-vision effect, sees how far she got and pushes one platform further himself — besting her by a single chamber and winning their wager.

Second place, and a choice

Learning she placed second overall, Jova watches Scorio publicly refuse Praximar's terms and any House sponsorship, then does the same herself — declining to return to the Academy or accept the "reward" of the Gauntlet's own runner-up position. She shares her journal's warning with Scorio directly in her own suite afterward, and the two agree, warily, to trust each other and pursue the truth behind it together. She leaves the Academy alongside him at Book 1's end, taking her own closest friends, Juniper and Zala, with her.

Book 2

Fleeing Bastion & the horror beneath

Jova leaves the Academy alongside the rest of the fellowship and takes part in the flight from Praximar's bounty, the killing of Flame Vault Xiang Xi, and the ten-day truce Scorio brokers with Bastion's rebel leadership (Book 2, Chapters 1–6). On the road to the Fiery Shoals, she and Scorio grow markedly closer; she opens up, in her own guarded way, about finding real friendship exhausting and about respecting Scorio specifically for sharing her drive. At his prompting, she demonstrates her Tomb Spark power on him directly for the first time on the page: her body erupts into a Lovecraftian mass of extra faces, teeth, and stretching flesh, accompanied by a near-subsonic shriek — a horror so effective that Scorio is left seeing afterimages of it for hours. Jova has never seen the transformation herself and has asked her friends not to describe it to her (Book 2, Chapter 11).

The two also work out, together, a new theory of advancement: that rank breakthroughs come from genuine self-honesty and confidence, not just Heart training — Jova frames Flame Vault specifically as a question of raising a Heart's "reactivity" until Ignition becomes reflexive (Book 2, Chapter 11).

The Ixithilion attack

During a goldyolk swarm near one of the Rascor Plains' floating Gold islands, Jova is gravely wounded twice over by a pack of Ixithilions — punched through the chest and stomach — but her power lets her keep fighting despite it. Believing she is about to die alongside Scorio, she begins to say something more personal to him before catching herself and running to help Davelos instead; both are healed moments later when Nova, the White Queen, arrives and saves the group (Book 2, Chapter 15–16).

Abilities

  • Igneous Heart: Ignited to Tomb Spark during the tournament's final match against Ravenna Accardi — the first in her cohort to do so.
  • Manifested power: a crackling black aura that grows stronger the more she is wounded — a damage-and-retaliation-scaling ability that makes her exceptionally dangerous to fight head-on, and appears to make her functionally immune to further harm once sufficiently triggered.
  • Shroud: a Tomb Spark-rank defensive barrier gained alongside her Second Trial.
  • Tomb Spark aura: an area-effect power capable of overwhelming a lesser opponent's will and courage outright, demonstrated against Ravenna Accardi.
  • Manifested power, clarified (Book 2): shown on the page for the first time — a full-body transformation into a shrieking, multi-faced horror that makes it viscerally difficult for onlookers to look at or fight her, rather than a purely defensive damage-scaling aura. Jova refuses to look at it herself.

Relationships

Scorio

Once dismissed as an unreformable Red Lister, now Scorio's closest ally outside his original circle. Their wager over the Gauntlet run, and the two-century-old warning she carried for him without knowing it, bind their futures together as they leave the Academy at Book 1's end. See Scorio's article for the locker mystery in full.

Juniper & Zala

Her roommates and only known close friends. Both choose to leave the Academy alongside her without hesitation once she declines Praximar's terms.

Scorio (Book 2)

The two grow noticeably closer on the road to the Fiery Shoals, trading Tomb Spark theory and, at his request, her true manifested form. At a moment both believe may be their last, she starts to say something more personal to him before catching herself — left unresolved as of Book 2, Chapter 15.

Trivia

  • Past-life titles: Queen of the Crimson Coast, the Enduring, Sovereign Light, Avenger of the Lost Vale. Highest rank attained: Imperator. Died less than a year before this incarnation, at LastRock; reborn 163 times.
  • Her outburst after their sparring duel — "I won't believe your lies!" — turns out to have been a reaction to a warning message she'd already half-remembered from her own journals, not simple Red Lister prejudice.
  • Placed second overall in the Gauntlet, immediately behind Scorio.
  • Was already known by reputation to Manticore's Dread Blaze Evelyn before ever meeting her — Evelyn recognized her on sight as "Jova Spike, the Charnel Duchess of LastRock," and knew that her death was what allowed a rival power, "the Blood Ox," to finally conquer LastRock in her absence (Book 2, Chapter 13).
