Instructor Helminth

"The Hell Whip." Nearly caught Scorio twice.

The Academy instructor who physically drags Scorio away after his Red List reveal in Book 1, Chapter 6. A Dread Blaze nicknamed "the Hell Whip" by students, with a whip-and-gravity combat style and a sharp inferential mind. She nearly identifies Scorio when he infiltrates the Academy disguised as a courier ("Memek") in Book 1, Chapter 27, and does identify him during Imogen's assault in Book 1, Chapter 38, pressing him for an explanation before the crisis overtakes everyone's attention.

Also serves as Geography of Hell instructor and the tournament's presiding official. Grants Scorio's Book 1, Chapter 47 request to enter the loser's bracket, then publicly offers any of the sixty-four remaining loser's-bracket contestants the chance to earn glory by fighting him in a preliminary bout — the move that gets him into the tournament at all. Her private training regimen involves squatting under an iron bar heavy enough to crack the stone floor while conducting business as usual.
