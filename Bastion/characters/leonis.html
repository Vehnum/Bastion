Leonis

"I couldn't wish for better comrades with whom to face an infuriatingly obtuse death."

Leonis is the third member of Scorio and Lianshi's group in the Gauntlet. He is the group's protector and morale-booster, quick to volunteer for danger and to crack jokes in the worst possible moments.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Bear-like and broad-shouldered, easily the largest of the group, with long hair usually worn loose or gathered into a bun and a beard he keeps trimmed once he can afford to. At the Academy he dresses with a deliberately relaxed, slightly slapdash flair — open robes, strings of polished wooden beads and silver thread.

Personality

Warm, loud, and instinctively protective, with a booming, battlefield-ready voice and a running stream of self-deprecating humor that rarely lets up even in genuine danger. Underneath the jokes is real loyalty and real worry — he's the one most likely to say out loud that a plan has gone too far, and the one who most openly presses Scorio to slow down.

Biography

Book 1

The Gauntlet and its aftermath

He dies in the Dark Room, throat torn out defending Scorio and Lianshi from a trio of imp-like creatures, and is reborn normally at the Academy. He is present alongside Lianshi when Scorio is exposed as a Red Lister and physically tries to intervene, without success, and spends the following months at the Academy still trying, without success, to find a way to help his lost friend.

Reunion & renewed brotherhood

Leonis is overjoyed to find Scorio alive at the Graveyard market in Book 1, Chapter 23, immediately pressing money and gifts on him ("friends don't stand on pride") and arranging his own Academy delivery privileges to smuggle Scorio inside to check his locker. He and Lianshi then join Scorio's weekly training runs into the ruins every Eighthday, treating it as genuine, valuable combat training rather than a favor — and, after discovering the Old Academy's still-functioning private Gauntlet, throws himself into repeated death-training alongside Scorio and Lianshi, despite finding the experience genuinely horrifying ("criminally insane murderers," he calls his own friend group, fondly).

He is the one who most openly worries about Scorio's growing recklessness, extracting a promise of "moderation" from him after a training session leaves all three of them badly shaken. He is present for the locker's Jova Spike revelation and for Imogen's assault on Bastion, fleeing into the Gauntlet alongside the others as Book 1, Chapter 40 ends.

Life at the Academy resumes

With Scorio and Naomi readmitted, Leonis welcomes them both into his and Lianshi's suite without hesitation, immediately dubbing it a "toddler's version" of an introduction to Academy life to spare Naomi's pride. He remains the group's emotional anchor: it's Leonis who physically intervenes in the mess-hall confrontation with Kuragin (nearly losing his jaw to a claw swipe for it), and Leonis who delivers a blistering, near-violent pep talk before Scorio's first tournament bout, forcing him to own the scale of what he's already survived rather than dismiss it as luck.

He was eliminated from his own tournament bracket earlier in the semester — teased mercilessly by Lianshi about the exact circumstances, which he refuses to specify beyond insisting his opponent was "a beast... of primal power and unearthly rage."

The pledge

In Book 1, Chapter 55, with Scorio wavering over House Chimera's offer, Leonis delivers an unusually serious, unguarded speech pledging his own loyalty regardless of houses, rank, or politics — framing the group's bond as something that predates and outranks any Academy structure. It's a rare moment of Leonis dropping the humor entirely, and one Scorio leans on later when the Chimera relationship sours.

A broken elbow in the old Gauntlet

Pushing himself during a Book 1, Chapter 58 climb through the Old Academy's private Gauntlet, Leonis takes a bad fall and breaks his elbow badly enough to need real recovery time — a blunt reminder, alongside Scorio's own fractured Heart, of how much risk the group has been absorbing training unsupervised. He shrugs the injury off publicly, true to form, but it visibly rattles Lianshi and Naomi.

Reaching Emberling: Nezzar's true nature

Leonis makes Emberling mid-run during a private Gauntlet training session in Book 1, Chapter 63, vanishing into his First Trial and returning noticeably subdued. His trial revisits the moment his past self, a child king of twelve, was forced by his mother's cold command to descend into a coliseum and kill his own usurping uncle in single combat before the assembled kingdom — the origin of both his title, the Golden King, and his weapon, Nezzar. He does not fully open up about the experience even to Scorio and Lianshi, and privately wrestles, alone on a hidden ledge above the Academy, with whether he is his own man or simply living up to his mother's expectations.

The Gauntlet's final run

Leonis joins Scorio, Lianshi, and Naomi for the semester's climactic Gauntlet attempt as an Emberling, fighting with Nezzar throughout and taking a severe leg wound crossing a poisoned pool chamber. In the twelfth chamber, swarmed by a horde of small fiends, Leonis holds the line alone to buy Scorio time, urging him with his final effort to press on rather than look back.

Leaving the Academy

Fiercely proud of Scorio's eventual win, Leonis is the first to leap up and cheer when Scorio is named the Gauntlet's overall victor, and joins the new seven-person fellowship — alongside Scorio, Lianshi, Naomi, Jova Spike, Juniper, and Zala — that leaves the Academy at Book 1's end.

Book 2

On the road (Book 2)

Leonis leaves Bastion with the rest of the fellowship, fights alongside them against Praximar's bounty-hunting classmates and, later, a goldyolk swarm and a pack of Ixithilions on the Rascor Plains. Still an Emberling as of Book 2, Chapter 20, he takes his sparring losses to Manticore's Dread Blazes Evelyn and Davelos in stride — Davelos breaks his nose in one exchange — and leans into black comedy about the fellowship's odds rather than dwelling on it. He's a willing subject in Scorio's coaching push to get Naomi, Lianshi, and himself to Tomb Spark before reaching the Fiery Shoals (Book 2, Chapters 8–14).

The Chasm (Book 2, Chapters 30–40)

Recruited onto Dameon's own team for a friendly Manticore scrimmage in Bastion, Leonis gets knocked out cold by a charged punch and revived by a Manticore elixir — his first real taste of Dread Blaze-caliber combat (Book 2, Chapter 25). At the Chasm, he trains properly alongside Lianshi, Zala, and Juniper while Scorio and Naomi are pulled into a separate, much harsher regimen; visibly uncomfortable with the growing gap between the two halves of the group, he and Lianshi both hold off swearing full Manticore membership out of solidarity, waiting for Scorio and Naomi to catch up (Book 2, Chapter 37). Still an Emberling as of Book 2, Chapter 40.

Abilities

  • Igneous Heart: Ignited to Emberling in Book 1, Chapter 63, trained through official Academy resources.
  • Nezzar: a massive hexagonal stone club inscribed with burning orange runes, inherited from his past life as the Golden King; capable of shattering fiends and stone alike in a single swing.
  • Physical strength: the group's frontline brawler by build and instinct, dramatically increased at Emberling rank.
  • Still Emberling as of Book 2, Chapter 40: has not yet reached Tomb Spark, despite active coaching from Scorio and Jova and proper training at the Chasm.

Relationships

Scorio

Treats him as a brother without qualification; the first to reach for his coin purse and the first to demand he take care of himself.

Lianshi

Easy, teasing, sibling-like friendship, longer-standing and more comfortable than either of their bonds with Scorio, simply by virtue of shared time at the Academy.

Trivia

  • Archspire reveal: once known as The Grim, the Golden King, Favored of Eragoth, and Wielder of Nezzar. Highest rank attained: Pyre Lord. Died 4 years before this incarnation; reborn 120 times — a relatively "normal" rebirth history compared to Scorio's anomalous 233-year gap.
  • Jokingly floats "Golden King" as an informal nickname he'd like to live up to, given his past title.
  • Breaks his elbow in Book 1, Chapter 58 during unsupervised Gauntlet training — the second member of the group, after Scorio, to visibly pay a physical price for the old Gauntlet's lack of oversight.
  • Gets his nose broken by Manticore's Davelos during a sparring test (Book 2, Chapter 13) and is fully unbothered by it.
  • Knocked unconscious by a full-power Dameon punch during a Manticore team scrimmage in Bastion, then revived by a Manticore elixir (Book 2, Chapter 25).
