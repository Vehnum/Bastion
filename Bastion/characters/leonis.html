Leonis

"I couldn't wish for better comrades with whom to face an infuriatingly obtuse death."

Leonis is the third member of Scorio and Lianshi's group in the Gauntlet. He is the group's protector and morale-booster, quick to volunteer for danger and to crack jokes in the worst possible moments.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Bear-like and broad-shouldered, easily the largest of the group, with long hair usually worn loose or gathered into a bun and a beard he keeps trimmed once he can afford to. At the Academy he dresses with a deliberately relaxed, slightly slapdash flair — open robes, strings of polished wooden beads and silver thread.

Personality

Warm, loud, and instinctively protective, with a booming, battlefield-ready voice and a running stream of self-deprecating humor that rarely lets up even in genuine danger. Underneath the jokes is real loyalty and real worry — he's the one most likely to say out loud that a plan has gone too far, and the one who most openly presses Scorio to slow down.

Biography

The Gauntlet and its aftermath

He dies in the Dark Room, throat torn out defending Scorio and Lianshi from a trio of imp-like creatures, and is reborn normally at the Academy. He is present alongside Lianshi when Scorio is exposed as a Red Lister and physically tries to intervene, without success, and spends the following months at the Academy still trying, without success, to find a way to help his lost friend.

Reunion & renewed brotherhood

Leonis is overjoyed to find Scorio alive at the Graveyard market in Chapter 23, immediately pressing money and gifts on him ("friends don't stand on pride") and arranging his own Academy delivery privileges to smuggle Scorio inside to check his locker. He and Lianshi then join Scorio's weekly training runs into the ruins every Eighthday, treating it as genuine, valuable combat training rather than a favor — and, after discovering the Old Academy's still-functioning private Gauntlet, throws himself into repeated death-training alongside Scorio and Lianshi, despite finding the experience genuinely horrifying ("criminally insane murderers," he calls his own friend group, fondly).

He is the one who most openly worries about Scorio's growing recklessness, extracting a promise of "moderation" from him after a training session leaves all three of them badly shaken. He is present for the locker's Jova Spike revelation and for Imogen's assault on Bastion, fleeing into the Gauntlet alongside the others as Chapter 40 ends.

Abilities

  • Igneous Heart: Ignited; currently a Cinder, trained through official Academy resources.
  • Physical strength: the group's frontline brawler by build and instinct, though not yet a match for a real Emberling in a straight fight.

Relationships

Scorio

Treats him as a brother without qualification; the first to reach for his coin purse and the first to demand he take care of himself.

Lianshi

Easy, teasing, sibling-like friendship, longer-standing and more comfortable than either of their bonds with Scorio, simply by virtue of shared time at the Academy.

Trivia

  • Archspire reveal: once known as The Grim, the Golden King, Favored of Eragoth, and Wielder of Nezzar. Highest rank attained: Pyre Lord. Died 4 years before this incarnation; reborn 120 times — a relatively "normal" rebirth history compared to Scorio's anomalous 233-year gap.
  • Jokingly floats "Golden King" as an informal nickname he'd like to live up to, given his past title.
