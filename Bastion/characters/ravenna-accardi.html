Ravenna Accardi

Jova's public rival, until an aura she couldn't stand against.

Jova Spike's public rival for most of the semester, favored by House Hydra once Jova turned the sponsorship down, and capable of tearing huge chunks of stone from the ground to hurl as projectiles. Faces Jova in what onlookers call the tournament's best match, landing hit after hit and very nearly winning before Jova abruptly reaches Tomb Spark mid-fight; Ravenna's own attacks then begin missing entirely as Jova's new aura overwhelms her nerve, and she is shoved from the ring in terrified retreat rather than beaten outright.

One of the cohort's Top Five alongside Jova, Zala, Kuragin, and Chen She.

Resurfaces in Book 2, Chapter 26 wearing House Kraken robes, still only an Emberling and visibly stalled since her Gauntlet loss to Jova. At a House Kraken party in Bastion she deliberately seduces Scorio using deceptively strong "golden elixir" served on Autocrator Octavia's orders — but breaks character mid-scheme back at her apartment and confesses the whole plan outright: she was meant to extract information about Scorio's dealings with the White Queen and Manticore, and at minimum to get him emotionally invested in a Kraken member. She explains her own Gauntlet loss left her humiliated and unable to sleep for a week, and that Octavia recruited her while she was at her lowest; she swore a Heart Oath to House Kraken and now wants out, but only Octavia can release her. Scorio encourages her to earn her own way free rather than wait to be released, and the two trade real information instead of going through with the seduction — Scorio reveals Praximar's corrupt mana-theft arrangement with House Basilisk; Ravenna passes on Octavia's private suspicion that a Deep Hell liaison, Charnel Duke Plassus, is somehow being manipulated (Book 2, Chapters 27–28). (Note: the source text spells her name "Acardi," one C, throughout this stretch of Book 2, versus the "Accardi" spelling used earlier and in this URL; kept as "Accardi" here for continuity rather than treated as a confirmed respelling.)
