Dola

"You don't get where I am without knowing when to take a bet."

Dola is a senior operative for House Basilisk, based out of a bar called the Double in a rough district of Bastion known as the Narrows.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Older, large, and powerfully built, with near-black skin and ivory hair shorn close except for the crown, from which slender braids fall past her shoulders nearly to her waist. Painted lips and strong, striking features give her a commanding presence even in a room full of hardened criminals.

Personality

Shrewd, pragmatic, and hard to read. She keeps a personal code — she refuses to work with anyone willing to harm a child, and is "not above killing, but only as a last resort" — without ever softening into anything like warmth. Feiyan warns Scorio that Dola is only ever nice to people she's about to spend as expendable.

Biography

Feiyan, a former Basilisk operative herself, introduces Scorio to Dola after he emerges from the ruins with nothing but a stolen glowing sunphire to his name. Dola interviews him carefully, correctly suspecting he might be an undercover Great Soul (his lack of calluses, his poise, his vague answers all give him away to her), but ultimately hires him for a single trial job: infiltrating House Chimera's skycrane yard to swap a valuable shipment for a fake.

The heist goes wrong, and Scorio escapes with the real prize — two vials of rare Sapphire mana — which he chooses to keep rather than deliver to Dola, making her his active enemy. She sends a member of her crew to stake out Scorio's old hideout in Chapter 29, but the thread hasn't been picked back up since; she appears to have faded into the background rather than remaining an ongoing threat.

Word reaches Scorio secondhand in Chapter 52 that Dola has "been asking after" him since Imogen's assault — a small, unresolved thread suggesting she hasn't forgotten the debt, even as the more immediate danger from her turns out to run through Kayla, an informant of hers who separately feeds Chancellor Praximar the leverage he uses to blackmail Scorio in Chapter 53.

Abilities

  • Criminal network: commands a crew of operatives across the Narrows and beyond, capable of surveillance and small-scale heists against the Houses.
  • Reads people quickly: identified Scorio as a probable undercover Great Soul within minutes of meeting him.

Relationships

Scorio

Employer turned enemy, after he kept the Sapphire vials from a job she sent him on.

Feiyan

A former operative of hers; Dola respects her enough to take a meeting with Scorio on Feiyan's word alone.

Trivia

  • Dola's interview technique includes checking a candidate's hands for calluses, breath, and eyes — physical tells for whether someone is lying about their background.
