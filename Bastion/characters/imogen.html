Imogen

"Hope dies. What could be crueler?"

Imogen is an Imperator — one of only 76 in Bastion's history — and, as of Chapter 40, the single most powerful individual to appear in the story. Her sudden arrival at Bastion, seeking a Portal home she believes may finally be reopening, is the climactic event of Book One's Chapters 38–40.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Pale to the point of looking unwell, with dark-ringed eyes, prominent cheekbones, full burgundy-painted lips, and freckles standing out starkly against waxen skin. Her hair and gown blend into the living shadow that constantly surrounds her, making her silhouette hard to separate from the darkness she travels within.

Personality

Grief-shattered and dangerously unstable beneath a veneer of dreamy, almost gentle detachment. She drifts between melancholy and sudden, world-altering resolve, and treats the assembled might of Bastion's defenders with genuine, weary indifference rather than malice — she isn't cruel so much as beyond caring what damage she causes in pursuit of what she wants.

Biography

A grief-shattered mind

Introduced in a dedicated interlude chapter, Imogen is shown wandering a vast, self-constructed pocket-reality: an endless labyrinth built around a beautiful palace she can never quite bring herself to reach, populated by illusory ghosts of "friends and lovers, enemies, and casual kills" whose spirits she has apparently consumed. Parts of the labyrinth are her own trauma given magical form, including an illusion of a crimson-haired man named Yohanis, fatally wounded over the heart, whom she is forced to banish/unmake at painful cost, and a lakeside picnic memory soaked in guilt she refers to, self-accusingly, as connected to "mass murders." She is fixated on an unnamed "he," imagined waiting for her at a ball in the palace she cannot reach.

According to Nova, the White Queen, Imogen's mind was permanently fractured eight years ago by "the price she paid to save the other Imperators" — an incident that also caused the collapse of Bastion's Empyreal Prophecy. She has been missing, presumed in hiding, ever since.

Arrival at Bastion

Imogen senses a stirring of "impossibly old" power from across all of hell and recognizes it, disbelieving, as coming from Bastion — the same faint, unreadable mana current Naomi detected leaking from the broken old Archspire during one of Scorio's private Gauntlet sessions. Hoping it means the long-sealed Dead Portal to Ettera is finally reopening — and that she might reunite with the "he" from her visions — she tears through her own self-made wards to escape her pocket-reality and flies for Bastion, casually draining the ambient Diamond mana from an entire ecosystem along the way to fuel her speed.

Arriving at the old Academy, she finds the Portal as sealed as ever. Her grief curdles into resolve: if it will not open on its own after nearly 900 years, she declares she will force it open herself — or destroy it trying. She begins physically dismantling the old basilica's wall to reach the Dead Portal directly, ignoring Pyre Lord Praximar's panicked attempts to placate her.

The battle for Bastion

Dozens of Bastion's most powerful Great Souls — led by Nova the White Queen and coordinated by the tactician Desiree — converge to delay her, by explicit admission unable to actually defeat her. Their strategy is pure attrition: harass her in rotating waves long enough (an hour or two, at best) for other Imperators to arrive from across hell in response to an emergency beacon. Notably, Nova and Lady Maeve are visibly relieved that Imogen has come alone — implying at least two other figures, named only as Zellair and Joranvyn, are feared to be of comparable or greater danger.

A combined all-out strike from four Great Souls — including one, Amity, who sacrifices his own life-force to power the blast — is the only attack shown to visibly stagger her, briefly burying her under rubble; she is freed and unharmed within moments. Chapter 40 ends mid-battle, with Imogen's incapacitating fog sweeping through the old basilica and Scorio's group narrowly escaping into the Gauntlet to avoid it.

Abilities

  • Rank: Imperator, burning Noumenon — the highest confirmed rank and mana grade in the story.
  • Shroud: a near-impervious personal defensive barrier.
  • Ferula: a signature manifested weapon named Mordecholio, capable of hurling beams of destructive darkness.
  • Reality-shaping: can reshape terrain and architecture at will within her sphere of influence.
  • Incapacitating fog: a mind-numbing black mist that renders anyone it touches passive and unresisting.
  • Realm-summoning: as an Imperator, theoretically able to summon legions of servants from her private pocket-reality (not yet used against Bastion).

Relationships

Yohanis

A crimson-haired man, fatally wounded over the heart, whose illusion she maintains and mourns within her personal labyrinth. His relationship to her — lover, victim, both — is unconfirmed.

The unnamed "he"

A figure she imagines waiting for her at a ball, whom she hopes to reunite with if the Portal to Ettera reopens. Identity unknown.

Nova, the White Queen

Leads Bastion's defense against her; the two converse with a strange, respectful familiarity mid-battle, suggesting some prior history or mutual regard.

Trivia

  • Her arrival is directly tied to the collapse of the Empyreal Prophecy eight years earlier, a piece of backstory previously only referenced in passing by minor characters.
