Davelos

"One down. Five more to go."

Davelos is one of the five founding Dread Blazes of Manticore, met by the fellowship in the Farmlands after Evelyn has already vouched for them. Where Evelyn is warm and immediately welcoming, Davelos is guarded and unconvinced — and says so to their faces before testing every one of them personally.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Handsome, with lustrous black skin and a broad, contagious smile, dressed in practical slate travel robes when not fighting. In combat his human shape gives way entirely to a towering, inhuman golem form: bulked shoulders and back, elongated arms, a spindle-thin waist, swollen thighs and long curved lower legs, all plated in burnished Copper shot through with rougher seams of Coal, crowned by clustered Copper spikes bursting from his shoulders. His features simplify to almost nothing in this form, eyes burning with a bright, brassy light.

Personality

Businesslike and skeptical where Evelyn is effusive. His first reaction to seven new recruits is open concern rather than delight ("How many friends we talking?"), and he makes Evelyn argue her case before he'll agree to anything. Once he commits to a decision, though, he follows through without complaint, and he's willing to give credit where it's earned — telling Scorio plainly that forcing him to use his Shroud "is to your credit," even mid-beatdown.

Biography

A skeptical first test

Davelos meets the fellowship in the Farmlands, already close with Evelyn (the two greet each other with a hug), and immediately questions whether Manticore should be taking on seven new recruits at once. Won over enough by Evelyn's case to give them a chance, he demands a sparring match to judge them for himself. His golem form proves nearly impossible to hurt — Scorio's claws punch clean through his palm without slowing him down — and he shrugs off both Jova's fear aura and Scorio's Tomb Spark compulsion field with no visible effect, for reasons never explained on the page. He ends the fight decisively: crushing Scorio's nose through a shattered Shroud, elbowing Leonis in the back mid-phase-shift, punching Zala in the gut the moment her butterflies land on him, and deflecting the Nightmare Lady's tail-blade with his own half-hemisphere Shroud. Afterward he concedes their forcing him to raise that Shroud at all "is to your credit," but defers Manticore's final decision to Dameon (Book 2, Chapter 12).

Meeting Druanna

On the road through House Kraken's Farmland holdings, Davelos greets Pyre Lady Druanna with careful, practiced courtesy, and afterward joins Evelyn in openly admiring both her power and her looks — enough that Evelyn teases him about it directly (Book 2, Chapter 14).

The Ixithilion attack

During the goldyolk swarm and subsequent Ixithilion ambush near the Golden Circuit, Davelos fights at Evelyn's side, repeatedly misting to let attacks pass through him and reforming to strike, killing at least one Ixithilion by driving his fist through its head. The mist form isn't invulnerability, though: a wounded Ixithilion manages to spear a needle into his back the instant he rematerializes, leaving him visibly hurt until Nova heals him along with the rest of the group (Book 2, Chapters 15–16).

At the council, and introducing Simeon

Davelos sits in on Nova's council session at the Fiery Shoals, listening without much comment. Afterward, reunited with fellow Dread Blaze Simeon, he's happy to needle him in front of the newcomers — casually revealing the nature of Simeon's possession power over Simeon's own mild protest ("Thanks, Davelos." / "Any time, buddy.") — evidence of an easy, long-standing friendship between Manticore's founders (Book 2, Chapter 20).

Abilities

  • Rank: Dread Blaze.
  • Copper-Coal golem form: transforms into a towering, plated, inhuman body immensely stronger and more durable than his human shape, with visible seams of Coal running through the Copper.
  • Mist form: dissolves into bright Copper mist and reforms at will, letting attacks pass harmlessly through him; requires a visible inhale beforehand, a "tell" both Naomi and Juniper notice and flag as exploitable — and one that doesn't stop him from being hurt the instant he re-solidifies.
  • Shroud: manifests as a half-hemisphere barrier, sturdy enough to deflect the Nightmare Lady's tail-blade outright.
  • Unexplained resistance to both Jova's fear aura and Scorio's Tomb Spark compulsion field.

Relationships

Evelyn

A fellow founding Dread Blaze and, evidently, an old friend — the two greet each other warmly and fight seamlessly together throughout the trip to the Fiery Shoals.

Simeon

Easy, teasing camaraderie between the two once reunited at the Fiery Shoals.

Scorio

The most openly skeptical of the fellowship's new members, and the one who tests them hardest — but comes away crediting Scorio for forcing him to raise his Shroud at all.

Dameon

Defers the fellowship's permanent membership to Dameon's judgment rather than deciding himself.

Trivia

  • Class of 869 — roughly the same generation as Evelyn.
  • At one point refers to Dameon as "Damian" rather than "Dameon" — likely the same kind of minor spelling slip seen elsewhere in the source text (compare Evelyn's "Mikoto" vs. Simeon's "Mitoko" for the same former classmate) rather than a second, distinct person.
  • One of the few named Dread Blazes whose fate is actually confirmed after the Book 2, Chapter 40 vault raid: he remains topside throughout and is unaffected by the cliffhanger that swallows Evelyn, Simeon, and two of Druanna's own escorts.
