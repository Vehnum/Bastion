Captain Thorne

"Be sure to pick your future friends wisely."

Captains The Celestial Coffer, the whale ship that carries harvested Gold mana out of the Fiery Shoals. A Dread Blaze for almost two decades by his own account, and openly content to stay one rather than chase further advancement. In Bastion for the Queen's Accord ceremony, he approaches Scorio with a personal recruitment offer from the Iron Tyrant of the Fury Spires himself — Scorio only, not his friends, starting with a year aboard the Coffer and eventual evaluation for the elite Iron Vanguard. Scorio declines on loyalty grounds; Thorne respects the choice, but leaves with a pointed, unexplained warning about Manticore — "be sure to pick your future friends wisely" — that Scorio can't get him to elaborate on (Book 2, Chapter 26).
