Juniper

A name out of Lianshi's own journals, and now one of the seven walking out of Bastion

Juniper is a blonde Academy classmate who moves from background face to founding member of Scorio's fellowship over the course of Book 1 — and who turns out to share an unexplained, centuries-old history with Lianshi.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Blonde; otherwise not described in detail so far.

Personality

Warm and forthright. Volunteers to help a stranger (Scorio) without being asked, and later approaches Lianshi directly to resolve painful unfinished business rather than letting it fester. Decisive once she commits — she joins Scorio's fellowship "without hesitation."

Biography

Book 1

Juniper first volunteers to tutor Scorio in Book 1, Chapter 44 (a job Lianshi redirects to herself instead), then resurfaces as one of the tournament's rising Emberlings, and finally as one of Jova Spike's two closest friends and roommates, alongside Zala.

Her name matches a figure from Lianshi's own centuries-old journals: a fellow acolyte from a past-life death cult who confessed her love for Lianshi and asked to flee the order together. The two women finally speak directly the night before the final Gauntlet run, comparing trial memories; Juniper forgives Lianshi for whatever she may have done to her in less fortunate branches of the past, and the two part on genuinely warm terms.

When Scorio and Jova both decline the Academy's rewards and walk out at the end of Book 1, Juniper asks to join them without hesitation, describing a feeling of her soul "singing" at the choice — becoming one of the seven founding members of the fellowship leaving Bastion.

Book 2

On the road (Book 2)

Juniper leaves the Academy with Jova and Zala and fights through the fellowship's early skirmishes in Bastion and, later, the goldyolk swarm and Ixithilion attack on the Rascor Plains. During Manticore's sparring test she lands some of the only solid hits scored against Dread Blaze Davelos, staggering him with her light-based punches even though he barely reacts (Book 2, Chapter 13).
