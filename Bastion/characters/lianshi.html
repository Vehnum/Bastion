Lianshi

"Whoever runs this place, or designed it, doesn't want us seeing into the next chamber."

Lianshi is one of Scorio's two companions from the Gauntlet, alongside Leonis. She is the group's most analytical member — the first to notice trap patterns, and the one who talks Scorio through the logic of each new chamber.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Tall, pale, and angular, with dark hair worn loose or in a braid and a single distinct streak of blue running through it. Dressed in Academy whites once reincarnated; notably cleaner and better-dressed than Scorio at every reunion, a contrast the story draws attention to.

Personality

Careful, observant, and quietly stubborn. She is the one most likely to reason a trap or a puzzle out loud, and the one most weighed down by guilt when she can't act on her convictions fast enough. Beneath a composed surface she carries real existential dread about her erased past and the sheer scale of her own history (97 lives), and channels both her guilt over Scorio and that dread into an intense, near-obsessive drive to grow stronger — rivaling Scorio's own.

Biography

Book 1

The Gauntlet

Lianshi dies in the Hall of Blades (the sixth chamber), badly cut across the torso, and urges Scorio to keep going without her. She is reborn normally at the Academy alongside Leonis, and both are present when the Archspire reveals Scorio's identity as a Red Lister and he is dragged away.

At the Academy

A dedicated interlude chapter shows Lianshi three weeks later, studying Mana Manipulation under Instructor Hera and still consumed with guilt and worry over Scorio's fate. She and Leonis have petitioned the chancellor and scouted the guarded holding areas at night, without success. She keeps a rare healing item, a Sublime Purification pill, set aside specifically for when — if — he returns.

Her private journals (over 150 volumes, recovered from her Academy locker) reveal that she has lived 97 total lives reaching back centuries, with adventures across many named regions of hell. Recurring figures from her past include Captain Marhab, Previc, Mewui, and the "brooding" Rombelier, along with an unresolved, apparently painful history with someone named Juniper. Notably, she has a recurring habit across lifetimes of tearing out and burning the journal pages describing her First Trial before each new incarnation — a mystery not yet explained.

Reunion & renewed loyalty

Lianshi and Leonis's hope is rewarded in Book 1, Chapter 23: Scorio finds them at the Graveyard market, and the reunion is immediate and emotional. Notably, Lianshi had already turned down a full House Kraken sponsorship offer out of anger at how Scorio was treated — a real cost paid out of loyalty. She gives him a rare, saved Sublime Purification pill to help offset the damage of training on raw Coal mana, and takes an active hand in a scheme to smuggle him into the Academy basilica to check his own locker, including learning to solidify mana into a simple copied key.

The trio then begin meeting every Eighthday to train together in the ruins, and later discover the Old Academy and its still-functioning, private Gauntlet — which Lianshi embraces wholeheartedly as a training method despite the very real pain of repeated in-Gauntlet deaths, matching Scorio's own intensity if not quite his recklessness.

She is present for the locker discovery that reveals Jova Spike's name as the sole contents of Scorio's empty coffer, and is instrumental in reasoning out why Scorio's past self likely left it there. She is also present for Imperator Imogen's assault on Bastion in Book 1, Chapters 38–40, escaping alongside the others into the Gauntlet as the confrontation reaches its climax.

A familiar name resurfaces

When Scorio returns to the Academy, one of several classmates who volunteer to tutor him is a blonde woman named Juniper — the same name that appears, unexplained and apparently painful, throughout Lianshi's centuries of private journals (see Trivia). Lianshi's reaction to the volunteering is not shown directly, but she firmly steers Scorio's tutoring arrangements toward herself instead. Whether this is the same Juniper, and what history the two of them may share, remains unconfirmed.

Otherwise settled into the resumed routine of Academy life, Lianshi needles Leonis relentlessly over his own tournament elimination, quietly excels in History of Bastion under Instructor Hera, and treats the return of both Scorio and Naomi to the suite as the family reunion she's been hoping for since Book 1, Chapter 23.

The pledge

Alongside Leonis in Book 1, Chapter 55, Lianshi puts her own version of the same promise into words — framed less around loyalty-as-sentiment and more as a considered, deliberate choice: she has run the numbers on Scorio's House Chimera situation and concluded the group is worth more to her than any sponsorship the Houses could offer. It's a rare moment of her guilt-driven intensity resolving into calm certainty rather than worry.

Reaching Emberling

In Book 1, Chapter 58, Lianshi ignites past Cinder to Emberling during a private testing session in the Old Academy, ahead of both Leonis and (at the Academy, at least) most of her official cohort. The breakthrough surfaces a power neither she nor the others expected: a combined invulnerability and immovability effect she can project over herself, briefly rendering her un-harmable and unable to be physically displaced at all. The group tests it cautiously — Leonis takes a full swing at her with a practice weapon and simply fails to move her an inch — and it's immediately clear the power reframes her as far more than the group's planner; she may be its most durable combatant outright.

The reveal visibly unsettles her as much as it excites her: Lianshi is plainly aware that outright invulnerability sits close to the kind of overwhelming, reality-bending power that drew Imogen's Empyreal Prophecy cohort toward the Pit in the first place, and says as much, quietly, before the celebration mood is allowed to close the subject.

Juniper, again

Juniper resurfaces twice more this stretch — once in the crowd at Jova Spike's sparring duel, and once in a brief, unexplained exchange of glances with Lianshi outside the mess hall that neither woman comments on afterward. Lianshi still has not addressed the connection directly, even as the pattern of appearances becomes harder to read as coincidence.

Reconciling with Juniper

After Kuragin's trial reveal shakes Scorio, Lianshi finally discloses her own Second Trial to the group: in a past life as an acolyte of a death cult, she chose to run away with a fellow acolyte, Juniper, who had confessed her love for her rather than report the confession to their superiors — the same painful history her journals have alluded to for weeks, and the same Juniper now enrolled at the Academy. The night before the final Gauntlet run, Juniper approaches her directly; the two finally speak, compare trial experiences, and Juniper forgives her for whatever she may have done to her in less fortunate branches of the past. Lianshi is left "excited and awful" in equal measure, and the two part on warm terms for the first time in the story.

The final Gauntlet run

Lianshi joins Scorio, Leonis, and Naomi for the semester's climactic Gauntlet attempt, her invulnerability power proving invaluable through the early Char- and Cinder-tier chambers. In the eleventh chamber — a dark room hiding invisible funnel traps and a web-slinging spider-child fiend — she is caught in webbing and dragged into a concealed pit; her invulnerability protects her from the spider's attacks but does nothing against the funnel's suction, and she slips from Scorio's grasp, ending her run.

Leaving the Academy

Openly delighted when Scorio wins the Gauntlet outright, Lianshi joins the new seven-person fellowship — alongside Scorio, Leonis, Naomi, Jova Spike, Zala, and Juniper herself, who asks to come along the moment she learns what's happening — that leaves the Academy at Book 1's end in search of the truth behind a two-century-old warning.

Book 2

Guide to the Rascor Plains (Book 2)

Lianshi becomes the fellowship's de facto tour guide once they're out on the Rascor Plains, supplying real-time lore on the Ash Belt, the Rain Wall, the floating Gold islands, and the Fiery Shoals — often to her friends' amusement. She reaches Emberling training milestones alongside Naomi and Leonis under Scorio's coaching, and is present for the goldyolk swarm and Ixithilion attack near the Golden Circuit (Book 2, Chapters 9–16).

Abilities

  • Igneous Heart: Ignited; currently an Emberling as of Book 1, Chapter 58, trained through official Academy resources.
  • Invulnerability & immovability: an Emberling-rank power rendering her briefly un-harmable and physically immovable; effective against direct attacks, but no defense against area effects like funnel traps.
  • Mana shaping: can solidify mana into simple, small physical shapes and lock them in place — used to copy an Academy key.
  • Tactical analysis: the group's default planner in combat and trap situations.

Relationships

Scorio

Her guilt over his exile runs deep; she has organized much of the group's effort to help him, from the locker infiltration to weekly training runs.

Leonis

Old, easy, sibling-like friendship, full of teasing, going back to before either remembers.

Trivia

  • Lianshi has begun, across multiple past lives, a personal project to index her own 150+ journals — and has died before finishing it more than once.
  • Her past-life titles (Nun of the Red, Guardian of the Second Death, Bestower of the Final Grace) all carry religious/funerary overtones, unexplained so far.
  • A classmate named Juniper volunteered to tutor Scorio in Book 1, Chapter 44; Lianshi's own journals reference an unresolved, apparently painful history with someone of the same name — confirmed as connected once the two finally speak in Book 1, Chapter 68.
  • Reaches Emberling in Book 1, Chapter 58, before Leonis and ahead of most of her cohort, with a surprise invulnerability/immovability power.
