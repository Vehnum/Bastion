Lianshi

"Whoever runs this place, or designed it, doesn't want us seeing into the next chamber."

Lianshi is one of Scorio's two companions from the Gauntlet, alongside Leonis. She is the group's most analytical member — the first to notice trap patterns, and the one who talks Scorio through the logic of each new chamber.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Tall, pale, and angular, with dark hair worn loose or in a braid and a single distinct streak of blue running through it. Dressed in Academy whites once reincarnated; notably cleaner and better-dressed than Scorio at every reunion, a contrast the story draws attention to.

Personality

Careful, observant, and quietly stubborn. She is the one most likely to reason a trap or a puzzle out loud, and the one most weighed down by guilt when she can't act on her convictions fast enough. Beneath a composed surface she carries real existential dread about her erased past and the sheer scale of her own history (97 lives), and channels both her guilt over Scorio and that dread into an intense, near-obsessive drive to grow stronger — rivaling Scorio's own.

Biography

The Gauntlet

Lianshi dies in the Hall of Blades (the sixth chamber), badly cut across the torso, and urges Scorio to keep going without her. She is reborn normally at the Academy alongside Leonis, and both are present when the Archspire reveals Scorio's identity as a Red Lister and he is dragged away.

At the Academy

A dedicated interlude chapter shows Lianshi three weeks later, studying Mana Manipulation under Instructor Hera and still consumed with guilt and worry over Scorio's fate. She and Leonis have petitioned the chancellor and scouted the guarded holding areas at night, without success. She keeps a rare healing item, a Sublime Purification pill, set aside specifically for when — if — he returns.

Her private journals (over 150 volumes, recovered from her Academy locker) reveal that she has lived 97 total lives reaching back centuries, with adventures across many named regions of hell. Recurring figures from her past include Captain Marhab, Previc, Mewui, and the "brooding" Rombelier, along with an unresolved, apparently painful history with someone named Juniper. Notably, she has a recurring habit across lifetimes of tearing out and burning the journal pages describing her First Trial before each new incarnation — a mystery not yet explained.

Reunion & renewed loyalty

Lianshi and Leonis's hope is rewarded in Chapter 23: Scorio finds them at the Graveyard market, and the reunion is immediate and emotional. Notably, Lianshi had already turned down a full House Kraken sponsorship offer out of anger at how Scorio was treated — a real cost paid out of loyalty. She gives him a rare, saved Sublime Purification pill to help offset the damage of training on raw Coal mana, and takes an active hand in a scheme to smuggle him into the Academy basilica to check his own locker, including learning to solidify mana into a simple copied key.

The trio then begin meeting every Eighthday to train together in the ruins, and later discover the Old Academy and its still-functioning, private Gauntlet — which Lianshi embraces wholeheartedly as a training method despite the very real pain of repeated in-Gauntlet deaths, matching Scorio's own intensity if not quite his recklessness.

She is present for the locker discovery that reveals Jova Spike's name as the sole contents of Scorio's empty coffer, and is instrumental in reasoning out why Scorio's past self likely left it there. She is also present for Imperator Imogen's assault on Bastion in Chapters 38–40, escaping alongside the others into the Gauntlet as the confrontation reaches its climax.

Abilities

  • Igneous Heart: Ignited; currently a Cinder, trained through official Academy resources.
  • Mana shaping: can solidify mana into simple, small physical shapes and lock them in place — used to copy an Academy key.
  • Tactical analysis: the group's default planner in combat and trap situations.

Relationships

Scorio

Her guilt over his exile runs deep; she has organized much of the group's effort to help him, from the locker infiltration to weekly training runs.

Leonis

Old, easy, sibling-like friendship, full of teasing, going back to before either remembers.

Trivia

  • Lianshi has begun, across multiple past lives, a personal project to index her own 150+ journals — and has died before finishing it more than once.
  • Her past-life titles (Nun of the Red, Guardian of the Second Death, Bestower of the Final Grace) all carry religious/funerary overtones, unexplained so far.
