The Warrens Group

Havert, Hestia & Salamander — the escape that went badly.

The small community of long-term exiles Scorio finds sheltering in the Warrens, alongside Nissa. Havert (stocky, wry, cheroot-smoking) and Hestia (freckled, wary, ~1 year in the Warrens) find him half-dead by an underground stream. Salamander ("Sal"), the group's longest-tenured member (~5 years), engineers their escape attempt using a rope-and-pulley system to force open the Brass Door.

The escape goes badly: a monstrous fiend forces the door the rest of the way open, killing Havert and Hestia; Nissa mercy-kills Havert mid-crisis in a moment of panic. Sal flees through the door into fiend territory, abandoning the others. Hestia is caught and dragged off while Scorio tries to free her. Sal's fate beyond that point remains unknown. See Nissa for how the sole survivor's story continues.
