Abentha

Skeptical of the truce. Backed it anyway.

A hard-faced, rail-thin rebel leader who presides over Bastion's uprising alongside Dola. Skeptical of Scorio's ten-day truce proposal but ultimately agrees to it, on the grounds that it costs the movement little and offers a real chance of avoiding a massacre.

Helps negotiate the rebellion's terms for the Queen's Accord alongside Dola, Gethane, Walsham, and Ferric (Book 2, Chapter 22), and gives Scorio a tearful public thanks and a farewell gift, unopened as of Book 2, Chapter 31, when the fellowship departs Bastion for the Chasm.
