Instructor Hera

Severe in the lecture hall, warm in private.

Severe and exacting in the lecture hall, warm and searching in private one-on-one meditation sessions — a contrast Scorio finds genuinely disorienting. Teaches both History of Bastion (five documented historical eras, from the Red Peace to the present) and Mana Studies. It's Hera who diagnoses the severe fracturing in Scorio's Heart, and who explains the mechanics of Heart burn-off, the Fallen, and the Four Trials to him in depth — see The Igneous Heart & Mana for details.

Also fights among the Defenders of Bastion during Imogen's assault, using light-beam and tower constructs.
