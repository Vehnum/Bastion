Naomi

Better known, at first, only as "the Nightmare Lady"

Naomi is a self-taught Emberling who has claimed a territory deep in the ruined third of Bastion, where locals know her only as the Nightmare Lady and warn newcomers to stay away. Unlike every other Great Soul in the story so far, she was not reincarnated — she was born and raised in Bastion.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

In human form: petite, with thick, heavy black hair she often lets hang forward to hide her face, and hazel eyes. Her Nightmare form, manifested at will, is a completely different silhouette: tall, gaunt, and bone-plated, with reverse-jointed legs, backswept horns, glowing sulfurous-green eyes, and a long, bladed, segmented tail capable of near-instant strikes.

Personality

Guarded, dryly sarcastic, and deeply self-protective, shaped by years of being dismissed by the institution that should have trained her. Beneath the mockery is a genuinely careful, principled teacher and a person still quietly working her way back from a period of real despair. She warms slowly and rarely admits it out loud.

Biography

Book 1

Origins

Naomi is the daughter of a mid-to-high-level bureaucrat in House Kraken, raised in the comparatively wealthy Ward 6. Every person in Bastion is born with an Igneous Heart, but almost none ever ignite it; Naomi's father, resentful of the Houses' favoritism toward reincarnated Great Souls, illegally funneled elixirs and high-grade mana to her, and she ignited her Heart unassisted at the age of nine — an extraordinary feat. The Academy admitted her rather than leave an uncontrolled prodigy loose in the city, but never actually trained her: she spent years on menial cleaning duty, quietly written off. After a personal betrayal she still won't discuss, she passed her First Trial, manifested her monstrous Emberling transformation, and left the Academy for the ruins.

Meeting Scorio

Naomi confronts Scorio in his ruin hideout over a pair of stolen Sapphire mana vials, and gives him three nights to either entertain her or die. He instead traps her Nightmare form between five overlapping walls of force (using Radert's chalk, which he'd concealed from her) and makes his real request: not mercy, but training. Moved despite herself, she agrees to one week — which, by Book 1, Chapter 20, has become an open-ended arrangement with no formal end date.

Her training regimen combines brutal daily sparring, long endurance runs, and meditation practice, built around her core philosophy that growth requires ambition fed by unrelenting frustration. She is candid that she is an unqualified teacher — she never received real instruction herself — but commits fully once convinced Scorio won't quit.

She still holds Scorio's two stolen vials of Sapphire mana, refusing to use them herself until she's certain of her own ambitions. She poses under the alias "Mistress Esoka", claiming a connection to House Kraken, when dealing with the alchemist Jelan at the Sincere Refinery — suggesting she maintains some quiet ties to her old life despite leaving it behind.

The Widow's Tower

After Scorio collapses from exhaustion following an obsessive, unsupervised night of solo Gauntlet runs, Naomi brings him to her actual home for the first time: a precarious, once-grand chamber near the top of a leaning tower she calls the Widow's Tower, decorated with a year's worth of scavenged treasures and alchemical equipment. There, she discloses — obliquely, but unmistakably — that she once considered suicide after leaving the Academy, treating the tower's structural precariousness as a passive wager with fate rather than jumping outright, and that returning to it nightly slowly became a way of choosing to keep living instead. It's the most vulnerable disclosure she has made, and Scorio receives it without pushing. She subsequently confronts him directly about his own reckless Gauntlet-grinding, recognizing the same self-destructive impulse in him that she once carried herself, and negotiates a more sustainable training schedule.

Meeting Leonis and Lianshi

Naomi meets Scorio's other two friends for the first time in Book 1, Chapter 38, visibly nervous despite her usual composure. The meeting goes well; she agrees to escort the group partway to the Old Academy on their training runs, and is present when Imogen arrives at Bastion, fighting alongside the trio and helping them flee the Imperator's incapacitating fog as Book 1, Chapter 40 ends.

Readmission to the Academy

After Imperator Sol asks — on Scorio's request — that Naomi be given a second chance, she is effectively unable to refuse an Imperator's favor, though she makes clear to Scorio that she never truly had a choice. Talked into staying by Scorio and Lianshi rather than retreating to her tower, she takes one of the two open bedrooms in the group's suite in the Hall of Golden Reflections — her first real room at the Academy, after years spent in "a single room off the servant's quarters, the size of a supply closet."

Feng's humiliation

Naomi's first sparring bout back at the Academy is against Instructor Feng, the Dread Blaze who years earlier refused to train her and told her, in front of a class, that she "wasn't a true Great Soul." He toys with her, slowing her Nightmare form to a crawl with his cone of dust until she can barely move, then dismisses her without her landing a blow. The defeat reopens an old wound; she breaks down afterward, admitting to Scorio how badly she wanted to hurt Feng and how humiliated she still feels by her own past treatment. Scorio's response — a promise that they'll both grow strong enough to come back and make Feng answer for it — visibly steadies her.

The mess-hall fight & final warning

When Kuragin publicly accuses Scorio of cowardice, Naomi shifts into her Nightmare form and impales his fist on her tail blade before Instructor Helminth breaks up the confrontation. She receives no lighter a punishment than Scorio despite not throwing the first blow — a fact she predicts bitterly beforehand, telling him plainly that the Academy wants them both gone and will use any excuse. Chancellor Praximar gives them both a final warning: one more infraction, of any kind, means expulsion.

Naomi declines to enter the class tournament even after Scorio explains it's his best shot at healing his fractured Heart, unwilling to accept House patronage or "perform like a trained animal" for the Houses' amusement — but she coaches him closely on strategy before his bouts and is visibly the first to identify the cost of his tournament win: a fresh, permanent burn-off scar on his Heart from consuming a sliver of it directly.

The rift at the Flame

Talked into joining Scorio's celebration at Feiyan and Helena's venue, Naomi spends the night uneasy and out of place. When she tries to warn a drunk, euphoric Scorio not to trust House Chimera's motives so readily, he snaps at her, accusing her of still hiding from life in her tower. She disappears into the crowd and doesn't return to the suite. When Scorio later finds her and tries to apologize, she rebuffs him coldly, telling him plainly that she doesn't think it's a good idea for him to keep visiting her.

Interlude — alone

A dedicated interlude chapter follows Naomi in the aftermath, alone and in real distress: unable to outrun her own thoughts through fighting or flight, and briefly giving in to her Nightmare form's darker impulses before catching herself and stopping — a self-destructive coping habit she'd sworn off and is plainly ashamed of. Amid that despair, she reaches a quiet decision: rather than return to the Academy, or sink back into her old isolation, she resolves to secretly watch over Scorio from a distance whenever he returns to the ruins, without ever announcing herself. Scorio does not know this as of Book 1, Chapter 60.

Watching from the shadows

True to her secret resolution, Naomi spends the following weeks shadowing Scorio's increasingly reckless second Black Star farm without revealing herself, intervening only when an Alben Worm surfaces to devour it. Nearly swallowed whole before Scorio abandons the farm to save her, she is furious and ashamed afterward, and in atonement (and grudging admiration) gives him her two saved Sapphire mana vials to salvage his plan to reach Emberling — effectively re-entering his life on her own terms.

After his Emberling breakthrough, Scorio wins a rematch duel against her and calls in the wager: she agrees to return to the Academy and round out his Gauntlet team, and he in turn strikes a bargain with Chancellor Praximar, swearing a Heart Oath that ties both their fates to the outcome of the run.

The final Gauntlet run

Naomi rejoins Scorio, Leonis, and Lianshi for the semester's climactic Gauntlet attempt, fighting throughout as the Nightmare Lady and taking an early, serious hip wound from a boar fiend that slows her the rest of the way. In the tenth chamber — a hall of animated golden statuettes — she is struck through the back while lecturing the others on strategy, ending her run, but not before telling Scorio she believes in him.

Leaving the Academy

Present for Scorio's public refusal of Praximar's terms and House sponsorship, Naomi tells him plainly that she was wrong to doubt him. She joins the seven-person fellowship — alongside Leonis, Lianshi, Jova Spike, Juniper, and Zala — that leaves the Academy at the end of Book 1 to seek out the truth behind a warning left in Jova's journal by Scorio's past self.

Book 2

An unexplained block against Evelyn (Book 2)

During Manticore Dread Blaze Evelyn's sparring test of the whole fellowship, Naomi finds herself physically unable to land a killing blow on Evelyn even when a clean opening presents itself — describing it afterward as a mental block rather than any physical restraint (Book 2, Chapter 8). She also bristles when Scorio suggests her own path to Tomb Spark may be blocked by unresolved anger at how the Academy and Bastion treated her, and walks away from the conversation rather than engage with it (Book 2, Chapter 12) — still an Emberling as of Chapter 20.

Crossing the Rain Wall

Aboard Manticore's whale ship, The Sloop, Naomi is the last of the fellowship to Ignite against the thin, oxygen-poor air of the Rain Wall crossing, gritting through it on stubbornness alone until she nearly loses consciousness; Scorio catches her before she slides off the deck (Book 2, Chapter 32).

The Coal purge

Diagnosed at the Chasm with the same untempered-body problem as Scorio — both were raised on nothing but raw Coal mana — Naomi volunteers to join him in Manticore's unsparing purge regimen: smashing and hauling boulders by hand for five straight months, Hearts forbidden from Igniting. She takes the physical toll harder than Scorio by her own admission (less raw strength, more grit), attacks the work "as if the rocks were her personal enemies," and grows visibly leaner and harder over the stretch, largely cut off from the rest of the fellowship's day-to-day company in the process. As of Book 2, Chapter 39 she remains an Emberling; the regimen addressed her body's tempering, not her rank (Book 2, Chapters 34–38).

Abilities

  • Nightmare form: a voluntary Emberling transformation — superhuman speed, strength, and a bladed tail capable of near-instant, precise strikes. Instructor Feng demonstrated a hard counter to it — his slowing dust cone — suggesting the tail alone is not enough against a sufficiently skilled Dread Blaze.
  • Igneous Heart: unusually large for her rank as a result of artificially accelerated childhood ignition; still working to condense it back down toward higher reactivity, in pursuit of Tomb Spark and eventually Flame Vault.
  • Alchemy & lore: self-taught via years of eavesdropping on Academy lectures through hidden passages; more knowledgeable about Bastion's systems than she lets on at first, confirmed again in Academy history class where she quietly out-answers even Instructor Hera's harder questions.
  • Untempered body (diagnosed Book 2, Chapter 34): like Scorio, raised in the ruins on nothing but raw Coal mana; completes the same five-month, Ignition-forbidden manual-labor purge he does to address it.

Relationships

Scorio

What began as a hostage negotiation, fractured after a drunken argument at the Flame, and repaired itself through her secret vigil, her sacrificed Sapphire vials, and finally fighting at his side through the Gauntlet's final run. By Book 1's end she is once more one of his closest companions, and the first to publicly admit she was wrong about him.

Leonis & Lianshi

Full suitemates through Book 1, Chapter 54, though Naomi is largely absent from the group from that point on. Leonis's teasing needles her constantly, but the group had closed ranks around her without hesitation before the rift, including during her breakdown after the Feng bout and the mess-hall fight with Kuragin.

Instructor Feng

A years-old grievance made freshly painful: Feng once refused to train her and told her publicly she wasn't a true Great Soul. Defeating him is now an explicit, shared goal between her and Scorio.

Trivia

  • Naomi has lived in "the Widow's Tower" for just over a year as of Book 1, Chapter 34.
  • She was never officially issued a rank title by the Academy and refers to herself only by her Emberling rank and her adopted nickname.
  • Her old Academy room, prior to leaving, was "a single room off the servant's quarters, the size of a supply closet" — a detail she volunteers, unprompted, on first seeing the group's shared pool.
  • Book 1, Chapter 60's interlude is the first chapter told from Naomi's point of view since her introduction, and the first time the wiki has direct access to her private thoughts.
