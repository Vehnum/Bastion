Naomi

Better known, at first, only as "the Nightmare Lady"

Naomi is a self-taught Emberling who has claimed a territory deep in the ruined third of Bastion, where locals know her only as the Nightmare Lady and warn newcomers to stay away. Unlike every other Great Soul in the story so far, she was not reincarnated — she was born and raised in Bastion.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

In human form: petite, with thick, heavy black hair she often lets hang forward to hide her face, and hazel eyes. Her Nightmare form, manifested at will, is a completely different silhouette: tall, gaunt, and bone-plated, with reverse-jointed legs, backswept horns, glowing sulfurous-green eyes, and a long, bladed, segmented tail capable of near-instant strikes.

Personality

Guarded, dryly sarcastic, and deeply self-protective, shaped by years of being dismissed by the institution that should have trained her. Beneath the mockery is a genuinely careful, principled teacher and a person still quietly working her way back from a period of real despair. She warms slowly and rarely admits it out loud.

Biography

Origins

Naomi is the daughter of a mid-to-high-level bureaucrat in House Kraken, raised in the comparatively wealthy Ward 6. Every person in Bastion is born with an Igneous Heart, but almost none ever ignite it; Naomi's father, resentful of the Houses' favoritism toward reincarnated Great Souls, illegally funneled elixirs and high-grade mana to her, and she ignited her Heart unassisted at the age of nine — an extraordinary feat. The Academy admitted her rather than leave an uncontrolled prodigy loose in the city, but never actually trained her: she spent years on menial cleaning duty, quietly written off. After a personal betrayal she still won't discuss, she passed her First Trial, manifested her monstrous Emberling transformation, and left the Academy for the ruins.

Meeting Scorio

Naomi confronts Scorio in his ruin hideout over a pair of stolen Sapphire mana vials, and gives him three nights to either entertain her or die. He instead traps her Nightmare form between five overlapping walls of force (using Radert's chalk, which he'd concealed from her) and makes his real request: not mercy, but training. Moved despite herself, she agrees to one week — which, by Chapter 20, has become an open-ended arrangement with no formal end date.

Her training regimen combines brutal daily sparring, long endurance runs, and meditation practice, built around her core philosophy that growth requires ambition fed by unrelenting frustration. She is candid that she is an unqualified teacher — she never received real instruction herself — but commits fully once convinced Scorio won't quit.

She still holds Scorio's two stolen vials of Sapphire mana, refusing to use them herself until she's certain of her own ambitions. She poses under the alias "Mistress Esoka", claiming a connection to House Kraken, when dealing with the alchemist Jelan at the Sincere Refinery — suggesting she maintains some quiet ties to her old life despite leaving it behind.

The Widow's Tower

After Scorio collapses from exhaustion following an obsessive, unsupervised night of solo Gauntlet runs, Naomi brings him to her actual home for the first time: a precarious, once-grand chamber near the top of a leaning tower she calls the Widow's Tower, decorated with a year's worth of scavenged treasures and alchemical equipment. There, she discloses — obliquely, but unmistakably — that she once considered suicide after leaving the Academy, treating the tower's structural precariousness as a passive wager with fate rather than jumping outright, and that returning to it nightly slowly became a way of choosing to keep living instead. It's the most vulnerable disclosure she has made, and Scorio receives it without pushing. She subsequently confronts him directly about his own reckless Gauntlet-grinding, recognizing the same self-destructive impulse in him that she once carried herself, and negotiates a more sustainable training schedule.

Meeting Leonis and Lianshi

Naomi meets Scorio's other two friends for the first time in Chapter 38, visibly nervous despite her usual composure. The meeting goes well; she agrees to escort the group partway to the Old Academy on their training runs, and is present when Imogen arrives at Bastion, fighting alongside the trio and helping them flee the Imperator's incapacitating fog as Chapter 40 ends.

Abilities

  • Nightmare form: a voluntary Emberling transformation — superhuman speed, strength, and a bladed tail capable of near-instant, precise strikes.
  • Igneous Heart: unusually large for her rank as a result of artificially accelerated childhood ignition; currently working to condense it back down toward higher reactivity, in pursuit of Tomb Spark and eventually Flame Vault.
  • Alchemy & lore: self-taught via years of eavesdropping on Academy lectures through hidden passages; more knowledgeable about Bastion's systems than she lets on at first.

Relationships

Scorio

What began as a hostage negotiation has become the most important relationship in both their lives — a mentorship that has clearly grown into mutual, if unspoken, deep affection.

Leonis & Lianshi

New and still slightly awkward as of Chapter 38, but off to a good start; she's agreed to help protect all three of them on their training runs.

Trivia

  • Naomi has lived in "the Widow's Tower" for just over a year as of Chapter 34.
  • She was never officially issued a rank title by the Academy and refers to herself only by her Emberling rank and her adopted nickname.
