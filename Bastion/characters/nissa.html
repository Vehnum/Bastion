Nissa

"We're the same, you and I. Red Listers. Survivors."

Nissa, once known as the Flayer of Men, is a fellow Red Lister Scorio first met sheltering in the Warrens in Book 1. Presumed lost after abandoning him during a botched escape attempt, she resurfaces in Book 2 as a rebellion agitator secretly working for House Basilisk.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Watery blue eyes, freckled, close-cropped crimson hair that regrows visibly as she heals. In Book 2 she spends much of her page time badly burned and scabbed, methodically peeling her own healing wounds rather than leaving them be.

Personality

Openly pragmatic and self-interested, and unapologetic about it. She frames her own survival instincts as identical to Scorio's own, refusing any pretense of altruism even while doing genuinely useful, even risky, things for the people around her. Guarded about her own affiliations to the point of deflecting direct questions outright, but quick to laugh, including at her own expense.

Biography

The Warrens (Book 1)

Nissa is part of the small community of long-term Red List exiles Scorio finds sheltering in the Warrens, alongside Havert, Hestia, and Salamander ("Sal"). When Sal's escape attempt goes wrong, she flees into the depths rather than risk dying and being reborn back into the same imprisonment — abandoning Scorio and the others in the process. See the Warrens Group for the full account of that escape.

Resurfacing in the rebellion

Nissa reappears in Book 2, Chapter 3, having Ignited her Heart at some point in the intervening months — she deflects every question about how or when. She survives Flame Vault Xiang Xi's mass flame attack on a crowd of protestors, charred but conscious, and offers Scorio and his fellowship shelter in a network of rebel safe houses beneath Bastion in exchange for an old debt, and the hope they might help the rebellion's cause.

She explains her change of heart bluntly: she is no longer fragile, can absorb serious punishment, and sees an opportunity for both vengeance and genuine purpose in a city full of people angry at the same Houses that imprisoned her. She resists Scorio's attempt to reconcile this with her earlier abandonment of him, framing both choices as the same underlying instinct: survive first, principles second (Book 2, Chapter 5).

Working for House Basilisk

Nissa eventually admits she is embedded in the rebellion at the direction of an unnamed mentor answering to Eira, Autocrator of House Basilisk — part of a deliberate Basilisk strategy to destabilize House Hydra and House Kraken's dominance in the wake of Basilisk's own losses during the Empyreal Prophecy. She states plainly that she doesn't care about Basilisk's cause and is using their resources, training, and protection purely for her own ends (Book 2, Chapter 7).

Vouching for Scorio

Nissa personally vouches for Scorio before Bastion's rebel leadership, including Dola and Abentha, helping secure the ten-day truce he negotiates in exchange for an audience with the White Queen (Book 2, Chapter 6).

Abilities

  • Damage-fueled retaliation: taking damage — including self-inflicted wounds, which she deliberately uses to keep her power charged — builds toward an attack that mixes whatever caused the damage (fire, stab wounds, falls). She reports growing more resistant to repeated methods of self-harm over time.
  • Rapid regeneration: heals from even severe burns in what would normally take weeks in a matter of hours.
  • Pain resistance: describes the sensation of serious injury as "floaty and unreal" rather than painful, which she credits with making her fearless in a fight.

Relationships

Scorio

A Warrens-era bond complicated by her having abandoned him once already. Scorio doesn't fully trust her in Book 2, but accepts her help regardless; she in turn seems genuinely glad to see him again.

Eira, Autocrator of House Basilisk

Nissa's ultimate patron, via an unnamed intermediary mentor; the source of her training, elixirs, and support since leaving the Warrens.

Dola

Both women lead factions within the same Bastion uprising and both, separately, answer to House Basilisk — though nothing on the page suggests either is aware of the overlap.

Trivia

  • Survives Flame Vault Xiang Xi's point-blank mass immolation attack outright — the clearest on-page demonstration yet of her power's resilience.
  • Deliberately stabs herself to keep her retaliation power charged, and warns Scorio and his friends not to watch her do it, since it upsets people.
  • Deflects every question about how she Ignited her Heart or who trained her, promising only that she "might" explain if things "play out" a certain way.
