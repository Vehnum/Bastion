Zala

Doesn't need to understand the choice to be part of it

Zala is Jova Spike's best friend, a fellow top-five Emberling, and — as of Book 1's end — one of the seven founding members of the fellowship leaving Bastion.

Personality

Loyal above all else. Startled but ultimately unsurprised when Jova makes a life-altering decision on the spot; admits openly that she doesn't fully understand what's driving it, but chooses to follow anyway.

Biography

Zala is Jova Spike's best friend and fellow top-five Emberling, first into the top hundred on the cohort's original Gauntlet run. She's present throughout the semester mostly at Jova's side, alongside their shared roommate Juniper.

When Jova declines the Academy's rewards and leaves with Scorio's group at the end of Book 1, Zala is startled but ultimately unsurprised, and chooses to come along herself — admitting she doesn't fully understand what's driving the decision, but wants to be part of it regardless. Becomes one of the seven founding members of the fellowship leaving Bastion.
