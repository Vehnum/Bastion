Scorio

"The odds of that being a coincidence are small, don't you think?"

Scorio is the protagonist of the book. He wakes with total amnesia inside the Gauntlet, alongside Lianshi and Leonis, and quickly proves resourceful and quietly decisive under pressure — the kind of person who volunteers to go through the next door first. His past life, once revealed, turns out to be nothing like the man he is now.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Described only sparingly, but consistent with a life of manual hardship by the time he reaches Bastion (Book 1, Chapters 11–30): lean and increasingly hard-muscled from training and near-starvation, with hair worn long and a rough beard by the time he reaches Bastion proper (later trimmed once he can afford a barber-equivalent). He wears whatever he can scavenge or trade for — Academy whites at the very start, then a series of increasingly worn and re-patched laborer's robes throughout his exile.

Personality

Quick-thinking and decisive under pressure, with a dry, self-deprecating sense of humor he uses to cope with horror rather than deny it. Fiercely loyal to the small circle of people who've earned his trust, and just as fiercely resentful of the system that discarded him without explanation. His defining flaw, called out explicitly by both Naomi and his friends, is an obsessive, self-destructive relationship with growth and power — a willingness to grind himself down, alone if necessary, in pursuit of strength, that more than once tips into genuine danger to his body and mind.

Biography

Book 1

The Gauntlet and the Red List

Scorio is among the 26 Great Souls to reach the Chamber of Balance, the Gauntlet's fifth trial, in that year's run — ranking 18th among them by the time he survived before falling to his death. But when the Archspire reveals his former identity — a litany of villain titles including The Scourer, the Abhorred, and Quencher of Hope and Unmaker of Joy — he is declared Proscribed and placed on the Red List. Chancellor Pyre Lord Praximar personally drains a portion of his Igneous Heart's power as "payment" for the inconvenience, then has him thrown through the Final Door into the Warrens to die.

Two details about Scorio's Archspire reading remain unexplained and clearly bother him: he last died 233 years ago (versus five or six years for every other Great Soul revealed that day), and he has only been reborn six times in Bastion's roughly thousand-year history. Naomi later mentions that unusually powerful souls can resist the Archspire's summons if they choose to — a detail Scorio has not yet been able to test against his own case.

Escape and exile

Scorio survives the Warrens, loses several fellow exiles (Havert and Hestia) in a failed escape attempt through the Brass Door, and stumbles into a hidden cavern where he finds the corpse of Radert, an explorer who once entered the Warrens voluntarily. Radert's cache — a collapsible bridge, an anchoring steel rod, and a stick of wall-conjuring chalk — becomes Scorio's core toolkit for the rest of Book 1, Chapters 1–20. Using them, he claws his way out of the underground and into the ruined third of Bastion itself.

Life in the ruins

Homeless and unrecognized, Scorio trades a stolen glowing "sunphire" for help from two street musicians, Feiyan and Helena, who introduce him to Dola of House Basilisk. A heist at House Chimera's skycrane yard nets him two vials of rare Sapphire mana — which he chooses to keep rather than hand over, making an enemy of Dola in the process, though this rivalry has since faded into the background.

Hiding deep in the ruins, Scorio is confronted by the Nightmare Lady, who takes his vials and gives him three nights to live unless he can best her. He traps her instead of fighting her, and uses the opening to ask for something other than mercy: to be trained. What begins as one grudging week becomes an open-ended mentorship, and eventually a real friendship.

Igniting his Heart

After weeks of training and a deliberately extreme gambit — oversaturating his Heart on raw Coal mana, then drinking an entire poisonous, unfiltered Black Star tincture on top of it — Scorio successfully ignites his Igneous Heart mid-fight during a sparring session with Naomi, becoming a Cinder. His Heart proves unusually deep, giving him strong long-term potential at the cost of a slower, harder road to each subsequent rank.

Reunion with Leonis and Lianshi

Scorio tracks his old companions to the Graveyard, a market built into an old cemetery district beside the current Academy where students shop every Eighthday. Their reunion is emotional and immediate; the three become a standing team, meeting weekly to train together in the ruins, with Leonis and Lianshi supplying money, gear, and gifts Scorio can't get on his own.

The empty locker & Jova Spike

With his friends' help — and a brief, nearly disastrous run-in with Instructor Helminth ("the Hell Whip"), who nearly identifies him — Scorio finally gains access to his own Academy locker. It is completely empty except for one thing: the name Jova Spike carved deep into the back wall, apparently a deliberate message left by his own past self. Who Jova Spike was to him, and what he entrusted her with, is unresolved and has become Scorio's central personal mystery.

The private Gauntlet

Searching the ruins for better mana, Scorio, Leonis, and Lianshi discover the Old Academy — Bastion's original, long-abandoned campus — and with it a still-functioning, completely unmonitored version of the Gauntlet. This gives the group effectively unlimited access to death-based training, and Scorio in particular leans on it heavily, to the point of alarming his friends and Naomi with his willingness to grind it alone and in obvious pain. Naomi eventually intervenes, brings him to her own hidden home to recover, and negotiates a more sustainable training regimen.

In parallel, Scorio establishes a hidden Black Star flower farm on an isolated, hard-to-reach rock island over a lava chasm, successfully harvesting enough condensed mana to brew a homemade "syrup" roughly equivalent to weak Bronze-grade mana — a genuinely notable achievement for someone without Academy resources.

Imogen's assault on Bastion

While exploring the Old Academy, Scorio's group is caught in the arrival of Imogen, an Imperator drawn to Bastion by residual power leaking from the old, broken Archspire. Evacuated to the current Academy amid the chaos, Scorio is questioned and partly exposed by Instructor Helminth before the group slips away to help however they can. Their contribution — deliberately disturbing the old Archspire to try to redirect Imogen's attention during the battle — is a real, if desperate, act of agency amid a fight far beyond their power to influence. Book 1, Chapter 40 ends with the group narrowly escaping Imogen's incapacitating fog by fleeing into the Gauntlet.

Escaping into the Gauntlet & talking down an Imperator

Alone among his friends, Scorio escapes Imogen's mind-control fog by triggering a death inside the old, private Gauntlet, timing it so he can recover quickly and return to his bier before she notices. Waking amid the captured Great Souls, he gambles everything on conversation: claiming kinship with Imogen's cause as a fellow Red Lister, he draws out that she recognizes him from a past life as a "companion" who once helped her destroy a rival and later betrayed the "Ul-Master" alongside her — and talks his way into being kept as a conscious witness to her "final act" rather than absorbed into her fog.

When Imogen's estranged husband, the Imperator Sol, arrives to stop her, Scorio is dragged along as her unwilling companion through a running battle across a mutating Bastion. At the fight's climax, with Sol's power almost spent, Scorio drinks a concentrated dose of his own Black Star syrup — roughly a full Silver-grade hit — and lands a single desperate punch on Imogen's Ferula. It does nothing physically, but the shock of it breaks her total focus on Sol for one instant, which is enough for Sol to overwhelm her and force her to yield. Imogen flees through a seam of darkness rather than be destroyed.

Sol's reward & readmission to the Academy

Sol heals the fresh damage Scorio's own gambit inflicted on his Heart, restoring it to its pre-Imogen state, and personally intervenes with Chancellor Praximar to have both Scorio and Naomi readmitted to the Academy with full standing — a favor Praximar cannot refuse, however much he resents it. The Charnel Duchess Nova, "the White Queen," personally escorts Scorio back to Praximar to make sure the order is honored.

Assigned to help restore order in the wounded Ward 4 alongside two other Cinders, Exero and Famissa, Scorio talks a furious, grieving crowd back from the edge of violence against the Great Souls with a blunt appeal to help the wounded first and argue politics later — his first real public act as a reinstated member of the Academy. That evening, he and Naomi are welcomed into Leonis and Lianshi's four-bedroom suite in the Hall of Golden Reflections, and Scorio personally talks Naomi into staying rather than retreating to the ruins.

Life at the Academy

Scorio throws himself into the Academy's full curriculum: morning runs on the Amber Circuit (where he briefly matches pace with rising stars Jova Spike and Ravenna Accardi before burning out), History of Bastion under Instructor Hera, Sparring and Forms under Instructor Feng, and team tactics under Instructor Rex. He loses his first sparring match against Feng but earns rare praise for igniting on Iron mana for the first time — a grade normally associated with Emberling rank, not Cinder.

One-on-one meditation with Instructor Hera delivers the worst news of his young career: months of training on raw Coal and unrefined Black Star tincture have left his Igneous Heart significantly fractured, venting roughly a third of any mana he draws in, with the damage worsening the harder he pushes himself. Natural healing would take a year or two; the fastest realistic fix is House sponsorship, which requires drawing favorable attention in the ongoing class tournament.

The mess-hall confrontation with Kuragin

Rumors spread that Scorio only sided with Sol at the last second, having first tried to curry favor with Imogen. Confronted publicly over it by Kuragin, one of the cohort's top five Emberlings, Scorio headbutts him rather than explain himself, and Naomi's Nightmare form nearly escalates the fight further before Instructor Helminth breaks it up. Kuragin receives a light punishment; Scorio and Naomi, as outsiders without the benefit of the doubt, are given a final warning directly from Chancellor Praximar: one more infraction of any kind and they will be expelled for good, Imperator's favor or not.

The tournament & House Chimera

With Naomi refusing to compete (she wants no part of House patronage), Scorio petitions Instructor Helminth directly to be slotted into the tournament's loser's bracket, five days before the next round. He wins a preliminary bout against the Cinder Etheren with a single Iron-fueled punch, then survives a brutal round-of-32 fight by, for the first time, deliberately burning a sliver of his actual Heart rather than just its stored mana — winning through sheer battering repetition once his Iron reserve ran out. The victory leaves his Heart with its first permanent burn-off scarring, a fact Naomi immediately identifies and is openly alarmed by.

The win draws the attention of House Chimera's recruiters, who interview him at length, test his Heart's capacity with an Attenuation Spider crystal ("high Iron, verging on Bronze; rank 35 — impressive for a Cinder"), and formally offer him sponsorship: healing treasures and pills for his fractures, mandatory meditation sessions to rein in his self-destructive ambition, and a House badge once the contracts are signed. Scorio accepts. Book 1 ends with him newly affiliated, still awaiting his official badge and paperwork.

The wager with Jova Spike

With his sponsorship confirmed, Scorio finally approaches Jova Spike directly to ask her to search her own journals for any record of him. She refuses on principle, calling him an unreformable Red Lister; stung, Scorio goads her into a wager instead. If he outperforms her at the semester's final Gauntlet run, she helps him; if he doesn't, he swears as a Great Soul to withdraw from the Academy for good. She accepts immediately, treating the oath as binding under pain of death.

A night at the Flame & a rupture with Naomi

To celebrate his sponsorship, Scorio's group sneaks out of the Academy through a servants' cistern passage to visit Feiyan and Helena's venue, the Flame. The night sours: a local youth publicly blames the assembled Great Souls for the city's suffering, and Feiyan warns that anti-Great Soul sentiment is boiling toward open unrest. Drunk and euphoric, Scorio lashes out at Naomi when she tries to warn him not to trust House Chimera too readily, telling her to stop "pretending you're still in your tower." She disappears into the crowd and doesn't return to the suite that night.

Praximar's blackmail

Summoned the next morning, Scorio expects punishment for the unauthorized outing. Instead, Praximar reveals he knows of Scorio's one criminal job with Dola's crew — the theft of Sapphire mana from House Chimera's own skycrane yard, the same House that has just sponsored him — and offers a choice: swear a binding oath to deliberately lose his next tournament match, never pass the Gauntlet's sixth chamber, and leave the Academy at semester's end, or have his crime exposed to House Chimera, ending his sponsorship and reputation outright. Praximar all but confirms he learned of the crime weeks earlier via Dola's associate Kayla, and had simply been waiting for the moment of maximum leverage.

Scorio and his friends conclude that open confrontation is hopeless — no Autocrator has ever been brought down through anything but internal politics — and that accepting Praximar's terms would only put him further under the man's control. Searching for Naomi in the ruins for comfort or counsel, Scorio finds her tower empty, then, on his way back, finds her in person; she coldly ends the conversation, telling him she was right not to trust him and that she no longer thinks it wise for him to visit her at all.

Renouncing House Chimera

Rather than let Praximar wield the crime as an ongoing weapon, Scorio preemptively confesses it to Recruiter Gelegos, withdrawing his own candidacy. Gelegos reveals the House had suspected all along and deliberately avoided confirming it so they could recruit him regardless, admires his honesty, but confirms the sponsorship is void now that it's been stated outright. Praximar's leverage evaporates with it — but so does Scorio's entire healing pipeline.

Reopening the old Gauntlet

Alone and dropping the tournament from his priorities, Scorio returns to hunting fiends for Heartstones to sell to Jelan, funding his first self-purchased Sublime Purification pill. He also returns to the sealed Old Academy, walled up by Praximar's order, and is unexpectedly aided by Nox, the Old Academy's guardian toad-fiend, who is grateful for a warning Scorio gave it during Imogen's rampage and shows him a hidden tunnel entrance beneath the ruins that bypasses the seal entirely. Scorio, Leonis, and Lianshi resume training in the private Gauntlet through this route, making their deepest joint progress yet — reaching the fourth chamber's vertical climb — the same Eighthday Lianshi reaches her own First Trial and becomes an Emberling.

Losing the tournament

Scorio publicly requests to spar Jova Spike in place of an assigned opponent during a class exercise, and lands two of three scoring points against her before losing — a genuine surprise to the class, and to Jova, whose reaction ("I won't believe your lies!") strikes him as oddly personal. In his actual next tournament bout, however, he's matched against Chloe, an Emberling whose power lets her teleport into optimal striking position each time she lands a hit; once triggered, her flurry proves unstoppable, and Scorio is eliminated from the tournament in Book 1, Chapter 60. Chastened but undeterred, he resolves that the Academy's systems will never let him catch up on their own terms, and turns his attention to library research on an unspecified "terrible idea."

Nox, the library, and a desperate plan

Scorio's "terrible idea" turns out to be a bargain with Nox, the Old Academy's Imperial Ghost Toad guardian: in exchange for a treatise on toad "Quantics" strategy dug out of the Academy library's restricted stacks, Nox agrees to teach him the outline of the Delightful Secret Marinating technique — the method Imperial Ghost Toads use to force their way past the natural limits of their Hearts by immersing themselves in a mana-conductive "imperial gel" and letting engorged mana condense endlessly around them. Scorio's plan: grow a second, massive Black Star farm (roughly four hundred plants, paid to Nox as guard fee once harvested), brew it into two batches of near-Ruby-strength tincture, and use Nox's gel to force his fractured Heart through ignition to Emberling in one deliberate, near-suicidal push. Both Lianshi and Leonis warn him off it in the strongest terms; Lianshi refuses to help plant the second farm, though she does not abandon him.

The Alben Worm and a ruined harvest

Days before the first batch matures, an Alben Worm — the same class of fiend that killed several of Scorio's fellow exiles in the Warrens years earlier — surfaces to devour the farm. Naomi, who has been secretly watching over the group the whole time, intervenes alone and is nearly swallowed whole; Scorio abandons the farm to save her rather than let the Nightmare Lady fight the worm by herself, and between the four of them (Naomi, Leonis, Lianshi, and Scorio) they drive it off, but the farm and its Coal mana reserve are destroyed in the process. Naomi, furious and ashamed at how close the gambit came to killing them all, unexpectedly gives Scorio her two saved Sapphire mana vials to replace the lost harvest, salvaging the plan and rejoining the group in the process.

Becoming an Emberling

Immersed in Nox's imperial gel with the Sapphire vials and his remaining Coal tincture poured in around him, Scorio uses the gel's stabilizing properties to draw in mana far past what his fractured Heart could otherwise survive, igniting and passing his First Trial to reach Emberling. His trial revisits his original life: a vision of his older brother, Eberro, about to be publicly hanged for treason against a king, with Scorio choosing to save him rather than let his martyrdom fuel a rebellion — a choice that manifests his Emberling power, a scaled, taloned combat form whose claws burn white-hot. He emerges from the gel and immediately wins a rematch duel against Naomi, convincing her (via a wagered bet) to rejoin the Academy and their Gauntlet team.

Blackmailing Praximar into readmitting Naomi

To secure Naomi's official return despite her long truancy, Scorio confronts Chancellor Praximar directly and offers a binding Heart Oath: if his team fails to outperform Jova Spike in the coming Gauntlet run, both he and Naomi will accept whatever terms Praximar chooses for their expulsion. Praximar, certain the bet is unwinnable, agrees.

Kuragin's trial and the brother he may have betrayed

Obsessed with learning more about his past after his Emberling trial, Scorio provokes Kuragin into an unsanctioned duel the night before the Gauntlet run, on the condition that regardless of the outcome Scorio refuses all healing before the run begins. He wins by ambushing Kuragin with his newly hidden Emberling powers, but pays for it: Kuragin, whose own trial intersected directly with Scorio's past life as "Sir Kuragin, the King's Warmonger," claims that the later Eberro — the same brother Scorio saved from hanging — was betrayed by Scorio himself, handed over to the king once he'd outlived his usefulness to the rebellion. Scorio cannot reconcile this with his own memory or instincts, and the confrontation costs him a shattered wrist that he is forced to enter the Gauntlet still healing from. Naomi later explains that a Great Soul's memories branch with each trial's outcome across different lives, meaning Kuragin's version may or may not be the one that "really" happened to the Scorio standing in the Academy today.

The second Gauntlet run

Scorio enters the Gauntlet with Leonis, Lianshi, and Naomi, all four now Emberlings against a field including Jova Spike, who has already reached Tomb Spark. The team crushes the first ten Char- and Cinder-tier chambers together, but the cost mounts: Naomi is struck down from behind by an animated golden statuette in the tenth chamber (room of the "dancing" assassins), Lianshi is pulled into a hidden funnel trap in the eleventh, and Leonis is overwhelmed holding off a swarm of small fiends alone to buy Scorio time in the twelfth, urging him to make it further than any of them.

A second trial and a change of heart

Alone, wounded, and nearly unable to stand, Scorio confronts the accumulated voices of his past — Praximar's cruelty, Kuragin's accusation, Jova's contempt, Imogen's own words back at him — and for the first time stops blaming Praximar, or the system, or anyone else for what his past selves may have done. Taking full responsibility for his own legacy without excuse triggers a spontaneous Second Trial: a vision of his rebel council choosing, at his own urging, to abandon a doomed direct confrontation with the king in favor of a long guerrilla campaign — the moment he first took command of the rebellion in his own right. He passes, reaching Tomb Spark, emerging fully healed with a condensed reservoir, a new defensive shroud, an area-effect aura that can overwhelm a foe's will, and horns.

Besting Jova Spike

As a Tomb Spark, Scorio destroys a rock fiend that once killed several of the Warrens' exiles, survives an acid-filled sphere chamber and an invisible-fiend hallway, and reaches the eighteenth chamber — a room of five rotating platforms guarded by masked executioners — where the Gauntlet shows him a vision of how far Jova got before being stopped on the third platform. Scorio, badly wounded and out of mana, forces himself one platform further than she managed before his own run ends — besting her by a single chamber and winning their wager.

Victory, defiance, and departure

Revived, Scorio learns he placed first in the entire cohort, ahead of Jova (second) and Chen She (third). Chancellor Praximar tries to frame the win as proof of the Academy's benevolence and press Scorio into public service quelling Bastion's unrest, but Scorio publicly declines both further Academy training and sponsorship from any House. Jova, having independently read a warning message left in her own journal by Scorio's past self — two centuries old, instructing his future incarnation to trust neither the Houses, the Imperators, nor "the Herdsmen," and to strive to become the Infernarch and break the cycle — publicly declines as well and leaves the ceremony with him. By the end of Book 1, Scorio, Jova, Lianshi, Leonis, Naomi, Juniper, and Zala have formed a seven-person fellowship and left the Academy together, intending to contact a group called Manticore to smuggle them out onto the Rascor Plains in search of the truth behind the warning.

Book 2

Fleeing Bastion & the Bastion uprising

Book 2 opens immediately at Book 1's close: the fellowship flees the Academy under a citywide bounty Praximar placed on their heads (a guaranteed second-year spot for whoever captures one of them), fending off a squad of lower-ranked classmates in the streets before going to ground with Scorio's old Warrens contact Nissa, who survived Book 1 and has since Ignited her Heart. Scorio witnesses a House Hydra Enforcer, Flame Vault Xiang Xi, burn protestors alive for defying martial law — and kills him in retaliation, the fellowship's first outright act of open rebellion against the Houses (Book 2, Chapter 3). He personally talks Bastion's rebel leadership, including a resurfaced Dola and a woman named Abentha, into a ten-day truce: if he can reach the White Queen at the Fiery Shoals and bring her back to arbitrate, the uprising stands down (Book 2, Chapter 6).

Joining Manticore & the road to the Fiery Shoals

Old contacts connect the fellowship to Manticore, an independent Deep Hell outfit; Dread Blaze Evelyn escorts them out of Bastion as provisional recruits (Book 2, Chapter 8–9). Crossing the Rascor Plains for the first time, Scorio is tested and soundly beaten in sparring by two more of Manticore's Dread Blazes, Evelyn and Davelos, and begins deliberately coaching Leonis, Lianshi, and Naomi toward Tomb Spark using a working theory he develops with Jova: that real rank breakthroughs come from emotional self-honesty, not just Heart training (Book 2, Chapter 11–12).

Near death at the Golden Circuit

A goldyolk swarm near one of the Plains' floating Gold islands nearly overwhelms the party, and the fight draws in a pack of Ixithilions that leave Scorio impaled and minutes from death before Nova, the White Queen, personally intervenes, kills the fiends, and heals the entire group (Book 2, Chapter 15–16). Recognizing Scorio, Nova brings the fellowship to the Fiery Shoals directly, days ahead of schedule.

Addressing the White Queen's council

At a full council session, Scorio makes an impassioned public case for Bastion's poor — taxes, shrinking wards, the unacknowledged failure of the Empyreal Prophecy — over open hostility from hardliners like Amity and Balco, with Pyre Lady Moira pressing him for concrete policy rather than dismissing him outright (Book 2, Chapter 19). In the same session, Nova announces she intends to leave the Fiery Shoals and the Rascor Plains behind for good to fight closer to the Pit, and asks Scorio to help assemble the rebellion's leaders for a final audience before she rules on Bastion's future.

The Queen's Accord

Nova confirms, before the full Fiery Shoals court, that the Empyreal Prophecy failed outright and that Bastion's Archspire has perhaps a decade left before it stops reincarnating Great Souls entirely — then portals the assembled court, Scorio's fellowship included, directly to Bastion's Council Citadel and announces she is leaving the Fiery Shoals permanently for Deep Hell, handing power to a restructured Celestial Consortium bound by Heart Oaths (Book 2, Chapter 21). At her request, Scorio personally carries the terms to Dola, Abentha, and the rest of Bastion's rebel leadership, who negotiate a fixed share of Gold mana earmarked for the city itself — a request Scorio's own account of the meeting persuades Nova to fold into her final charter (Book 2, Chapter 22). Days later, all four Autocrators publicly swear Heart Oaths to the ratified Queen's Accord in Imperator Square before a crowd of thousands, and Nova departs for Deep Hell for good (Book 2, Chapter 30).

A rift with Jova

Waiting on the Council's ruling, Scorio is confronted by Jova over what she sees as his abandoned "obsessive" drive for power in favor of Bastion's politics; the argument turns genuinely cruel — Scorio calls her "a punching bag that keeps getting up" compared to himself and Naomi, and immediately apologizes, but the damage doesn't undo (Book 2, Chapter 23).

Meeting Dameon, and a diagnosis

Scorio finally meets Manticore's Dread Blaze Dameon in person, alongside fellow Dread Blazes Simeon, Davelos, and Evelyn, in a friendly team scrimmage that goes badly for him: Dameon diagnoses a real, previously unaddressed flaw — months of training on nothing but raw Coal mana have left Scorio's body untempered beneath his rank, bleeding mana out faster than he can absorb it into physical resilience. Dameon agrees to bring him along regardless, but warns him plainly that fixing it will cost him "double" whatever the rest of the fellowship has to put in (Book 2, Chapters 24–25).

The Iron Tyrant's offer, and a House Kraken trap

Captain Thorne relays a personal recruitment offer from the Iron Tyrant himself — a path to the elite Iron Vanguard, Scorio only, no friends included — which Scorio declines outright on loyalty grounds (Book 2, Chapter 26). The same night, Ravenna Accardi, now House Kraken, lures him into what turns out to be an Autocrator Octavia-orchestrated honeytrap meant to extract information about Manticore and the White Queen; Ravenna breaks character and confesses the whole scheme before it goes further, and the two trade information instead — Scorio reveals Praximar's corrupt mana-theft arrangement with House Basilisk; Ravenna passes on Octavia's own suspicion that a Deep Hell liaison, Charnel Duke Plassus, is somehow being manipulated (Book 2, Chapters 27–28).

A night with Nox

Shaken and restless, Scorio wanders back to the ruins and reunites with Nox, who is preparing to leave Bastion for good via hidden tunnels fiends use to move freely in and out of the city — a detail that complicates the standard account of Bastion having always been under siege, since Nox says most fiends have no real interest in entering a place as "small, dangerous" and Coal-poor as Bastion in the first place. The conversation steadies him, and he resolves his post-victory malaise is a natural lull rather than a real loss of drive (Book 2, Chapter 29).

Departure for the Chasm

Bastion's rebel leaders send Scorio off with public thanks and a gift he hasn't yet opened; the fellowship boards Manticore's whale ship, The Sloop, crewed by the pilot Ydrielle, and crosses the Rain Wall and Farmlands to reach the Chasm, Manticore's base of operations and the site of a lost artifact Dameon is hunting using memories from a past life as an Imperator (Book 2, Chapters 30–33).

The Coal purge

Diagnosed with the same untempered-Coal problem as Scorio, Naomi joins him in an unsparing regimen set by Dameon: smashing and hauling boulders by hand, Hearts forbidden from Igniting, meant to physically "wring" months of accumulated Coal out of their bodies before either can safely temper on better mana. It lasts five months, isolates them from the rest of the fellowship's day-to-day camaraderie, and brings Scorio to a genuine low point — including a moment of real indifference to his own safety at the Chasm's edge — before he demands real training or threatens to walk away, and Manticore's Dread Blazes agree the purge has gone far enough (Book 2, Chapters 34–38).

Bronze training, and the end of a friendship

Escorted by a newly promoted Jova to the Chasm's Bronze level to begin healing his Heart properly, Scorio apologizes again for their Chapter 23 argument; Jova rebuffs him flatly, telling him he was never truly her equal and that she'll be "long gone" among people who value real work by the time he catches up. Scorio accepts it and moves on without further argument — a real change from his temper earlier in the Book (Book 2, Chapter 39).

The vault raid

Three weeks later, Manticore brings in House Kraken's Pyre Lady Druanna to force open a collapsed vault holding the artifact, the Font, in exchange for exclusive sale rights. Scorio pulls an anxious, extended stint of topside perimeter duty while the raid, expected to take about two hours, stretches to nearly three full day-night cycles. Book 2, Chapter 40 ends unresolved: the recovery cage returns with Dameon carrying Druanna's body, and Dread Blazes Evelyn, Simeon, Mavel, and Tsing Ma all missing. Their fates are not yet known.

Abilities

  • Igneous Heart: Ignited to Tomb Spark by the end of Book 1, having condensed his once-huge reservoir back to its original size during his Second Trial. Still significantly fractured from earlier overuse, though healing is faster and easier to manage at his new rank.
  • Scaled combat form: black scales sweep across his arms, shoulders, back, and thighs when ignited, with six-inch, white-hot talons capable of cutting through stone and armor; as a Tomb Spark, twin curving horns also emerge.
  • Shroud: a Tomb Spark-rank defensive barrier that can be summoned to block a single powerful attack, at significant mana cost.
  • Tomb Spark aura: a hemispherical field of overwhelming presence that can stun, intimidate, or freeze a lesser foe in place; instrumental in his solo kill of a purple-flamed rock fiend and his fight through the eighteenth Gauntlet chamber.
  • Delightful Secret Marinating technique (adapted): learned in outline from Nox and used once, via Imperial Ghost Toad gel, to force an ignition past his Heart's natural limits — the method by which he reached Emberling.
  • Radert's treasures: a collapsible bridge, an anchoring steel rod, and a stick of chalk that raises invisible walls of force.
  • Combat training: increasingly disciplined hand-to-hand technique under Naomi, including the meditative "First Form," combined with hard-won experience from dozens of deaths in the old Gauntlet.
  • Tomb Spark aura, clarified (Book 2): confirmed to be a compulsion-type power rather than pure intimidation — it makes whatever Scorio wills feel overwhelmingly important to anyone caught in its field, capable of both fixating a target's attention and physically pressing them away, though it does not override free will outright.
  • Untempered body (diagnosed Book 2, Chapter 24): months of training on nothing but raw Coal mana left his body "venting" mana faster than it could absorb it into physical resilience, a flaw distinct from his fractured Heart. Addressed via a five-month, Ignition-forbidden manual-labor regimen (Book 2, Chapters 34–38) that Manticore's Dread Blazes call unprecedented in its completeness; as of Book 2, Chapter 39 he has begun properly tempering with Bronze mana for the first time.

Relationships

Leonis & Lianshi

Reunited in Book 1, Chapter 23, the three now train together every Eighthday. Both have become Scorio's most reliable support — financially, emotionally, and now magically, as the discovery of the old Gauntlet lets them train as equals for the first time.

Naomi

What began as a hostage negotiation became the most important relationship in Scorio's life, fractured after a drunken argument at the Flame, and was then repaired — first through her secret vigil over him, then her Sapphire vials sacrificed to save his gambit, and finally fighting at his side through the Gauntlet's final run. By the end of Book 1 she is again one of his closest companions and a founding member of the fellowship leaving the Academy with him.

Jova Spike

Once a stranger who dismissed him outright, then his closest ally outside his original circle. Their wager over the Gauntlet run — and the two-century-old warning Scorio's past self left in her journal — reshaped both their futures at Book 1's end. That closeness didn't survive Book 2: a cruel exchange in Chapter 23 and Jova's own rapid rise to Flame Vault ended the friendship outright by Chapter 39, with Jova telling him plainly he was never truly her equal. See her dedicated article for details.

Ravenna Accardi

A House Kraken Emberling and Jova's old public rival at the Academy. Used as bait in an Octavia-orchestrated honeytrap in Book 2, Chapter 27, she breaks character mid-scheme and confesses outright rather than go through with it; the two part on unexpectedly honest terms, trading real information instead of a night neither wants.

Dameon

Manticore's Dread Blaze leader, and the one whose approval Scorio's provisional membership actually depends on. Diagnoses Scorio's untempered body bluntly, sets him a punishing recovery regimen, and doesn't pretend the cost isn't real — but never withdraws the offer to bring him along either.

Pyre Lord Praximar

The man who condemned and physically wounded him, later blackmailed him over the Dola affair, and was finally humiliated in public when Scorio won the Gauntlet outright and rejected everything Praximar tried to offer him.

Sol

A onetime past-life "companion," and the Imperator whose favor secured Scorio's readmission, Naomi's readmission, and the healing of his Heart. Scorio owes him a debt he has no way to repay.

Kuragin

A top-five Emberling and rival whom Scorio defeated in an unsanctioned duel to learn what Kuragin's own trial revealed about Scorio's past-life brother — a claim of betrayal Scorio has not been able to reconcile with his own memory.

Leonis & Lianshi

Both fought at his side through the final Gauntlet run — Leonis holding off a fiend swarm alone to buy him time, Lianshi caught by a hidden funnel trap — and both chose, without hesitation, to leave the Academy and follow him regardless of the danger.

Nissa

A Warrens-era acquaintance who abandoned Scorio to the fiend that killed Havert and Hestia; resurfaces in Book 2 as a rebel agitator secretly working for House Basilisk. Scorio doesn't fully trust her, but she vouches for him with Bastion's rebel leadership and is instrumental in getting the fellowship off the streets and connected to Manticore.

Nova, "the White Queen"

Saves Scorio's life at the Golden Circuit and personally hears out his case for Bastion's poor. Her decision to withdraw from the Fiery Shoals happens, in part, because of the audience he requested — making him an unwitting catalyst for one of the biggest political shifts on the Rascor Plains in years.

Trivia

  • Scorio adopted "Memek" as an alias while infiltrating the Academy — borrowed on the spot from a real busker he'd befriended earlier the same Book.
  • He is the only major character shown so far whose reincarnation gap (233 years) is wildly out of line with the rest of his cohort; a warning left in Jova Spike's journal, written by his own past self, suggests it was deliberate.
  • Sol addresses him as "old friend," implying a much closer and longer past-life relationship than Scorio has any memory of.
  • He is only the second member of his cohort (after Jova) to reach Tomb Spark before the semester's end, and the only one to do so entirely without House or Academy resources.
  • Nox, the Old Academy's guardian toad-fiend, addresses him as "Favorite Friend Scorio."
  • Scorio's past-life warning message names a mysterious group called "the Herdsmen" alongside the Houses and Imperators as forces not to be trusted — their identity is completely unexplained as of Book 1, Chapter 80.
  • His stated Book 2 goal — becoming the Infernarch, a title Bastion has not seen filled in nearly a thousand years — is, by his own admission, likely impossible within the roughly seven to ten years before the ruins consume Bastion entirely.
  • Kills Flame Vault Xiang Xi, a House Hydra Enforcer, in Book 2, Chapter 3 — the fellowship's first killing of a Great Soul in open defiance of a House, and the act that makes them wanted for more than just truancy.
  • Publicly reveals his full past-life title list to a crowd of Bastion rebels to prove his sincerity (Book 2, Chapter 6) — the first time he's done so outside the Academy or a life-or-death fight.
  • Manticore's leadership jokingly credit the fellowship's run of improbable luck to "Fate's Whisper," a folk phenomenon said to originate from the Vale of Regrets.
  • Personally recruited by name by the Iron Tyrant of the Fury Spires, via Captain Thorne — an offer he declines because it excludes his friends (Book 2, Chapter 26).
  • Survives an unprecedented five-month, Ignition-forbidden purge of Coal-tempering alongside Naomi; Manticore's Dread Blazes say they've never seen anyone complete it (Book 2, Chapters 34–38).
  • As of Book 2, Chapter 40, was present for — but not part of — the raid on the Chasm's Gold-level vault that ends the chapter with four Dread Blazes missing and Druanna unconscious or worse; his own fate is unaffected, but the outcome for the others is unresolved.
