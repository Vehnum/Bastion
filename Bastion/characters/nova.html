Nova, "the White Queen"

A legendary defender with more history than she's shown so far

Nova, called "the White Queen," is a legendary Charnel Duchess who leads Bastion's assembled defenders against Imogen's Book 1, Chapter 38–40 assault, and who rules the Fiery Shoals and the surrounding Rascor Plains by right of preeminence. The wiki flagged her in Book 1 as a figure worth tracking closely, and Book 2 pays that off directly: she saves Scorio's life, hears his case for Bastion's poor, and announces her intention to abandon her post at the Shoals for good.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Golden wings and a white Ferula scepter are her most visible markers in battle, consistent with the "White Queen" title.

Personality

Commanding but personally warm. Thanks Scorio directly and sincerely for his role in the battle against Imogen, and follows through on Sol's readmission request herself rather than delegating it — small gestures that read as genuine rather than performative.

Biography

Book 1

When Imogen descends on Bastion in Book 1, Chapter 38, Nova leads the assembled defenders (coordinated by the tactician Desiree) in a deliberate holding action — by explicit admission, none of them expect to actually defeat an Imperator, only to buy time for reinforcements. She and Lady Maeve are visibly relieved that Imogen has come alone, implying Nova has direct knowledge of at least two other individuals, Zellair and Joranvyn, feared to be of comparable or greater danger — later confirmed to be fellow members of the Fallen, formerly of House Basilisk.

According to Nova, Imogen's mind was permanently fractured eight years ago by "the price she paid to save the other Imperators" — the same incident that caused the collapse of Bastion's Empyreal Prophecy. Mid-battle, she and Imogen converse with a strange, respectful familiarity that suggests real prior history or mutual regard between them, never explained on the page.

After Sol defeats Imogen, Nova personally escorts Scorio back to Chancellor Praximar and stays to ensure Sol's request — readmitting both Scorio and Naomi — is actually honored, thanking Scorio warmly before departing to attend to a crisis of her own at "the Fiery Shoals," a location not yet described.

Book 2

The Iron Tyrant's suitors & a private ambition (Book 2 Interlude)

Between books, Nova is shown fielding a wave of invitations and pressure from other House and Deep Hell powers — Crimson Earl Endergrast, the Circle of Blades, Maulven the Yellow, Blood Baroness Shikibu — all urging her to abandon Bastion and commit her full strength to the war on the Pit, now that so few Great Souls remain to fight it. Her aide, the Dread Blaze Desiree, assumes Nova would advance to Crimson Earl if she left; privately, Nova intends to skip that rank entirely and become a full Imperator. She resolves that before leaving, she will bind Bastion's Autocrators to Heart Oaths guaranteeing an equitable, peaceful power-sharing arrangement over the Fiery Shoals' Gold mana in her absence (Book 2, Interlude — The White Queen).

Rescuing Scorio at the Golden Circuit

Nova's preeminence over the Rascor Plains lets her sense powerful fiends the moment they Ignite nearby. She personally intervenes when a pack of Ixithilions nearly kills Scorio and Jova Spike near one of the floating Gold islands, destroying the fiends in three shots from her Ferula and fully healing the entire party with a wave of white light. Recognizing Scorio, she opens a portal directly to the Fiery Shoals for the whole group, skipping the rest of their overland journey (Book 2, Chapter 16).

Hearing Scorio's case & announcing her departure

Nova convenes a full council at the Fiery Shoals to hear Scorio's account of Bastion's brewing rebellion. In the same session, she announces she will withdraw her preeminence from the Rascor Plains and Bastion for good and travel deeper into Hell to aid the war against the Pit directly — framing the timing as an opportunity to leave behind "a new and stable configuration" rather than a hasty exit. She intends to negotiate new Gold mana terms with the Iron Tyrant, bind the Bastion Autocrators to Heart Oaths, and meet personally with both the Autocrators and the rebellion's leaders before making a final ruling on Bastion's future (Book 2, Chapter 19).

Abilities

  • Rank: Charnel Duchess, burning Ruby — among the highest confirmed ranks seen in Bastion outside the Imperators.
  • Halo: a large-scale restorative/protective effect, unique among named characters so far.
  • Ferula: a signature manifested weapon — a white scepter.
  • Flight: golden wings.
  • Preeminence: a Charnel-Duchess-and-above authority that grants dominance over a claimed domain — her preeminence now extends over much of the Rascor Plains around the Fiery Shoals, letting her sense powerful fiends the moment they Ignite their Hearts and intervene from a distance.
  • Portal travel: can open instant portals within a "band" of mana she can reach, but jumping between distant bands of Hell is far more costly — a limitation given as the real reason she's stayed at the Fiery Shoals for years rather than advancing deeper.

Relationships

Imogen

Leads Bastion's defense against her, but converses with her mid-battle with a strange, respectful familiarity that implies unexplained prior history.

Sol

Refers to him with evident personal warmth and relief when he arrives, suggesting a longstanding alliance or friendship between the two.

Scorio

Personally secures his readmission to the Academy, thanks him for his role in the battle against Imogen, and in Book 2 saves his life outright before hearing his case for Bastion's poor — a case that directly precedes her announcement that she's leaving the Rascor Plains.

Desiree

A Dread Blaze and her closest aide, both as battlefield tactician in Book 1 and political advisor in Book 2. Desiree is the one drafting the Heart Oath framework meant to keep Bastion stable after Nova's departure.

Trivia

  • Directly informs Praximar of Sol's arrival and demands (per Praximar's own page), implying she operates with authority independent of, and possibly above, the Academy's chancellor.
  • Her offhand explanation of Imogen's fracturing is the wiki's only first-hand account of what actually happened during the Empyreal Prophecy's failed assault on the Pit — see Great Souls & Rank for the fuller context.
  • Departs to handle a crisis at "the Fiery Shoals" — an unexplained location and one of several loose threads the wiki expects to matter more in Book 2.
  • Privately intends to become a full Imperator rather than stopping at Crimson Earl, a goal her own aide Desiree doesn't seem to know (Book 2, Interlude — The White Queen).
  • Her announced departure from the Fiery Shoals visibly unsettles nearly everyone at her own council, even those too cautious to say so to her face (Book 2, Chapter 19).
