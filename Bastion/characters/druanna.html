Pyre Lady Druanna

Rated above Praximar in a straight fight.

Oversees House Kraken's security across the Farmlands. Encountered on patrol; can summon a twenty-foot obsidian golem wielding six swords and sprout blades of black glass from shadow. Manticore's Davelos rates her above Praximar in a straight fight.

Brought to the Chasm in Book 2, Chapter 40 at Manticore's request, arriving with her own escort of two Dread Blazes, the dour, grim Mavel and the round-faced, genial Tsing Ma. Hasn't visited the Chasm in six years by her own account, and is shown giving Jova a mark of respect greater than she shows even Dameon — attributed on the page to Jova's past-life pedigree, not just her current rank. Druanna leads the descent to the Chasm's Gold level with her six-armed golem to force open the collapsed vault holding Manticore's target artifact, the Font.

Book 2, Chapter 40 ends on an unresolved cliffhanger. The recovery cage that was expected back in about two hours instead returns after nearly three full day-night cycles, with Dameon carrying Druanna's body and Mavel, Tsing Ma, and two other Dread Blazes all missing. Her condition — unconscious, gravely wounded, or worse — is not stated on the page, and should not be assumed either way pending later chapters.
