Evelyn

"Any friend of Druanna's is a friend of ours."

Evelyn is one of the five founding Dread Blazes of Manticore and the first of them the fellowship meets, recruiting them into the organization in the Farmlands well before Davelos comes around to the idea. She is missing, along with Simeon and two of Druanna's escorts, after the Book 2, Chapter 40 vault raid.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

Presents as warm and approachable even before she opens her mouth — the kind of welcoming presence that puts the fellowship at ease within moments of meeting her in the Farmlands. No further physical description is given on the page beyond her manner and bearing.

Personality

Effusive and immediately welcoming, the opposite of Davelos's guarded skepticism. She vouches for the fellowship on Druanna's word alone and argues Davelos into giving them a chance rather than waiting for proof. Easily amused — she's the one who teases Davelos in front of the group about his obvious admiration for Druanna.

Biography

Recruiting the fellowship

Evelyn meets the fellowship first among Manticore's founders, in the Farmlands. She tests all seven members of the group in unarmed combat at once and defeats them without real difficulty, but is impressed enough by the attempt — and by Scorio and Jova Spike placing as the top two graduates of their Academy class — to recruit them on the strength of Druanna's recommendation, well before Davelos is convinced. Her welcome is immediate and unguarded, setting the tone for the group's entire relationship with Manticore going forward (Book 2, Chapter 8).

Leaving Bastion

Accompanies the fellowship out of Bastion as they set out for the Rascor Plains, continuing to build rapport with the group during the journey (Book 2, Chapters 9–10).

Crossing the Rain Wall and testing the newcomers

Present alongside Davelos when he tests the fellowship in combat, though she herself does not fight in the sparring match; afterward she teases Davelos openly about his reaction to meeting Druanna (Book 2, Chapters 12, 14).

The Ixithilion attack

Fights at Davelos's side during the goldyolk swarm and the subsequent Ixithilion ambush near the Golden Circuit, the two of them working in close coordination against the insectoid attackers (Book 2, Chapters 15–16).

At the Fiery Shoals

Present for Nova's council session and the reunion with Simeon, continuing on with the group toward the Chasm and the vault beneath the Fiery Shoals (Book 2, Chapters 16–20).

The vault raid

Goes below with the raiding party at the Book 2, Chapter 40 cliffhanger and is among those missing afterward, along with Simeon and two of Druanna's own escorts — unlike Davelos, who remains topside and is confirmed safe.

Abilities

  • Rank: Dread Blaze.
  • Hair-web travel: her hair extends into an enormous entangling web, letting her "travel" instantly along her own strands — functionally an unwilling-to-be-struck compulsion rather than true teleportation.

Relationships

Davelos

A fellow founding Dread Blaze and old friend; the two greet each other warmly and fight together seamlessly. She's also the one who teases him about his obvious interest in Druanna.

Druanna

Recruits the fellowship on Druanna's recommendation alone, suggesting an existing relationship of trust between the two of them predating the story.

Simeon

Fellow founding Dread Blaze, reunited with the group at the Fiery Shoals; later missing alongside her after the Chapter 40 vault raid.

Trivia

  • The first of Manticore's five founding Dread Blazes the fellowship meets.
  • One of the characters left unaccounted for after the Book 2, Chapter 40 cliffhanger, along with Simeon and two of Druanna's escorts.
