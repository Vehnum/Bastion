Sol

"I will not be party to genocide."

Sol, also known as Sol the Just, is an Imperator and the estranged husband of Imogen. He arrives at Bastion in Book 1, Chapter 41 to stop her assault, and his intervention — assisted, at the critical moment, by Scorio — brings the crisis to an end.

Appearance Personality Biography Abilities Relationships Trivia

Appearance

A powerfully built man who appears in his forties despite the physique of someone half that age, with long ash-brown hair, a beard flecked with white at the chin, and a severe, dauntless expression. Dressed in plain cream robes tied with a black sash — deliberately unadorned for someone of Imperator rank.

Personality

Grave, controlled, and unmistakably still in love with the woman he now has to fight. Sol carries visible grief and self-recrimination — he wonders aloud whether "his greatest failing" was letting his love for Imogen blind him for too long — but never lets that grief compromise his judgment. He is unfailingly courteous to those weaker than him, including Scorio, and quick to reward genuine service.

Biography

Confronting Imogen

Sol descends into the basilica mid-crisis, having sensed Imogen's presence from elsewhere in hell, and pleads with her to stand down: the Portal cannot be opened from Bastion's side, and forcing it would mean the deaths of the tens of thousands of ordinary citizens still living in the city. Imogen refuses, framing the deaths as liberation rather than genocide, and the two fight in earnest — a running battle across Bastion in which Sol repeatedly multiplies into over a hundred simultaneous duplicates, each wielding a golden, lightning-wreathed Ferula spear and a fluctuating golden Halo.

At the fight's climax, with his duplicates spent and his own strength failing against Imogen's, Scorio's brief interruption of her total focus is enough for Sol to overwhelm her attack and force her to yield preeminence. Imogen flees through a seam of darkness rather than be destroyed. Sol does not pursue.

Rewarding Scorio

Sol heals the damage Scorio's own gambit inflicted on his Igneous Heart, restoring it to its state before Imogen's intervention, and personally intervenes with Chancellor Praximar to secure Scorio's readmission to the Academy — and, at Scorio's specific request, Naomi's as well. He reveals, almost in passing, that he and Scorio were "companions" in a past life — that Scorio helped him "destroy Ivaskar against the Herdsmen's wishes" and later betrayed someone called "the Ul-Master" alongside him — but declines to say more, promising only that they will speak again "deeper in Hell."

Abilities

  • Rank: Imperator, one of only 76 in Bastion's history.
  • Ferula & Halo: a golden, lightning-wreathed spear and a fluctuating golden disc of light that can call down bolts of golden lightning.
  • Duplication: can manifest over a hundred simultaneous, independently combat-capable duplicates of himself, each collapsing back into the original when spent.
  • Preeminence: as with Imogen, can force a defeated opponent's power to yield entirely once his attack overwhelms theirs, and can heal Igneous Heart damage in others.

Relationships

Imogen

His wife, in every past life they can both remember. He refuses to return to her, but the grief is plainly unresolved on both sides; Imogen's parting words accuse him of being responsible for whatever happens next.

Scorio

A "past-life companion" whose distraction of Imogen at the critical moment turned the battle. Sol rewards him generously — healing his Heart and securing his and Naomi's readmission — while withholding almost everything about their shared history.

Trivia

  • The White Queen, Nova, refers to him with evident personal warmth and relief, suggesting a longstanding alliance or friendship between the two.
  • Sol implies he "lost the ability to predict [Imogen's] actions a long time ago," hinting that this was far from her first destructive appearance.
