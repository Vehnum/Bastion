Old Memek

Comic relief who became a cover identity, then a keeper of the Flame.

A garrulous, self-mythologizing elderly street musician who briefly guides Scorio through Ward 11 in search of Feiyan and Helena's club. Comic relief; Scorio later borrows his name as a cover identity when infiltrating the Academy.

In Book 2, with the Flame formally closed, Memek is the one actually keeping it running and lets the fellowship shelter there while they wait to meet Manticore (Book 2, Chapters 7–9).
