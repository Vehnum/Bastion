Simeon

"Thanks, Davelos." — "Any time, buddy."

One of Manticore's five founding Dread Blazes, met by the fellowship at the Fiery Shoals rather than in the Farmlands with Evelyn and Davelos. Poetic and reserved, he shares Lianshi and Scorio's fascination with Hell's unsolved mysteries.

His power — a defensive form of bodily possession — visibly makes him uncomfortable to discuss, and he only reveals its nature at all because Davelos outs him for it in front of the newcomers, teasing him openly ("Thanks, Davelos." / "Any time, buddy."). The two are evidently old friends despite Simeon's obvious embarrassment (Book 2, Chapter 20).

He's openly in a relationship with Ydrielle, the Sloop's pilot.

At the Chasm, Simeon takes part in Manticore's Chapter 40 raid on a collapsed vault holding the artifact Dameon has been hunting. Book 2, Chapter 40 ends on an unresolved cliffhanger: the recovery cage surfaces with Dameon carrying House Kraken's Pyre Lady Druanna, but Simeon and Evelyn are both missing, along with two of Druanna's own Dread Blazes. His fate is not yet known.
