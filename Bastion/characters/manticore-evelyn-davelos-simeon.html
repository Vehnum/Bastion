Manticore: Evelyn, Davelos & Simeon

Three of five founders. Each personally tested the fellowship.

Three of Manticore's five founding Dread Blazes, each of whom personally tests the fellowship before vouching for them. Evelyn is warm, playful, and recruits the group out of Bastion; her hair extends into an entangling web and lets her "travel" along it instantly. Davelos is more guarded about outsiders and can turn his body to a Copper-and-Coal metallic mist; he breaks Leonis's nose and Zala's ribs in a single sparring bout without much apparent concern. Simeon, met at the Fiery Shoals, is poetic and reserved, sharing Lianshi and Scorio's fascination with Hell's unsolved mysteries; his own power, a defensive form of bodily possession, visibly makes him uncomfortable to discuss.
