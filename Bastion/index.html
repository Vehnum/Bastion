Codex Entry · Overview

Bastion is dying, and it has ten years left to find out why.

A hollow cylinder built into the first plain of hell, Bastion has spent nearly a thousand years reincarnating its fallen heroes — the Great Souls — to fight a war it may already be losing. This archive follows Scorio, a newly-reborn soul whose own past turns out to be the last thing anyone expected. Book 1 never leaves Bastion; later books are expected to take the story beyond it.

Setting: Bastion, the Rascor Plains, Hell Genre: Progression Fantasy / LitRPG Status: Book 1, Chapter 80
