Story · Book Summary

Book 1

Untitled — a complete reader's summary (Chapters 1–80)

Book 1 opens with Scorio waking with total amnesia inside the Gauntlet, and follows his fall from promising newcomer to condemned exile to self-made power — alongside his companions Lianshi and Leonis, and his mentor Naomi — entirely within the walls of Bastion. The book never leaves the city, ending instead with its whole cast departing it for the first time.

Chapters 1–10 — The Gauntlet and the Red List

Scorio, Lianshi, and Leonis fight through the Gauntlet's death-trap chambers together, losing two other group members along the way. Reborn at the Academy, Scorio learns his past life was a notorious villain, The Scourer, and is declared Proscribed and thrown through the Final Door into the Warrens by Chancellor Praximar, who drains part of his Igneous Heart on the way out. In the Warrens, Scorio survives a catastrophic failed escape attempt that kills or scatters everyone sheltering there, discovers a dead explorer's cache of magic items, and claws his way out into Bastion's ruined outskirts, where he meets two street musicians who agree to help him.

Chapters 11–20 — The Nightmare Lady

Broke and alone, Scorio takes a job from a House Basilisk crew boss, Dola, and steals a pair of rare Sapphire mana vials during the heist rather than hand them over. This draws the attention of the Nightmare Lady — Naomi, a self-taught Emberling who rules a corner of the ruins. After Scorio out-maneuvers her in a standoff, she agrees to train him. Over the following chapters he learns the fundamentals of mana manipulation, discovers Naomi's own outsider history at the Academy, and finally ignites his Igneous Heart at the book's midpoint, via a deliberately dangerous overdose of homemade Black Star tincture.

Chapters 21–30 — Reunion and the Empty Locker

Newly a Cinder, Scorio reunites with Lianshi and Leonis, both thriving at the Academy and immediately willing to risk their own standing to help him. With their support he infiltrates the Academy to check his own reincarnation locker — and finds it empty except for one name carved into the wall: Jova Spike, the cohort's standout prodigy. In parallel, Scorio establishes a hidden Black Star flower farm and begins scouting the deep ruins for a rumored source of richer mana, eventually sighting the ruins of Bastion's original, abandoned campus.

Chapters 31–40 — The Old Academy and Imogen

Scorio, Leonis, and Lianshi reach the Old Academy and discover a still-functioning, completely unmonitored version of the Gauntlet — giving them unlimited access to death-based training. Scorio leans on it hard enough to alarm everyone around him, prompting Naomi to intervene and open up about her own past. Then the book's climax arrives: Imogen, an Imperator drawn by residual power in the old, broken Archspire, descends on Bastion convinced the long-sealed way home has reopened. Dozens of the city's most powerful Great Souls converge in a desperate holding action against her, and Scorio's group narrowly escapes her incapacitating fog into the Gauntlet as the chapter ends — setting up the climax resolved in Chapters 41–42, below.

Chapters 41–50 — Readmission and the Tournament

Alone among his friends, Scorio escapes Imogen's mind-control fog and talks his way into remaining conscious during her rampage, drawing on a past-life connection she recognizes. When her estranged husband, the Imperator Sol, arrives to stop her, Scorio's single, well-timed distraction turns the fight; Imogen is forced to yield and flees Bastion. Sol heals Scorio's Heart and personally secures both his and Naomi's readmission to the Academy.

Back at the Academy, the group is reunited in Leonis and Lianshi's suite, and Scorio and Naomi throw themselves into the full student curriculum — runs, sparring, tactics, and mana theory — only to learn that Scorio's months of unsupervised training have left his Igneous Heart significantly fractured. A mess-hall confrontation with a rival Emberling, Kuragin, nearly gets both Scorio and Naomi expelled, earning them a final warning directly from Chancellor Praximar. Scorio enters the ongoing class tournament's loser's bracket as his best hope of earning House sponsorship, wins two brutal bouts — deliberately burning a sliver of his own Heart to survive the second — and is recruited by House Chimera at the book's current end point, contract still unsigned.

Chapters 51–60 — Fractures

The tournament run that earned Scorio a House Chimera offer starts to cost him almost immediately. Chancellor Praximar, having learned of Scorio's old crime with Dola through an informant known only as "Kayla," blackmails him in Chapter 53: throw a match, avoid clearing the old Gauntlet's sixth chamber, and be gone from the Academy by semester's end, or face exposure. Around the same time, a chance confrontation with the cohort's standout prodigy, Jova Spike, turns into a wager over their unexplained locker connection — one Scorio narrowly loses on points against her in an informal duel, though the wager itself (outperform her at the coming Gauntlet run, or leave the Academy for good) remains live.

A celebration at Feiyan and Helena's club curdles into disaster: a drunk, euphoric Scorio snaps at Naomi when she warns him off House Chimera, and she disappears rather than return to the suite. A dedicated interlude chapter follows her alone afterward, in real distress, before she reaches a quiet decision: rather than come back, she'll watch over Scorio from the ruins in secret, without his knowledge. Elsewhere, Lianshi reaches Emberling with a surprising invulnerability/immovability power, and Leonis breaks his elbow pushing himself in the old Gauntlet — both signs the group's unsupervised training is starting to exact a real physical toll. Bastion's civic unrest deepens in the background, culminating in a violent incident tied to a man named Makiros.

Scorio renounces House Chimera's offer outright rather than accept Praximar's terms, then deliberately reopens the old Gauntlet in defiance, before ultimately losing his next tournament match to a teleporting opponent, Chloe, in Chapter 60 — hinting at a "terrible idea" he has not yet revealed to anyone, group included.

Chapters 61–70 — The Marinating Gambit

Scorio's "terrible idea" is revealed: aided by a library treatise and House Chimera recruiter Gelegos, he strikes a bargain with Nox, the Old Academy's Imperial Ghost Toad guardian, to learn an adapted version of the Delightful Secret Marinating technique — a way to force his fractured Heart past Emberling using a mana-conductive "imperial gel" pool. He plants a second, massive Black Star farm to fuel the attempt, only for an Alben Worm to nearly destroy it; Naomi, secretly watching over the group the whole time, helps drive the worm off and gives Scorio her saved Sapphire vials to salvage the plan. Scorio reaches Emberling in the gel, his First Trial revealing a past-life brother, Eberro, whom he saved from hanging; he then defeats Naomi in a rematch to bring her back into the fold, and Praximar readmits her officially in exchange for a binding Heart Oath tying Scorio and Naomi's fate to the outcome of the coming Gauntlet run. Meanwhile Jova Spike reaches Tomb Spark first in the cohort, defeating Ravenna Accardi in the tournament's climactic match, and independently rediscovers a warning in her own journal left by Scorio's past self two centuries ago.

Chapters 71–80 — The Gauntlet's End

The semester's mandatory final Gauntlet run eliminates Naomi, Lianshi, and Leonis one after another, each falling to protect the others, while Scorio, alone from the thirteenth chamber onward, undergoes a spontaneous Second Trial after finally taking full responsibility for his past selves' actions, reaching Tomb Spark himself. He presses on through increasingly lethal chambers to see how far Jova got via the Gauntlet's shared final-vision effect, then surpasses her by a single chamber to win the entire run. At the victory ceremony, Scorio publicly rejects both further Academy training and House sponsorship; Jova, having shared the warning from her journal with him privately, does the same. Together with Leonis, Lianshi, Naomi, and Jova's own close friends Juniper and Zala, they form a seven-person fellowship and leave the Academy — and Bastion itself — for the first time in the book, intending to contact a group called Manticore and begin investigating the warning's claims about the Houses, the Imperators, and "the Herdsmen."

Open threads heading into Book 2

  • Who are "the Herdsmen," named in Scorio's past-life warning alongside the Houses and the Imperators as untrustworthy?
  • Why did Scorio take 233 years to be reborn, versus five or six for everyone else — and was it deliberate, per his own warning?
  • What is the full history between Scorio and Sol — and what did their past selves do to "the Ul-Master"?
  • Where did Imogen flee to, and will she return to Bastion?
  • Did Scorio's past self really betray his brother Eberro, as Kuragin's trial claims, or did that only happen in a different branch of his history?
  • Will Naomi finally explain her own first ignition, given she was never reincarnated and had no First Trial?
  • Is it truly possible for Scorio to become the Infernarch within Bastion's roughly seven-to-ten-year remaining lifespan?
  • What is Manticore, the group the fellowship intends to contact to leave Bastion, and can it be trusted given its poor reputation?
  • How will Chancellor Praximar respond to losing both of the semester's Tomb Sparks to public defection on the same day?
  • Who is Kayla, and what is her connection to both Dola and Praximar?
  • Bastion itself has an estimated ten years left before the expanding ruins consume it entirely, and civic unrest continues to escalate in the background.
