Book 2: Rascor Plains

Not yet begun — but the title alone confirms where the fellowship is headed

This article is a placeholder. Book 2 has not yet been read, and nothing on this page is confirmed by the text beyond its title, Rascor Plains — the same region of hell Bastion is built into. The title suggests Book 2 widens its scope from the city itself to the broader Plains surrounding it, consistent with the fellowship leaving Bastion at the very end of Book 1.

What else is known comes entirely from how Book 1 ends: Scorio, now a Tomb Spark, has left the Academy and Bastion itself for the first time, leading a seven-person fellowship — Leonis, Lianshi, Naomi, Jova Spike, and her own close friends Juniper and Zala — out onto the Rascor Plains with the help of a group called Manticore. Their stated goal is to investigate a two-century-old warning, left by Scorio's own past self, against trusting the Houses, the Imperators, or "the Herdsmen," and to work toward Scorio somehow becoming the Infernarch before Bastion's roughly seven-to-ten remaining years run out. Whether the series follows that goal directly, and what Manticore and the Herdsmen actually are, remains completely unknown until Book 2 is read.
