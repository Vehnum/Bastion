Book 2: Rascor Plains

The fellowship leaves Bastion behind — and finds out how small Bastion actually is

Book 2 opens exactly where Book 1 ends: Scorio and his seven-person fellowship flee the Academy under a bounty Praximar has placed on their heads, and are out of Bastion within days. Where Book 1 treated Bastion as effectively the whole world, Book 2 immediately recontextualizes it: the city turns out to be a single walled hole in the Rascor Plains, itself only the near edge of a Hell that extends for untold distance beyond it — the Farmlands, the floating Gold islands, the Fiery Shoals, and, farther still, the Iron Weald and the war on the Pit itself. Bastion's crisis, so central to Book 1, is repeatedly shown to register as a minor provincial squabble to the Great Souls who actually run Hell's deeper layers.

Chapters 1–10: Out of Bastion

The fellowship fights through squads of bounty-hunting classmates in the streets of Bastion, and Scorio kills a House Hydra Enforcer, Flame Vault Xiang Xi, after he burns a crowd of protestors alive. Sheltering with a resurfaced Nissa, Scorio personally negotiates a ten-day truce with Bastion's rebel leadership — including Dola and a woman named Abentha — promising to bring the Charnel Duchess Nova, the White Queen, back to arbitrate. Old contacts connect the group to Manticore, an independent Deep Hell outfit; Dread Blaze Evelyn tests the whole fellowship in single combat, wins handily, and escorts them out of Bastion as provisional recruits.

Chapters 11–20: The road to the Fiery Shoals

Crossing the Ash Belt, the Rain Wall, and the Farmlands, the fellowship is repeatedly outclassed by Manticore's senior Dread Blazes — Evelyn, Davelos, and later Simeon — while Scorio and Jova develop a working theory that real rank breakthroughs come from emotional honesty, not just Heart training. A goldyolk swarm near one of the Plains' floating Gold islands draws in a pack of Ixithilions that leave Scorio and Jova minutes from death, until Nova personally intervenes, heals the group, and brings them directly to the Fiery Shoals. There, Scorio makes his case for Bastion's poor before a full council — and Nova announces, in the same session, that she intends to abandon the Fiery Shoals for good to fight closer to the Pit, asking Scorio to help arrange a meeting with the rebellion's leaders before she rules on Bastion's future.

Chapters 21–30: The Queen's Accord

Nova returns from her private meeting with the Iron Tyrant and, before the full Fiery Shoals court, confirms in plain language what Bastion has spent years half-denying: the Empyreal Prophecy failed for good, three Imperators were lost, and the Academy's Archspire has perhaps a decade left before it stops reincarnating Great Souls at all. She announces she is leaving the Fiery Shoals permanently for Deep Hell, and portals the assembled court — Scorio's fellowship included — directly to Bastion's Council Citadel, where she lays out a restructured Celestial Consortium to govern in her stead: new Gold-mana distribution quotas favoring the Houses and Bastion itself, a rotating ninety-day Consortium leadership, and binding Heart Oaths for all four Autocrators. At Nova's request, Scorio personally brings the terms to Bastion's rebel leadership — Dola, Abentha, and the others — who negotiate for, and win, a fixed share of Gold mana earmarked for the city itself.

The chapters that follow cost Scorio as much as they earn him. A public argument with Jova over his shifting priorities turns genuinely cruel on both sides; a House Kraken honeytrap involving Ravenna Accardi nearly compromises him before she breaks character and confesses the whole scheme; and Manticore's Dread Blaze Dameon finally appears on the page in person, diagnosing a serious, previously unaddressed flaw in Scorio's Heart: months of training on nothing but Coal mana have left his body untempered beneath his rank, a debt Dameon says will cost him "double" whatever the rest of the fellowship has to put in. The Act closes with the public signing of the Queen's Accord in Imperator Square, all four Autocrators swearing their Heart Oaths before a crowd of thousands, and the fellowship departing Bastion aboard Manticore's whale ship, The Sloop, bound for the Chasm.

Chapters 31–40: The Chasm

Manticore relocates the fellowship to the Chasm, a mile-wide sinkhole riddled with centuries of collapsed Great Soul colonies, where Dameon — drawing on memories from a past life as an Imperator — is hunting a specific lost artifact, the Font, capable of drawing Gold mana from the ground on demand. Diagnosed with the same Coal-tempering problem as Scorio, Naomi joins him in an unsparing, five-month regimen of manual labor meant to physically purge the bad mana from both their bodies, while the rest of the fellowship trains properly alongside Manticore's Dread Blazes. The cost is real: by the end of it, Scorio and Naomi are both changed, leaner and harder, and largely cut off from the rest of the group's day-to-day camaraderie. Jova reaches Flame Vault during this stretch — the first member of the fellowship to do so — and her friendship with Scorio, already strained, ends in a blunt, one-sided conversation in which she tells him plainly she no longer considers him her equal.

With Scorio and Naomi's purge finally deemed close enough, Manticore calls in House Kraken's Pyre Lady Druanna to help force open the collapsed vault holding the Font, in exchange for exclusive rights to sell it once recovered. The raid, expected to take about two hours, stretches to nearly three full day-night cycles. Book 2, Chapter 40 ends on an unresolved cliffhanger: the recovery cage returns to the surface with Dameon carrying Druanna's body, and Dread Blazes Evelyn, Simeon, Mavel, and Tsing Ma all missing. Their fates are not yet known.

Open threads heading further into Book 2

What happened to Druanna, Evelyn, Simeon, Mavel, and Tsing Ma in the Gold level of the Chasm (Chapter 40's cliffhanger, unresolved); the anonymous House Kraken message Scorio never received, refused on his behalf by Ydrielle (Chapter 37); whether the fellowship's provisional place in Manticore ever becomes full membership, and on what terms, now that Dameon has appeared on the page and set explicit conditions for Scorio; the "sense of being watched" reported by multiple Manticore members in the Chasm's depths; Octavia's suspicion that Charnel Duke Plassus is being manipulated by someone or something (relayed secondhand by Ravenna Accardi, Chapter 28); and whatever Praximar and Pyre Lady Moira were maneuvering toward with House Basilisk, last mentioned before Nova's departure and not revisited on the page since.
