The Four Houses

Bastion's governing factions, mostly run by Great Souls who never left

Bastion is governed, loosely and competitively, by four great Houses, each headquartered in the city and each running enterprises that stretch out into hell itself. Most House leadership consists of Great Souls who chose administration over pushing further into the Pit — a choice regarded, by at least some Great Souls, as a kind of quiet surrender.

House Hydra

The most politically prestigious House; source of most of the ruling council. Chancellor Pyre Lord Praximar is its Autocrator, and is the only one seen unsurprised by the White Queen's Book 2 announcement of her departure — strongly implying advance warning via Pyre Lady Moira's power.

House Kraken

The wealthiest House, with "endless vaults of treasure." Naomi's birth family serves House Kraken. Its Autocrator, named on the page for the first time in Book 2, Chapter 21, is Octavia — a striking, self-possessed figure in gold ceremonial robes who separately orchestrates a honeytrap against Scorio via Ravenna Accardi (Book 2, Chapter 27–28).

House Chimera

The youngest House, known for deep-hell acquisitions. Operates the skycrane yard Scorio and Dola's crew infiltrate for a shipment of Sapphire mana. Sponsored Scorio in Book 1, Chapter 50. Its Autocrator, named in Book 2, Chapter 21, is Raugr — bald, square-jawed, and severe, described as visibly angered by the White Queen's departure announcement.

House Basilisk

The eldest and most nefarious House, willing to steal from rivals and hire deniable operatives regardless of background. Dola works for Basilisk out of the Narrows. Its Autocrator, named in Book 2, Chapter 21, is Eira — a reclusive sylph with a boyish crop and a manner that verges on manic; also Nissa's ultimate patron.

The Queen's Accord (Book 2)

In Book 2, Chapter 21, Nova, the White Queen, announces she is permanently withdrawing from the Fiery Shoals and restructures how the Houses share power over Bastion and the Rascor Plains in her absence, via a body called the Celestial Consortium. All four Autocrators publicly swear binding Heart Oaths to the ratified charter, called the Queen's Accord, before a crowd of thousands in Imperator Square (Book 2, Chapter 30). Key terms, as laid out on the page:

  • Gold mana distribution: the bulk still ships to the Iron Weald via the Celestial Coffer, but each House now also receives 5% of each shipment to distribute among its own members, with the House currently holding Consortium leadership receiving an additional 5%. A final 5% is invested directly in Bastion's own upkeep, overseen by Bastion's Council — a provision Scorio's relayed negotiations with Bastion's rebel leadership helped secure.
  • Consortium leadership rotates through the four Houses every ninety Bastion days (Hydra held the first rotation; Chimera is next, per Book 2, Chapter 37).
  • Member Houses swear Heart Oaths against bribery, extortion, and manipulation of the distribution process, with regular audits and graduated penalties for violations.
  • All four Autocrators swear off armed conflict with each other, direct or by proxy, and commit resources instead to defending the Farmlands and the Rascor Plains and staffing a quarter of the security detail for the Crucible and its Siphon.
  • The Iron Tyrant acts as a neutral third-party adjudicator for disputes, to be invoked only as a last resort.

Bastion also seats its first civilian Council representative under the new Accord (Book 2, Chapter 37). See Book 2 and The Fiery Shoals for the surrounding events.

The Academy

The Academy, where Great Souls are reborn and trained, is closely tied to the Houses but formally its own institution; the Houses compete to recruit graduating Great Souls with promises of wealth and power, and most Academy leadership positions are themselves held by Great Souls.

Sponsorship & recruitment

Each House maintains a recruitment office within the Academy and sends representatives to scout the ongoing class tournament. House Chimera's recruitment interview with Scorio in Book 1, Chapter 50 — conducted by Principal Recruiter Selena, alongside Recruiters Wrack and Gelegos — illustrates the general process: an extended, probing interview into the candidate's background and motives, a Heart evaluation using specialized measuring treasures, and a package of healing pills, elixirs, and mandatory meditation sessions calibrated to the candidate's specific weaknesses. Selena is explicit that the House invests for the long term ("we want you to reach Imperator one day, not Dread Blaze tomorrow"), a philosophy shaped by past centuries in which overfed young Great Souls burned out, went mad, or had their Hearts shatter entirely.

Unrest

The Houses' authority is currently under real strain: the failure of the Empyreal Prophecy a few years ago, ongoing riots, and a rising political movement called the Deniers — who argue Bastion's entire founding narrative is propaganda used to justify privileging Great Souls over common-born citizens — have left the ruling order visibly shaken.
