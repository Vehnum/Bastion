The Gauntlet

Every Great Soul dies here first — on purpose

The Gauntlet is an ancient trial every newly-reincarnated Great Soul must run, stripped of all memory and all past-life power, immediately upon awakening. Entrants (called Chars for the duration) are grouped in fours and sent through a sequence of lethal chambers; death within the Gauntlet is real, but not permanent — it simply ends that entrant's run and deposits them, alive and memoryless, in the Academy's basilica for the true reincarnation ceremony.

Structure & scoring

Each chamber passed is worth points, escalating the deeper an entrant gets (1 point each for chambers 1–5, 5 points each for chambers 6–10, 10 for the 11th, and so on), with an estimated maximum possible score of 1,440. Both individual and team (four-person group average) scores are recorded. The further a Great Soul progresses, the greater their share of harvested past-life power at rebirth.

Known chambers (this cycle)

  1. The Tomb Field — a vast copper-floored chamber of numbered burial-like openings, where entrants first awaken; ends at a wall-spanning beam of amber light.
  2. The Iron Hallway — a crossbow-bolt trap triggers on entry. 175 of 412 entrants died here this cycle.
  3. The Dark Room — small clawed "imp" creatures, one per entrant, ambush in near-total darkness. 148 more died here.
  4. The Hall of Blades — a narrow, trapped corridor of retracting blades triggered by hidden patterns in the walls. 63 more died here; only 26 of the original 412 continued on.
  5. The Chamber of Balance — a hallway that steepens into a nearly vertical incline as entrants crawl its length, eventually pitching most survivors back down to their death. Historically the point at which every entrant fails outright. This cycle, 26 entrants made it this far; 23 died within the chamber itself (including Scorio, ranked 18th among them), while three — the first in 27 years to do so — fully crossed it into an unnamed further chamber, where all three died almost immediately after entering.

The Trials

Distinct from the Gauntlet itself: after a Great Soul ignites their Igneous Heart and becomes a Cinder, they advance through a further sequence of Trials, the first of which grants Emberling rank. To trigger it, a Great Soul must first deepen their Heart's mana capacity as far as they're able or willing to risk, then ignite it with focused, deliberate purpose — at which point they are automatically drawn into a personal Trial Room, the same way entrants are pulled into the Gauntlet. Its contents are unique to each individual and closely guarded; Naomi refuses to describe her own, on the grounds that foreknowledge would distort Scorio's experience of his. It's confirmed that whatever power a Great Soul manifests on passing their First Trial reflects something essential about who they are (Naomi's monstrous Nightmare form; Jova Spike's wound-scaling black aura). Academy students are expected to pass their First Trial and reach Emberling within their first year, in time for a mandatory, much harder second Gauntlet run at year's end.

Notably, Lianshi's personal journals show a recurring pattern, across dozens of past lives, of tearing out and burning any pages describing her own First Trial before each new incarnation — an unexplained mystery.

See The Igneous Heart & Mana for how passing successive Trials ties into recovering fragments of a Great Soul's suppressed past-life memory, and why most Great Souls permanently plateau at Dread Blaze without ever managing it.

The old Gauntlet

The Old Academy, Bastion's abandoned founding-era campus, holds a second, much older version of the Gauntlet, still accessible via a handful of surviving, mana-active reincarnation biers near its ruined Archspire. Because the old Academy is unmonitored, entries here go completely unrecorded — giving Scorio, Leonis, and Lianshi effectively unlimited access to death-based training once they discover it in Book 1, Chapter 30, bypassing the normal two-runs-per-year limit entirely.

This version is harder and more decayed than the current Gauntlet, with reconfigured obstacles (a triple-blade ambush in place of the crossbow-bolt trap; a room of animated combat statues, three of which activate at a time in randomized weapon loadouts). Deaths here are agonizingly painful — full sensory pain that lingers uncomfortably after revival — despite leaving no physical injury on the real body. Crucially, the Gauntlet's difficulty appears to scale to the rank of whoever enters: rooms that challenge a group of Cinders become dramatically harder, with new hazards and stronger enemies, when run by an Emberling like Naomi. The first five rooms are understood to constitute a "Char/Cinder tier"; the sixth room onward scales to Emberling difficulty, and so on.

Reviving in the old basilica leaves a person briefly incapacitated and vulnerable to real danger — on one occasion a live fiend wanders in and nearly kills Scorio and Naomi moments after they revive from a Gauntlet death.

This cycle's results

412 Great Souls entered. Only three fully crossed the Chamber of Balance into the unnamed chamber beyond: Jova Spike (fastest), Ravenna Accardi (seven seconds behind), and Massamach. Scorio reached and died within the Chamber of Balance itself, ranked 18th among that group; his fellow group members Leonis and Lianshi died in the Dark Room and the Hall of Blades respectively, while a fourth group member, Asha, died in the Iron Hallway.

The mandatory end-of-year run

As foreshadowed, the cohort's mandatory second official Gauntlet run takes place at the close of the first year (Book 1, Chapters 61–80), scaled to reward Emberling- and Tomb Spark-level performance and worth far greater rewards: House sponsorship upgrades, healing treasures, and for whoever progresses the farthest, the Academy's full backing and effective choice of any House. Scorio, Leonis, Lianshi, and Naomi enter as a team of four Emberlings; Naomi, Lianshi, and Leonis are eliminated one by one across the first twelve chambers, including a hall of animated golden statuette assassins, a web-and-funnel-trap fiend chamber, and a horde of small fiends that overwhelms Leonis even as he buys Scorio the time to press on alone.

Alone from the thirteenth chamber onward, Scorio's own Second Trial triggers spontaneously mid-run, elevating him to Tomb Spark and healing him completely; he goes on to solo a rock fiend, an acid-filled spherical chamber, and an invisible-fiend gauntlet before reaching the eighteenth chamber — a ring of five rotating platforms guarded by masked executioners — where the Gauntlet's shared final-vision effect shows him how far Jova Spike made it before being stopped on the third platform. Scorio pushes himself one platform further, besting her by a single chamber and finishing first overall; Jova places second, and Chen She places third.
