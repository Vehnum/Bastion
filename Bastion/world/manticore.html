Manticore

An independent outfit that recruits for character, not raw talent

Manticore is a small, independent Deep Hell organization that Scorio's fellowship is provisionally recruited into during Book 2. It first appears as a name in Book 1's closing chapters — a contact who could smuggle the group onto the Rascor Plains — and becomes a major presence once the fellowship actually leaves Bastion (Book 2, Chapters 8–40).

Philosophy & structure

Manticore operates entirely outside the four Houses' authority, and prides itself on transparency among its own members: no hoarded treasures, no withheld secrets, no dangling incentives to keep lower ranks doing drudge work. Its stated guiding principle is to do "whatever it takes" to reach the Pit, together, rather than individually. As of Book 2 it has around thirty-nine to "over forty" members (the figure is given both ways on the page): five Dread Blazes (its founding leadership, "Class of 867" or thereabouts, reborn only around six years ago), twenty-six Flame Vaults, five Tomb Sparks, and three Emberlings — tiny compared to a House like Hydra, estimated at roughly a thousand Great Souls. Manticore recruits deliberately: Dread Blaze Evelyn states outright that raw talent and Gauntlet performance matter less to them than character and shared conviction.

Manticore previously counted an Imperator among its founders, named on the page for the first time in Book 2, Chapter 32 as Jarisca; her current status (the text does not confirm whether she died or simply left) nearly caused the group to disband and fold into one of the Houses. Its remaining Dread Blaze leadership chose instead to stay independent, and now screens new recruits accordingly.

Leadership

Five Dread Blazes lead Manticore, all now confirmed on the page:

  • Evelyn — recruits and escorts the fellowship out of Bastion; her manifested power lets her hair extend into an enormous entangling web and "travel" instantly along her own strands, effectively an unwilling-to-be-struck compulsion rather than true teleportation. Missing, along with three other Dread Blazes, at the end of Book 2, Chapter 40 (see below).
  • Davelos — meets the group in the Farmlands and tests them again in sparring; can turn his body to a Copper-and-Coal metallic mist and reform at will, and is noticeably more standoffish about recruiting outsiders than Evelyn.
  • Simeon — met at the Fiery Shoals; poetic and reserved, with a defensive power that lets him assume an opponent's appearance and neutralize them peacefully, a holdover ability from an era when Great Souls fought each other more often. In a romantic relationship with the Sloop's pilot, Ydrielle. Also missing as of Chapter 40.
  • Dameon — Manticore's de facto leader, now appeared on the page in full (Book 2, Chapter 24 onward). Uniquely among the five, he carries memories and learned skills from a past life as an Imperator, which he uses to guide the group toward a specific lost artifact at the Chasm. See his own article for detail. His prior "touch" from Pyre Lady Moira of House Hydra, previously treated as an ongoing security concern, has not been mentioned again on the page since.
  • A fifth Dread Blaze remains unnamed.

Manticore's whale ship, The Sloop, is piloted by Ydrielle, a non-Dread-Blaze mana-manipulation specialist confirmed in Book 2, Chapter 31 — correcting this page's earlier, more speculative note about a crew member named "Eldrin"; that name does not recur on the page and appears to have been an early misreading.

Why the fellowship was recruited

Evelyn tests all seven members of Scorio's fellowship in unarmed combat at once and defeats them without real difficulty, but is impressed enough by the attempt — and by the fact that Scorio and Jova Spike are the top two graduates of their Academy class — to escort them to the Fiery Shoals as probationary recruits. By the time they arrive, Manticore's leadership has started describing the group's string of improbable successes as being "Whispered" — a folk phenomenon, said to originate from the Vale of Regrets, in which a wind singles out a Great Soul for a run of unlikely good fortune. Dameon later admits candidly that recruiting Scorio, Naomi, Leonis, and Lianshi at all was mostly a "package deal" to secure Jova, whose stone-mastery power he considers formidable enough to change what Manticore can accomplish (Book 2, Chapter 38).

Camp Manticore & the hunt for the Font

Manticore's base of operations is the Chasm, a mile-wide sinkhole whose depths have swallowed multiple prior Great Soul colonies without explanation over the centuries. Camp Manticore, at the Chasm's rim, has stood roughly three years, built over the ruins of older, abandoned camps at the same site. Dameon, drawing on memories from a past life spent in a colony on the Chasm's fifth level, has spent well over half a year directing the search for a specific lost artifact: the Font, said to duplicate, on a personal scale, the process by which Bastion's floating Gold islands draw mana up from the depths. Manticore intends to sell it exclusively to House Kraken in exchange for enough wealth and treasure to push much of its membership up a full rank (Book 2, Chapters 33, 38, 40).

The final push to recover it — using House Kraken Pyre Lady Druanna's golem to force open a collapsed vault — is the subject of Book 2, Chapter 40's unresolved cliffhanger: the recovery cage returns to the surface with Dameon carrying Druanna's body, and Dread Blazes Evelyn, Simeon, and Druanna's own escorts Mavel and Tsing Ma all missing. Their fates, and whether the Font was recovered at all, are not yet known.
