Manticore

An independent outfit that recruits for character, not raw talent

Manticore is a small, independent Deep Hell organization that Scorio's fellowship is provisionally recruited into during Book 2. It first appears as a name in Book 1's closing chapters — a contact who could smuggle the group onto the Rascor Plains — and becomes a major presence once the fellowship actually leaves Bastion (Book 2, Chapters 8–20).

Philosophy & structure

Manticore operates entirely outside the four Houses' authority, and prides itself on transparency among its own members: no hoarded treasures, no withheld secrets, no dangling incentives to keep lower ranks doing drudge work. Its stated guiding principle is to do "whatever it takes" to reach the Pit, together, rather than individually. As of Book 2 it has thirty-nine members: five Dread Blazes (its founding leadership, "Class of 867" or thereabouts, reborn only around six years ago), twenty-six Flame Vaults, five Tomb Sparks, and three Emberlings — tiny compared to a House like Hydra, estimated at roughly a thousand Great Souls. Manticore recruits deliberately: Dread Blaze Evelyn states outright that raw talent and Gauntlet performance matter less to them than character and shared conviction. Its current base of operations is the Chasm, a ravine near Bastion larger than the city itself, whose depths nobody has ever fully explored; Manticore has been investigating unusual mana fluctuations there since Imogen's Book 1 assault on Bastion.

Manticore previously counted an Imperator among its founders; her death (unnamed, undetailed on the page) nearly caused the group to disband and fold into one of the Houses. Its remaining Dread Blaze leadership chose instead to stay independent, and now screens new recruits accordingly.

Leadership

Five Dread Blazes lead Manticore. Three have appeared directly on the page:

  • Evelyn — recruits and escorts the fellowship out of Bastion; her manifested power lets her hair extend into an enormous entangling web and "travel" instantly along her own strands, effectively an unwilling-to-be-struck compulsion rather than true teleportation.
  • Davelos — meets the group in the Farmlands and tests them again in sparring; can turn his body to a Copper-and-Coal metallic mist and reform at will, and is noticeably more standoffish about recruiting outsiders than Evelyn.
  • Simeon — met at the Fiery Shoals; poetic and reserved, with a defensive power that lets him assume an opponent's appearance and neutralize them peacefully, a holdover ability from an era when Great Souls fought each other more often.

A fourth, Dameon, has final say on whether the fellowship's provisional membership becomes permanent, but has not yet appeared on the page as of Book 2, Chapter 20; he has been "touched" by Pyre Lady Moira of House Hydra, which the other Dread Blazes treat as an ongoing security concern. A fifth Dread Blaze remains unnamed. Manticore also owns a single whale ship, The Sloop, generally crewed or captained by members named Simeon mentions in passing — Eldrin and, separately, a member named Dameon possibly aboard it at times.

Why the fellowship was recruited

Evelyn tests all seven members of Scorio's fellowship in unarmed combat at once and defeats them without real difficulty, but is impressed enough by the attempt — and by the fact that Scorio and Jova Spike are the top two graduates of their Academy class — to escort them to the Fiery Shoals as probationary recruits. By the time they arrive, Manticore's leadership has started describing the group's string of improbable successes as being "Whispered" — a folk phenomenon, said to originate from the Vale of Regrets, in which a wind singles out a Great Soul for a run of unlikely good fortune.
