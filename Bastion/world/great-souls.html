Great Souls & Rank

A thousand years of dying for the same city

Great Souls are Bastion's reincarnating heroes: warriors, rulers, and legends of past lives who, upon death, are gathered by the Archspire and reborn roughly a year later to fight again. Every citizen of Bastion is born with an Igneous Heart, but only Great Souls arrive already primed by centuries of accumulated growth — and even then, only a fraction ever rise far.

The reincarnation cycle

New Great Souls wake with no memory beyond their name and a fragmentary dream of dying, first inside the Gauntlet, then for real in the Academy's basilica, on a jeweled bier arranged in circles around the Archspire. Touching a golden sigil on the Archspire causes it to speak aloud each soul's past titles, their highest rank ever achieved, how long it has been since their last death, and their total number of rebirths since Bastion's founding — the closest thing most Great Souls get to knowing their own history.

A soul's power is not evenly distributed on rebirth: Gauntlet performance determines how much of a soul's accumulated past-life power the Archspire grants them at the start of the new life. It's also said that unusually powerful souls can resist or delay the Archspire's summons if they choose to — an explanation offered for why most Great Souls are reborn within five or six years of death, while a rare few, like Scorio, take far longer.

The Red List

A small number of returning souls are found, via the Archspire's revelation, to have committed "heinous actions against Bastion" in a past life. These are declared Proscribed and placed on the Red List, stripped of power, and cast through the Final Door into the Warrens to die — a fate that has befallen Scorio despite his own total lack of memory of any wrongdoing.

The Path of Ascension

Ranks are earned by igniting one's Igneous Heart and then progressively passing a series of Trials. Both the current and old Academies depict the full rank ladder as a mosaic tree, the Path of Ascension, each rank tied to the grade of mana it burns:

  1. Char — Heart unignited. Everyone starts here.
  2. Cinder — burns Copper. (Scorio's and Lianshi's current rank.)
  3. Emberling — burns Iron. (Naomi's, Leonis's, and Lianshi's current rank.)
  4. Tomb Spark — burns Bronze. (Scorio's and Jova Spike's current rank, both reached during the Book 1 Gauntlet finale.)
  5. Flame Vault — burns Silver.
  6. Dread Blaze — burns Gold. The top of the mosaic's "roots" — everything through here is considered mere preparation, and it's the rank most Great Souls plateau at permanently.
  7. Pyre Lord — burns Sapphire. The trunk begins here; true power is said to start at this rank. Highest attainable while remaining in Bastion. Praximar's current rank; Lianshi's past-life rank.
  8. Blood Baron — Scorio's past-life rank.
  9. Charnel Duke — burns Ruby. Ravenna's and Lianshi's past-life rank; Nova "the White Queen" is a Charnel Duchess.
  10. Crimson Earl — burns Diamond. Massamach's past-life rank.
  11. Imperator — burns Noumenon, the rarest mana grade, found only near the Pit itself; too precious even to depict on the mosaic. Only 76 Imperators in Bastion's entire history. Jova Spike's past-life rank; Imogen's current rank.

From Pyre Lord upward, Great Souls develop qualitatively different, formally named powers not available to lower ranks: a personal defensive barrier (Shroud), a signature manifested weapon (Ferula), an Aura, and (at least for Charnel Duchess Nova) a large-scale restorative/protective Halo.

According to Naomi, the underlying driver of advancement at every stage is the same: ambition fueled by unrelenting frustration. Great Souls who become content with the power, comfort, or authority they've already gained simply stop growing — which is why so many never pass Dread Blaze.

The theoretical endpoint of the entire system is the Infernarch: the Great Soul who finally destroys the Pit, hell's source of evil, and reopens the sealed way home to Ettera. In nearly a thousand years, no one has managed it — a failure most recently and publicly underscored by the collapse of the Empyreal Prophecy a few years before the story begins.

The warning & "the Herdsmen"

As of Book 1's end, the Infernarch has taken on new, more urgent significance. A message left over two centuries ago by Scorio's own past self, preserved (and eventually rediscovered) in one of Jova Spike's journals, warns his future incarnation not to trust the Houses, the Imperators, or a group referred to only as "the Herdsmen" — and claims that the only way to break Bastion's reincarnation cycle for good is to become the Infernarch himself. The message is deliberately vague, its author explaining that committing more detail to paper had already drawn dangerous attention in that past life. The identity and nature of "the Herdsmen" is completely unexplained as of Chapter 80. Scorio, Jova, and the rest of their new fellowship intend to investigate the warning after leaving Bastion at Book 1's close.

House sponsorship

The roughly 80 Great Souls who scored best in a given year's first Gauntlet run are offered formal sponsorship by one of the Four Houses shortly after the reincarnation ceremony — access to specialty trainers, better elixirs and pills, and inside knowledge (such as what to expect in the First Trial) not available otherwise. Most accept; notably, Lianshi turned down a House Kraken offer out of anger at how Scorio was treated, and Jova Spike refused House Hydra's offer for unexplained reasons of her own.

Notable Great Souls introduced so far

  • Jova Spike — fastest of the three Great Souls to fully cross the Chamber of Balance this cycle, and first of her cohort to reach Emberling. Past rank: Imperator. See full article.
  • Ravenna Accardi — second of the three, losing to Jova by seven seconds. Past rank: Charnel Duke.
  • Massamach — third of the three; seen visibly searching the crowd for someone during the ceremony. Past rank: Crimson Earl.
  • Scorio — reached and died within the Chamber of Balance, ranked 18th among that group; revealed as Proscribed. Past rank: Blood Baron. See full article.
  • Imogen — a currently active Imperator whose assault on Bastion is the climax of Chapters 38–40. Now understood to be one of the Fallen (see The Igneous Heart & Mana), having burned away her own Heart eight years ago. See full article.
  • Sol, "Sol the Just" — Imogen's estranged husband and an Imperator in his own right; ends her assault on Bastion in Chapter 42. See full article.
  • Nova, "the White Queen" — a legendary Charnel Duchess who leads Bastion's defense against Imogen. See full article.
