Cosmology · Geography Overview

Hell

Bastion is a hole in the ground. This is the rest of the hole.

Hell is the world Bastion is built into — not a metaphor, a place name, used matter-of-factly by everyone born there. Book 1 spends its entire runtime inside Bastion's walls, and the city's own residents talk as though it's the whole of the world worth knowing. Book 2 starts to correct that: the fellowship leaves Bastion behind in its opening chapters and finds a much larger, mostly unmapped territory on the other side of the gates.

The one organizing rule everyone in-story agrees on: the deeper into Hell you go, the worse it gets. Mana grades run richer, fiends run larger and stranger, and the Curse bites harder, the closer a place sits to the Pit — the source, at the bottom of everything, of every drop of mana and every fiend in existence. This article is a rough survey of what's been named so far, roughly in order of distance from it. It will grow as the fellowship travels farther.

Bastion

The city itself sits at what its own inhabitants call "the top of Hell" — the shallowest, safest point anyone lives in numbers. All four of its gates lead out in a direction its own geography calls "south," toward progressively rougher territory. See Bastion for the city itself.

The Rascor Plains

The region immediately beyond Bastion's walls, and the only stretch of Hell mapped in any real detail so far: the Ash Belt, the Rain Wall, the Farmlands, the floating Gold islands, and the crashed island now known as the Fiery Shoals. See The Rascor Plains for the full breakdown.

The Iron Weald

Referenced so far only as the destination of the Fiery Shoals' harvested Gold mana, shipped out aboard the Celestial Coffer. Home to the Fury Spires, ruled by a figure known as the Iron Tyrant, who owns the ship outright even though it's operated day-to-day by the Celestial Consortium. Nothing else about the Weald or its politics has been shown on the page yet; treat this as a placeholder pending further chapters.

The Vale of Regrets

Named only in passing, as the reputed origin of Fate's Whisper, a folk phenomenon in which a wind is said to single out one person in a crowd — the kind of story Manticore's leadership invoke half-joking about the fellowship's own run of luck. Nothing about the Vale itself has been described directly yet.

LastRock

A domain deeper into Hell than anything else named so far, ruled in a past life by Jova Spike as its Charnel Duchess — the text doesn't say for how long, only that she died there less than a year before her current rebirth. In her absence, a rival power called the Blood Ox conquered LastRock outright. Manticore's Dread Blaze Evelyn recognized Jova on sight by this history alone, which gives some sense of how far LastRock's reputation still carries.

The Pit

The deepest point there is, and the one every other distance in this article is measured against: hell's own source, the origin of all mana and all fiends, and the thing the long-dead Empyreal Prophecy failed catastrophically to destroy. See The Pit & Ettera for the full cosmology, including why destroying it is tied to ever reopening the way home.

A note on scope

This page exists to keep the shape of Hell straight as the series introduces more of it, not to pretend the map is complete. Bastion and the Rascor Plains are documented in real detail because the story has actually spent time there; the Iron Weald, the Vale of Regrets, and LastRock are single mentions awaiting real description; and everything below LastRock, down to the Pit itself, is presently unnamed. Expect this list to get longer, and the placeholders to get shorter, one book at a time.
